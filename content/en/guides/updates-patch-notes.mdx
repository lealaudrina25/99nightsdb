99 Nights in the Forest is patched on a two-week rhythm, which makes it genuinely easy to fall behind. This page is the running log: newest update first, oldest at the bottom, with the parts that actually change how you play — new classes, new items, new codes — pulled out of each patch.

Last verified: **2026-10-09**. Grandma's Favourite Games ships headline updates on Saturdays and does not always publish full written patch notes, so anything not confirmed by at least two sources is marked as unconfirmed below rather than guessed at.

## How the Update Schedule Works

Two patterns are worth knowing before you read the list:

- **Updates land on Saturdays.** Since June 2026 they have arrived roughly every fourteen days.
- **Most content updates run as a Saturday-to-Saturday event window**, usually seven days. Limited-time badges, shop rotations and event areas expire when the window closes.

Two practical consequences: check the game on Saturday morning, and never leave a limited badge to the final evening.

The fishing update that introduced that content added two classes, and the cheaper one is still the standard answer to early food pressure. See [**Fisherman Class**](/classes/fisherman-class).

The January Fairy update added the biome, and our [Fairy](/entities/the-fairy) page keeps that separate from the NPC.

## Helping Happy (October 3 – 10, 2026)

The current update, and the one that has changed the most things at once in months. Officially titled **Helping Happy**, it opened on Saturday **3 October 2026** as the run-up to Halloween and runs to **Saturday 10 October**.

The premise is simple: **Happy**, the pumpkin-headed Halloween visitor, arrives at camp and is immediately torn apart by the Ram. You spend the event finding his six body parts and putting him back together. There is **no class, level or quest requirement** to start — Happy appears when a run loads.

### Step 1: Pick Up Happy's Scythe

Look beside the wooden stake near the campfire: Happy's carved pumpkin head sits on it, and **Happy's Scythe** lies on the ground next to him. Talk to him first — you get a short scene explaining what happened — then take the Scythe.

The Scythe is the event's tool and weapon at once. It has **three levels**, it turns purple at Level 2 and gains a dark-energy effect at Level 3, and it levels by being used on webs, spider sacks and enemies. It starts out dealing roughly Spear-level damage, so it is not a free upgrade to your main weapon — it is the key to the event.

Clearing webs does more than progress the Scythe. A broken web can drop a random item such as ammo or morsels, **or it can spawn a Cultist**, so do not cut webs at the edge of the map with no health left.

Our [Scythe](/weapons/how-to-get-scythe) page keeps this weapon separate from the two other scythes in the game, which is the mistake most guides make.

### Step 2: Find the Six Body Parts

The parts are **not in fixed places**. The forest regenerates every run, so each piece is tied to a *structure or enemy type* rather than a coordinate. Search by landmark, not by a screenshot.

| Body part | Where to look | What you actually do |
|---|---|---|
| **Right Leg** | Windmill | Search the structure, collect the leg, carry it back |
| **Left Leg** | Barn | Search the barn; the entrance may be webbed |
| **Left Arm** | Stone-brick building | Clear the webs inside, then collect it |
| **Right Arm** | **Alpha Wolf** | Kill the wolf holding it and take the drop |
| **Hat** | **Cultist** | Kill the Cultist wearing it |
| **Torso** | Pumpkin farm cart | Harvest **eight pumpkins** and load them onto the cart |

Two of those are fights rather than searches:

- The **Right Arm's Alpha Wolf** has **125 HP and hits for about 25**. In the Forest biome it only starts appearing once the campfire is **Level 3**; in other biomes it turns up at night. See [Alpha Wolf](/entities/the-alpha-wolf).
- The **Hat's Cultist** sits at roughly **100–150 HP** and already raids your camp at night, so you will often meet the right one while defending. See [The Cultist](/entities/the-cultist).

And one is a farm job: the **Torso** is pinned under a cart at a pumpkin farm, and it only comes free once **eight pumpkins** have been loaded onto it. That is the same crop our [Pumpkin](/items/pumpkin) page covers.

**You cannot store the parts.** Each piece has to be carried back to the stake by hand, one at a time, and it attaches automatically when you arrive. That return trip is why food and wood matter: the event almost never fits into one safe night, so keep the camp supplied while you search.

### Step 3: The Ram Step Everyone Misses

Handing in all six parts does **not** finish the event. Happy gets rebuilt, the Ram immediately knocks his pumpkin head off again, and the quest appears stuck.

The fix is a night step: **wait for the Ram to appear after dark, then lure it into something solid** — a tree, a stone structure, a built wall. The collision frees the head. Carry it back to the stake and the rebuild completes. Our [Ram](/entities/the-ram) page covers how it moves and what actually stops it.

### Badges and the Reward You Choose

Three badges ship with the event:

| Badge | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Reaping** | Take Happy's Scythe to its maximum level |
| **Strawcraft** | Help reassemble Happy |
| **Sweeping** | Destroy **200 webs** with Happy's Scythe |

Earn all three and you unlock **Happy's Cart** furniture. One source tallies the three badges at **21 Diamonds** between them; treat that total as single-source rather than checked.

Finishing also lets you pick **one of three rewards** — you get a choice, not all three:

- **Vampire Cloak**
- **Bouncing Blade**
- **Laser Cannon**

That last one is the same weapon as our [Laser Cannon](/weapons/how-to-get-laser-cannon) page, where the Cyborg route is the permanent way to get it.

### The Rest of the Patch

Beyond the event, the same update shipped a set of quieter changes: navigation improvements, new building and planting options, item fixes and smaller interaction changes. None is a headline on its own, but the planting additions matter if you run a farm — see [Seed Packs](/items/seed-packs).

## New Classes (September 5 – 12, 2026)

The most recent *content* update before Helping Happy. Officially titled **New Classes**, opened as a Roblox event on Saturday, September 5, 2026 and running to September 12. The developer's own description was "new classes, daily quests, fire offerings and more".

**Three new classes: Escape Artist, Elemental and Woodsman.** Their names are confirmed by the official Fandom Update Log entry added on September 5 and by several independent class-by-class videos published the same weekend.

**Daily quests** — recurring objectives that give you a reason to log in every day, drawing on survival tasks, gathering, exploring and combat.

**Fire offerings** — a ritual-style system tied to the campfire and survival progression. Check the campfire area first after any update that mentions it; that is where the new rewards unlock.

**Three quest badges**, one per class: Woodsman's Quest, Elemental's Quest and Escape Artist's Quest. All three appeared the day the update launched.

What is **not** confirmed: the **Diamond prices and per-level perks** of the three classes were not published at launch. No source has documented them, so they are not listed here rather than invented. If you are deciding what to buy, see [Best Class](/classes/best-class), which only covers classes with documented numbers.

## Biome Refresher (August 22 and 29, 2026)

A two-part pass described by the developer as "some love for the biomes".

**Part 1 — Snow Biome (Aug 22).** An Owl's Nest feather-reward loop, a bell that slows freezing, an Ice Temple Boost Pad upgrade and an Ice Bow reward, plus three new badges. Boars gained tusk drops and a craftable defensive helmet recipe — see [The Boar](/entities/the-boar) for what that recipe is and where it is sourced.

**Part 2 — Jungle Biome (Aug 29).** Refreshed terrain and updated exploration routes around the temples, ruins and treehouse areas, along with Jungle Pits and the Cat entity. No new code shipped with this one.

The practical takeaway: if you last explored the Jungle months ago, the routes you memorised have changed. Re-walk it rather than trusting old paths.

Their +1 day-counter multiplier is the other half of how a [speedrun in 99 Nights in the Forest](/guides/speedrun-guide) finishes inside 90 minutes.

The gate above is only half of it. The other half is the win condition itself — all four rescues completed before the run resolves. Both halves, with the campfire levels they depend on, are on [how to win 99 Nights in the Forest](/guides/how-to-win-99-nights).

## Kids Have Jobs (August 8, 2026)

This one quietly changed the economy of a camp. All four rescued kids now take a passive job around camp:

| Kid | Job |
|---|---|
| Dino Kid | Chops wood |
| Kraken Kid | Catches fish |
| Squid Kid | Cooks food |
| Koala Kid | Scavenges Scrap |

Three new **Daycare badges** cover raising Kraken, Squid and Koala Kid to Level 5.

Why it matters more than it sounds: rescued kids stop being pure liability and become a slow background income of wood, food and Scrap. Over a long run that compounds. If you have been skipping rescues because they cost more than they return, that calculation changed in August.

## What Is Live Right Now

The live update is **Helping Happy** (3 – 10 October 2026). It adds the Happy's Scythe quest, six recoverable body parts, a night-time Ram step, and three badges that unlock Happy's Cart. The full walkthrough sits just below the top of this page.

The previous patch, the **2x Diamond Weekend** (19 – 22 September), has ended. It doubled Diamond earnings, added sales on selected classes, and introduced the `awardwinner_yay` reward code.

**One part of it is still disputed.** That update is widely reported as having revamped the Class Shop so that **every class stays available** instead of rotating through a daily stock of about six. Only one wiki reports this, and two other class guides updated in September 2026 still describe the rotating shop — so treat "every class is always available" as **unconfirmed** until you see it in your own lobby. If the shop is still rotating, keep your Diamonds and check daily rather than paying for re-rolls.

**Next expected update: Saturday, 17 October 2026.** That is a projection from the fourteen-day cadence plus one outlet's listing of 18 October, not an announcement — nothing has been published about it yet.

A good illustration of why that matters to players: fishing arrived on 15 August 2025, and the building that handed out rods was replaced outright in February 2026. Published guides still describe the old arrangement. Our [**fishing guide**](/guides/fishing-guide) tracks the change.

## How to Check What Just Changed

Updates ship without a written changelog often enough that this order is worth memorising:

1. **Class Shop** — new classes, and whether anything is on sale.
2. **Campfire** — offering and ritual mechanics live here first.
3. **Quest and badge menus** — new limited badges expire with the event window.
4. **Codes** — most updates drop one. Check [Active Codes](/codes/active-codes) rather than trusting a video thumbnail.
5. **Join a fresh server** if the new content does not appear. Old servers frequently miss the update entirely.

Badge rosters move with the updates, which is why the diamond totals on our [All Badges](/guides/all-badges) page disagree with each other.
