Halloween in 99 Nights in the Forest is not a single drop — it is a **season that runs 18 September to 15 November 2026**. Helping Happy (3–10 October) was the run-up, not the event itself. Once the season is live you play a Trick or Treat loop at night, spend the Candies you collect at the **Pumpkin Reaper's** shop, and unlock a limited-time **Trick or Treater** class. The season ships in three parts, so the shop and rewards keep growing through early November.

Last verified: **2026-10-09**. The developer publishes the season in parts and does not always release a full item list up front, so anything not yet confirmed by an in-game source is marked below rather than guessed at.

## Quick Answer

- **When:** the Halloween season is live **18 September – 15 November 2026**.
- **The loop:** at night, light houses with **Halloween Candles** and walk up to **Trick or Treat** them for **Candies**.
- **The shop:** the **Pumpkin Reaper** NPC runs a Halloween shop that grows to **14 items** across the season.
- **The class:** **Trick or Treater** is a limited-time class you buy with Candies (30 in the 2025 run; see *Where Sources Disagree*).
- **The payoff:** permanent taming skins (Pumpkin Bunny, Skeleton Wolf) and base decor are the items worth saving Candies for.

## How the Trick or Treat Loop Works

Halloween mode adds a nightly routine on top of the normal survival run:

1. **Get Halloween Candles.** These are the "key" you use to light houses. They drop and are found during the season the same way other event items appear — keep a stack before nightfall.
2. **Light houses at night.** Walk up to a house, use a Candle to light it, then interact to **Trick or Treat**. Lit houses pay out **Candies**.
3. **Pumpkins are everywhere.** The field fills with pumpkins during the season, which is also why pumpkin-based recipes and the Pumpkin Reaper fit the theme.
4. **A cauldron appears.** From Part 2 (Potion Making, 25 September) a cauldron sits next to the crafting table; add ingredients to brew **potions** and pull more **Candies**.
5. **Carnival events fire.** Halloween mode reliably triggers carnival events, the same limited mini-events that show up in other seasonal modes.

The loop is low-risk compared to a boss fight: you are farming a currency, not surviving a raid. The only real limit is how many houses you can light before dawn.

## The Pumpkin Reaper's Halloween Shop

The Pumpkin Reaper NPC is the season's shopkeeper. The shop is confirmed to reach **14 items** by the end of the season, added in waves. **Six are confirmed in the current build; eight more roll out through Parts 2 and 3** (the source flags "eight more items slated for addition" around late October).

Here is the confirmed set so far, ranked by what matters to a player — cost in Candies versus whether the unlock is **permanent**:

**Where the value actually is:**

- The three **20-Candy furniture** pieces are the cheapest permanent base decor you can grab — buy them early, they never leave.
- The two **taming skins** are the real end-game prizes. **Pumpkin Bunny (150)** and **Skeleton Wolf (500)** are permanent pet unlocks, so if you want Halloween pets that stay after the season, save your Candies for these instead of spending on the class.
- The **Trick or Treater class** is **limited** — it only exists during the season. It is worth the Candies only if you will actually play through November, because it boosts your candy income (see below) rather than giving a permanent power.

The **Trick or Treater** class is the one item that pays you back: buying it raises your candy take to **+1 per house (4 total)** and improves your odds of getting treats. That makes it a candy-farming multiplier, not just a cosmetic class.

## The Trick or Treater Class

Trick or Treater is a **limited-time class** sold in the Halloween shop for Candies.

- **Effect:** +1 Candy per house (up from the base 3 to 4) and a higher chance of good treats when you Trick or Treat.
- **Cost:** 30 Candies in the 2025 run. The 2026 price has not been separately confirmed by an in-game source, so treat 30 as a projection (see *Where Sources Disagree*).
- **Limited:** it leaves when the season ends on 15 November 2026. There is no confirmed path to keep it afterward.
- **Verdict:** buy it early in the season if you plan to farm Candies for the permanent taming skins — the faster candy income pays for itself.

## How to Farm Candies Fast

Candies are the only currency the Halloween shop takes, so the efficient play is to maximize the nightly loop:

1. **Buy Trick or Treater first** if you can afford it — the +1 per house compounds across every house you light.
2. **Light every house you can reach** before dawn. More lit houses = more Trick or Treat interactions = more Candies.
3. **Use the cauldron (Part 2+).** From 25 September the cauldron next to the crafting table turns ingredients into potions **and** Candies — a second candy source on top of houses.
4. **Stock Halloween Candles during the day** so you are never blocked at night waiting to light a house.
5. **Aim for the permanent skins.** 150 + 500 = 650 Candies for both taming skins; that is the realistic season-long goal, so spend deliberately.

A rough pace: a base run lights ~3 houses (3 Candies), a Trick or Treater run lights ~4 houses (4 Candies) plus cauldron output. Over a multi-week season that is enough to clear both permanent skins without grinding every night.

## What Parts 2 and 3 Add

The season is delivered in three parts, not all at once:

- **Part 1 (live at season open, 18 September):** the Trick or Treat loop, the Pumpkin Reaper shop, and the Trick or Treater class.
- **Part 2 — Potion Making (25 September 2026):** the cauldron unlocks next to the crafting table; adding ingredients brews potions and yields Candies. This is the second candy source and the start of Halloween-themed consumables.
- **Part 3 — Grand Finale (1 November 2026):** the season's closing drop, expected to complete the 14-item shop and add the remaining rewards.

Because the shop grows across the season, **check the Pumpkin Reaper every few days** — items that were "TBA" in the table above fill in through late October and early November. Dates can shift; verify against in-game notices.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Halloween 2026 one update or a season?**
A season. It runs 18 September to 15 November 2026 and ships in three parts, unlike a normal seven-day Saturday drop.

**How do I get Candies?**
Light houses with Halloween Candles at night and Trick or Treat them. From Part 2 (25 September) the cauldron next to the crafting table also yields Candies.

**Is the Trick or Treater class permanent?**
No. It is a limited-time class that leaves when the season ends on 15 November 2026. The two taming skins (Pumpkin Bunny, Skeleton Wolf) are permanent.

**What should I spend Candies on first?**
The three 20-Candy furniture pieces early, then save for the permanent taming skins (150 + 500). Buy the Trick or Treater class only if you will farm through November.

**When does the Halloween shop reach 14 items?**
Across Parts 2 and 3 — the eight remaining items roll out through late October, with the Grand Finale on 1 November 2026.

**Does Helping Happy count as Halloween?**
Yes, as the run-up. Helping Happy (3–10 October) introduced Happy's Scythe and the six-body-part quest; the broader Trick or Treat season is what follows it.
