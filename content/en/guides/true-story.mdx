**Short answer: the premise is inspired by a real event, the monsters are not.** The four missing children you rescue come from a true survival story — four indigenous Colombian children who lived 40 days alone in the Amazon in 2023. The Deer, the cultists and everything supernatural are fiction the developers added for horror.

Last verified: **2026-09-29**.

The game's own night cycle is a supply problem long before it is a story, and our [Ammo and Guns](/guides/ammo-guide) guide treats it as one.


On **1 May 2023**, a Cessna 206 flying over the Colombian Amazon suffered engine failure and crashed into the rainforest in the Caquetá province. Seven people were on board.

Three adults died — the pilot, another passenger, and **Magdalena Mucutuy Valencia**, the mother of the four children. Some accounts record that the mother survived the crash and died four days later; that detail is reported inconsistently, so treat it as unverified.

The four children survived:

| Name | Age |
|---|---|
| Lesly Jacobombaire Mucutuy | 13 |
| Soleiny Jacobombaire Mucutuy | 9 |
| Tien Ranoque Mucutuy | 4 |
| Cristin Ranoque Mucutuy | 11 months |

They belong to the **Huitoto** (Witoto) indigenous people, whose territory spans the Colombia–Peru border of the Amazon.

Each child in this story was behind a padlock, and our [Keys and Locked Doors](/guides/keys-and-locked-doors) guide is the only page that lists every key the game gives you.

Eight diamonds and a long grind are worth it only if you get to the end of the run. What 'the end of the run' actually requires, and the badges that cannot coexist with it, are on [how to win 99 Nights in the Forest](/guides/how-to-win-99-nights).

From here the material the tier-5 bench asks for — the **Gem of the Forest** — runs on its own route, with its own fragment sources. See the [Gem of the Forest](/items/gem-of-the-forest) page for all of them on one table.

## How Four Children Survived 40 Days

This part is the reason the story became world news, and it is the part the game does *not* simulate.

**Indigenous knowledge.** Lesly, the eldest, drew on Huitoto knowledge passed down through generations: which seeds, roots and fruits were edible, which plants were poisonous, and how to move through dense rainforest without becoming lost.

**Salvage.** They recovered cassava flour (fariña) from the wreckage, which carried them through the first stretch.

**Foraging and shelter.** When the flour ran out they ate wild fruits and seeds, and built makeshift shelters from branches against the rain.

**Threats were real but ordinary.** Jaguars, venomous snakes and mosquitoes — not monsters. Lesly carried the eleven-month-old through the jungle while caring for the other two.

Four children behind four padlocks, and four fights afterwards that you have to survive on whatever health is left; our [Healing, Bandages and Medkits](/guides/healing-guide) guide covers the health side of a rescue.

## The Rescue: Operation Hope

Colombia's military and indigenous volunteer search teams ran a joint operation named **Operación Esperanza** ("Operation Hope"). For weeks they found only traces: a baby's bottle, a pair of scissors, small footprints, an abandoned shelter.

On **9 June 2023** — 40 days after the crash — searchers heard the baby crying and found all four children alive, severely malnourished and dehydrated. They were airlifted to a military hospital in Bogotá and discharged on **14 July 2023**.

A Netflix documentary, *The Lost Children*, covers the event.

Astronomy and the night sky are the game's one direct borrow from the real story, and our [Weather and Night Events](/guides/weather-and-night-events) guide continues that thread.


This is the split most players actually want.

| Real | Invented |
|---|---|
| Four children lost in a hostile forest | The Deer |
| A plane crash as the inciting incident | The cultists who worship it |
| Rescue as the objective | The Owl and other supernatural entities |
| Starvation, exposure, predators | Crafting, classes and combat systems |
| Forest knowledge as the difference between life and death | 99 nights instead of 40 days |
| A large-scale search effort | Wendigo folklore and ritual lore |

The number is the clearest change: **40 days became 99 nights.** The four children became Dino Kid, Kraken Kid, Squid Kid and Koala Kid — renamed, given animal-themed identities, and placed in an entirely fictional forest.

## Where The Deer Actually Comes From

The Deer has no connection to the Amazon story. Three separate threads feed it:

**Wendigo folklore.** The Wendigo is an Algonquian figure tied to hunger, greed and the winter wilderness. The Deer matches its profile closely: nocturnal, gaunt, endlessly hungry, and driven off by light. See [The Deer](/entities/the-deer) for how that translates into behaviour you have to survive.

**In-game lore.** The game establishes its own origin: the forest was once a research facility running animal experiments. A deer escaped, caused mass casualties, and the survivors formed a cult that worships it as something divine.

**The 2018 game The Forest.** The survival-horror structure — build a camp, hold it through the night, face cultists — follows that game's template.

One popular fan theory connects The Deer to the children's mother, pointing at a syringe found near a bear cage and the plane wreckage in the map. **The game does not confirm this**, and no source we checked does either. Treat it as a theory, not lore.

## Is the Link Officially Confirmed?

Here is the honest position, because most sites skip it.

Every major wiki and guide covering the game names the 2023 Amazon survival as its inspiration, and the parallels are specific enough to be persuasive: four children, a plane crash, a jungle, a large rescue.

What we could **not** find is a published statement from Grandma's Favourite Games explicitly naming the event. So the accurate phrasing is: **strongly supported editorial and community consensus, not an official announcement.** If the developer ever says it plainly, this page will say so instead.

## A Note on the Real Story

Worth saying once: the real event was a tragedy in which a mother and two other adults died and four very young children nearly did. The game is entertainment built on the shape of it, not a retelling. The children are not characters in the game, and the monsters have nothing to do with them.
