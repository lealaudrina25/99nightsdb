## What You Are Actually Trying to Do

99 Nights in the Forest is a co-op survival game on Roblox by Grandma's Favourite Games. You join up to 24 other players at a campfire and try to survive 99 in-game nights while hostile entities hunt you in the dark.

Every run has the same shape:

1. **Day** — gather wood, food and materials. Push out further than yesterday.
2. **Dusk** — get back to camp. Feed the fire.
3. **Night** — hold the light. Entities patrol outside it.
4. **Repeat** — each night the pressure increases.

Runs end in one of two ways: the fire goes out, or someone wanders into the dark. Almost every early failure is one of those two things.

This whole section is about one object, so it is worth knowing what the six levels actually buy. [Campfire](/guides/campfire) lists the ladder, the fuel each step costs, and what the fire powers while it burns.

Nothing on that page matters before the fire does, and [Kiwi](/entities/the-kiwi) is the clearest proof, the cheapest tame in the game locked behind its most expensive level.

## The Campfire Is Everything

The campfire is the single most important object in the game. While it burns, the area around it is safe. When it dies, everything changes.

- **Feed it wood before it gets low.** A fire at 10% is a fire about to go out, and relighting it at night is how players die.
- **Assign one person to wood.** In a full camp, someone should be a Lumberjack or simply dedicated to hauling wood. Camps without one stall around night 15.
- **Do not stand at the edge of the light.** The safe zone is the lit circle, and the edge of it is where entities reach you first.

- **Cook before you eat.** [Chef and Cook](/classes/chef-class) — raw Morsel and Steak hurt you, and both food classes start from that one habit.

## Your First Day

Spend your first daylight cycle close to camp. Priorities in order:

1. **Wood** — the fire cannot run on promises. Fill it first.
2. **Food** — hunger drains constantly. Cook what you find rather than eating it raw.
3. **A weapon** — even a basic axe turns a losing fight into a survivable one.
4. **Scouting** — learn where the nearest structures are while it is still bright.

Do not chase distant loot on day one. Distance is the thing that kills you at dusk, and on your first run you do not yet know how long it takes to walk back.

One rule that sits alongside them: sprinting empties the bar far faster than walking, so the run home costs food as well as time. See [**Hunger**](/guides/hunger).

One rule that sits alongside them: if someone goes down in the open, carry the body back to camp before reviving. The full system is on the [**death and respawn**](/guides/death-and-respawn) page.

The first nights decide whether you get to use a gun at all, and our [Ammo and Guns](/guides/ammo-guide) guide is the supply side of that answer.


**Stay in the light.** This is the whole rule. The Deer patrols outside the campfire's safe zone at night and pursues anyone who leaves it.

**The Deer cannot be killed.** A flashlight stuns it briefly, which is your only reliable escape tool. Do not treat it as a boss to farm. If you are reading this because you want to know how to kill it — you cannot. Escape, stun, and return to the light.

**Cultists are different.** They are hostile during both day and night and will push into your camp. They are the reason wall and defence builds matter later on.

**Do not leave camp alone at night.** If a run needs doing after dark, take someone. A downed player outside the light is usually a dead player.

Each rescue is +1 day per night, and four of them are what a [speedrun in 99 Nights in the Forest](/guides/speedrun-guide) is built on.

A rescue is a locked cave, a key and a pack to clear — the whole idea in one step, expanded in our [Keys and Locked Doors](/guides/keys-and-locked-doors) guide.

How a run actually finishes is a question almost nobody answers up front. The true ending needs every missing child back in camp before Day 99 resolves, and [how to win 99 Nights in the Forest](/guides/how-to-win-99-nights) takes the win condition out of the campfire and ammo advice it usually gets buried under.

## Rescue the Missing Kids Early

There are four missing children hidden across the map, in different structures. Rescuing them is one of the highest-value things you can do early:

- It unlocks extra badges.
- The children later take on jobs that produce food and resources for the camp.
- Some of the associated badges are the easiest ones in the game to earn.

Each kid spawns in a different structure, and newer updates have added more of them with their own job roles. Check the missing kids location page for the current list before you start searching blindly.

Buying combat before economy is the one that costs the most time to undo. The cheapest fix is a permanent food supplier — see [**Fisherman Class**](/classes/fisherman-class) for what 50 Diamonds buys.

Crafting a tenth bandage costs more than the first five did, and the arithmetic behind that is in our [Healing, Bandages and Medkits](/guides/healing-guide) guide.

Treating the Deer as a boss to farm is on the mistake list, and our [Bosses](/guides/boss-guide) guide explains why it cannot be killed.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why it ends runs |
|---|---|
| Letting the fire drop below 20% | Relighting at night pulls you away from safety |
| Wandering out at dusk for one more item | You will not make it back before dark |
| Fighting the Deer | It cannot be killed — stun and leave |
| Eating raw food | Hunger returns faster than you think |
| Spending Diamonds on two half-classes | Diamonds only unlock classes; commit to one |
| Skipping the missing kids | You lose free food production and easy badges |

If diamonds are your next step, the cheapest badge route, ranked by work per diamond, is on our [All Badges](/guides/all-badges) page.

Fishing is the cheapest next step and the one that pays for itself first: 20 Mossy Coins buys renewable food, ammo and medical supplies with no ammo spent getting them. [**Our fishing guide**](/guides/fishing-guide) starts from walking up to the right NPC.
