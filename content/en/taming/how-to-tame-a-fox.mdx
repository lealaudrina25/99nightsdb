The **Arctic Fox** is the cheapest, easiest combat pet you can field in the Snow Biome — and one of the few snow animals a Good Taming Flute handles. It is fast, annoying in packs, and cheap to feed: **3 Steak + 3 Chili across three cycles**. This page is the practical how-to; the fox's stat line, drop table and pack behaviour are on [The Arctic Fox](/entities/the-arctic-fox), and the full per-animal food table is on [Taming Guide](/taming/taming-guide).

Last verified: **2026-09-30**.

## What You Need Before You Start

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Good Taming Flute** | The Tier 2 flute (see below) |
| **3 Steak + 3 Chili** | 1 Steak + 1 Chili per cycle × 3 cycles — get them from [Pet Food](/taming/pet-food) |
| **A free, undamaged Arctic Fox** | Found in the Snow Biome; not a fox you have already hit |

The Good Flute exists for animals like the Arctic Fox; [Kiwi](/entities/the-kiwi) is the one that never climbs that far, and ours is the page on what the gate it does need actually costs.

## Step 1 — Get the Good Taming Flute

The Arctic Fox needs the **Good Taming Flute**, the Tier 2 flute. You do **not** start with it — the flute is a tier you earn:

- You begin with (or find) the **Old Taming Flute** at the **Domestication Cabin** (also called the Abandoned Animal Shelter) near camp.
- Tame easy animals — **Bunnies and Wolves** — to fill the flute's XP bar. See [How to Tame a Wolf](/taming/how-to-tame-a-wolf) for the fastest early XP route.
- Once the bar is full, take the flute to the **Upgrade Station** (the Workshop, shown as crossed tools on the map) to upgrade Old → Good. The upgrade chain, the bench and its campfire gate are on the [Taming Flute](/items/taming-flute) page.

The full tier list and upgrade path are on [Taming Guide](/taming/taming-guide). A note on sources: most published taming tables (games.gg, sportskeeda, 99-nights.wiki, bloxodes) list the Arctic Fox under the **Good** Flute; one guide (rosenberryrooms) lists it under the Old Flute. Because flute tiers are reported inconsistently between guides, and the game's own prompt is the authority, **bring a Good Flute to be safe** — if your flute tier does not match, the mini-game simply will not start.

Fox spawns ride the same cold-weather table that snow biomes use, which our [Weather and Night Events](/guides/weather-and-night-events) guide documents.


Arctic Foxes live only in the **Snow Biome**, and they spawn in **packs of 2–5** (occasionally alone). Most guides agree they appear once your campfire reaches **Level 2+**, so you usually meet them while chasing the [Polar Bear](/entities/the-polar-bear) or the Mammoth.

The Snow Biome itself, the freeze meter, and why you go there are covered on [Snow Biome](/locations/snow-biome).

Find a calm fox away from the others — and, crucially, **do not damage it before you start**. A fox you have already hit will not take the flute.

## Step 3 — Play the Mini-Game

Walk up and step into the **green circle** around the fox. A vertical bar appears with a light-green target zone and a red marker. Hold to push the marker up, release to let it fall; keep it inside the green zone until the progress bar fills. Each completed attempt pacifies the fox long enough to feed it and flips one "face" above its head to happy.

Because foxes are small and fast, the mini-game is easy — easier than a Wolf. Older guides say the **Zookeeper** class widens the green zone and makes it trivial; that minigame perk has since been replaced, so check the current perks on [Zookeeper class](/classes/zookeeper-class).

## Step 4 — Feed It

After each successful mini-game, the fox asks for food. Feed it **1 Steak + 1 Chili**, then repeat. Three cycles total:

| Cycle | Food |
|---|---|
| 1 | 1 Steak + 1 Chili |
| 2 | 1 Steak + 1 Chili |
| 3 | 1 Steak + 1 Chili |
| **Total** | **3 Steak + 3 Chili** |

When every face flips to hearts, the fox is yours. **Steak** drops from wolves and bears; **Chili** scatters randomly across the map (and grows from Chili Seeds), so grab it whenever you see it — the ingredient sources are on [Pet Food](/taming/pet-food).

## Rules That Break Taming

- **The fox must be undamaged when you start.** If it has already taken a hit, the flute will not work — let it reset or find another in the pack.
- **Match the prompt exactly.** The food a fox asks for can shift between runs or patches, so always read the speech bubble in your own run before you commit your stack.
- **One at a time.** Foxes are pack animals; if you aggro one, the whole group joins. Pull a single fox away from the pack before you start the flute, or you will be swarmed mid-tame.

## What the Arctic Fox Pelt Is For

Killing a fox drops an **Arctic Fox Pelt (0–1)**. Important: the Arctic Fox Pelt is **not** wanted by the Pelt Trader, and it cannot be turned into biofuel. Its **only** use is crafting **warm clothing** — a Beanie or an Arctic Fox Hat — at the Warm Clothing Shop; the full five-piece table is on [Warm Clothing](/items/warm-clothing). So tame the fox for the companion, and only hunt it for pelts if you need cold-resistance gear.

The pelts that *do* trade at the Pelt Trader are **Wolf, Alpha Wolf and Bear** pelts — see [How to Get the Rifle](/weapons/how-to-get-rifle) for what the trader's ladder unlocks; the ladder itself, with the fourth and fifth rungs, is on [The Pelt Trader](/items/pelt-trader).

## The Two-Pet Limit

You can keep **two tamed pets** at once (three as Zookeeper, five as [Beastmaster](/classes/beastmaster-class) — the caps are on [Best Class](/classes/best-class)). To tame a new one, **release** an existing pet first (it despawns). An Arctic Fox is a cheap, fast distractor, so it pairs well with a tanky [Bear](/entities/the-bear) or Polar Bear.

## Sources

The **Good Flute + 3 cycles + Steak ×3 + Chili ×3** recipe is consistent across games.gg, sportskeeda, 99-nights.wiki and bloxodes, all checked 2026-09-30. The **Arctic Fox Pelt → warm clothing only (not the Pelt Trader, not biofuel)** fact is corroborated by 99-nights-in-the-forest.com, the miraheze wiki and pockettactics.
