The honest bottleneck in taming is **food, not fighting**. You can clear the mini-game and win the hearts, but if you turn up without the right ingredient the animal stays angry and the night arrives. This page is organised by **ingredient** — what needs it, and where to actually get it. For the per-animal cycle counts and flute tiers, see [Taming Guide](/en/taming/taming-guide); for which pet is worth the food, see [Best Pet](/en/taming/best-pet).

Last verified: **2026-09-29**.

## Read This First

- **Raw meat damages you.** Morsel, Steak and Rib all hurt when eaten raw. Cook them at the campfire (or in a Crock Pot) before they are useful — and before you feed them to an animal.
- **Read the speech bubble in your own run.** The Wolf and Alpha Wolf food requirements are the two that shift between runs and between published guides. Everything below is the most commonly reported version; your run may differ by a Morsel.
- **You only hold 2 pets by default** (3 as Zookeeper, 5 as Beastmaster) — see [Best Pet](/en/taming/best-pet). Food is what you spend to reach the animal you actually want, so don't waste Steak on a Bunny.

| Ingredient | Needed by | Where to get it |
|---|---|---|
| **Carrot** | Bunny | Farm Plots, the Grocery Store, scattered in the world |
| **Berry** | Kiwi | Berry Seeds (grow in Farm Plots) or foraged in the world |

The food sources above are corroborated across backyarddrunkard's consumables list, games.gg and sportskeeda's taming tables, all checked 2026-09-29.

## The Two That Actually Hurt

**Steak** and **Morsel** are what most runs live or die on, because the Wolf — your first real combat pet — wants both. The good news: wolves are everywhere, and a single wolf drops 1 Steak plus 2–3 Morsels. Farm the wolf packs that wander near your camp and the Wolf's food pays for itself quickly. See [The Wolf](/en/entities/the-wolf) for the fight and tame details.

**Pumpkin and Cake** are the endgame wall. A Mammoth wants 10 Pumpkin + 5 Cake across five cycles, and Polar Bear wants a huge amount of Stew. Build as many Farm Plots as you can, dedicate them to Pumpkins, and **do not eat your Cake for healing** if you are saving for a Mammoth — rest at the campfire instead.

## Setting Up the Farm

The campfire and Farm Plots are a wood problem before they are a food problem — see [How to Get Wood Fast](/en/items/how-to-get-wood-fast) for the campfire that cooks your Stew and Cake.

- **Farm Plots** grow Carrot, Corn, Pumpkin, Chili and Cake (from seeds). Berry and Chili also regrow from seeds foraged in the world.
- **Ponds** give Mackerel (and Salmon) for the Green Frog — fish with a rod once your campfire is high enough to unlock the better ponds.
- **Hunt, don't buy.** Steak, Morsel and Rib come from animals, not shops. Wolves for the early pets, Bears and Mammoths for the late ones.
- **Cook everything.** Raw meat is a trap. A functioning campfire turns Morsel/Steak/Rib from liability into food, and the Crock Pot turns them into Stew for the apex pets.

## What NOT to Do

- **Don't tame on an empty stomach.** Bring the exact food; going back to camp mid-tame usually means the animal wanders or night falls.
- **Don't eat your taming food.** That Steak was for the Wolf, not for you — unless you have surplus.
- **Don't skip Farm Plots.** Every apex pet bottlenecks on Pumpkin/Cake; the plots are the only reliable supply.
- **Don't trust a single guide's food count.** Wolf and Alpha Wolf requirements vary by run — verify in your own bubble.
