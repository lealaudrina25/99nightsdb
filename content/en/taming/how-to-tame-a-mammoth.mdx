The Mammoth is the highest-HP creature in 99 Nights in the Forest — **800 HP**, more than any other animal in the game — and taming one turns that wall of health into a tank that follows you home. It is also a whole-run project: the food bill is the largest of any tame, and the ingredient that gates it is the one people forget to farm.

This page covers exactly what it wants, where to find one, and the parts that actually cost people the tame. For the animal itself — its 30 damage, the knockback, why your pets cannot reach it — see [The Mammoth](/entities/the-mammoth).

Last verified: **2026-09-30**.

## Quick Answer

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Flute | **Strong Taming Flute** (Tier 3) — lower tiers cannot start it |
| Cycles | **5** |
| Food | **2 Pumpkin + 1 Cake per cycle** = **10 Pumpkin + 5 Cake total** |
| Where | **Snow Biome only** |
| Level gate | Campfire **level 4+** (one source — treat as likely) |
| HP | **800** — the highest in the game |
| Real bottleneck | **Cake**, not Pumpkin |

A Mammoth's tame is billed in Pumpkin and Cake rather than Steak, so it is the one apex pet our [Steak](/items/steak) page can mark as paying you instead of charging you.

## What You Get

The Mammoth is a **tank and a pack mule**. Its value is not damage — it is the fact that things take a very long time to kill it, so it holds a line, soaks a wave, and hauls loose items while you do something else. Our [Best Pet](/taming/best-pet) ranking puts it in the endgame tier and calls it "huge tank and pack mule, clears groups".

Two honest caveats:

- **It is the highest-HP animal, not the hardest-hitting one.** The [Polar Bear](/entities/the-polar-bear) does more damage per hit. If you want a killer, that is a different tame; if you want something that refuses to die, this is it.
- **A tamed Mammoth reportedly deals about 30 damage per hit.** That figure has one source, so treat it as approximate rather than a stat to plan around.

The Mammoth bills you in Cake and Pumpkin, where the [Berry](/items/berry) is the other end of the ladder, one fruit for a whole tame.

## Exactly What It Wants

This is the rare case where the internet agrees. **Five separate guides give the identical requirement**, which almost never happens in this game:

| | Per cycle | × 5 cycles |
|---|---|---|
| **Pumpkin** | 2 | **10** |
| **Cake** | 1 | **5** |

If you have seen conflicting numbers for the Mammoth elsewhere, you have probably seen the same requirement written two different ways — two foods per cycle versus ten across the whole tame. Both are correct. Our [Taming Guide](/taming/taming-guide) explains the same per-cycle-versus-total confusion across every animal.

- **Cake has two masters.** [Chef and Cook](/classes/chef-class) — the Mammoth tame wants 5 Cake, and the Chef's Carrot Cake is the only recipe that adds night vision to it.

Mammoth tame bills are dominated by Cake; [Kiwi](/entities/the-kiwi) is the mirror image, one Berry against a Campfire Level 6 fire that runs to roughly 275 logs and 170 coal.

The Mammoth bill is Pumpkin and Cake with no [Carrot](/items/carrot) in it at all, which makes the Bunny the opposite end of the same taming economy.

## Cake Is the Real Wall

Everyone plans for the Pumpkins. The Pumpkins are not the problem.

**Pumpkin** comes from Farm Plots, and plots are cheap — you can dedicate a row of them and forget about it. **Cake** is the slow one: it is found in structures away from camp, grown in Farm Plots, or cooked as a recipe, and its growth rate is the thing people complain about. Five Cakes does not sound like much until you are waiting on them.

Three practical rules:

1. **Start the Cake line before the Pumpkin line.** Pumpkin is throughput you can scale with more plots; Cake is the long pole.
2. **Do not eat your Cake for healing.** This is the single most repeated piece of taming advice in the community, and it is right — a Cake spent on your own hunger bar is a Cake you now have to grow again. Rest at the campfire instead.
3. **Build the maximum number of plots you can.** Our own [Pet Food](/taming/pet-food) page covers every ingredient source in detail, and its verdict matches this one: Pumpkin and Cake are the endgame wall, and the plots are the only reliable supply.

The campfire and the plots are a wood problem before they are a food problem — see [How to Get Wood Fast](/items/how-to-get-wood-fast).

The XP for that upgrade starts with Bunnies and Wolves, and our [Bunny](/entities/the-bunny) page is the fastest first tame on it.

The free Old flute comes from the Animal Shelter, mapped on our [Cabins](/locations/cabins) page.

The cake that blocks this tame needs a farm, and the [Fairy](/entities/the-fairy)'s seed shop is where one starts.

## Getting the Flute First

You cannot start this tame without a **Strong Taming Flute**. A Good or Old flute simply will not engage the animal, and no amount of food changes that.

The route, from our [Taming Guide](/taming/taming-guide): find an Old Taming Flute at the **Abandoned Animal Shelter** near camp, tame **Bunnies and Wolves** to fill the flute's XP bar, upgrade at the **Upgrade Station** (the Workshop, crossed-tools icon), repeat with an Alpha Wolf or two, then upgrade again to Strong. The three tiers, what each unlocks and what the upgrade costs are on the [Taming Flute](/items/taming-flute) page.

**Useful detail:** feeding an injured pet the food it was tamed with also builds flute XP. Healing your existing pets is not wasted downtime — it is flute progress.

And the sequencing mistake worth avoiding: **do not chase a Mammoth early.** Turning up with a half-levelled flute and ten Pumpkins is how you lose a rare spawn.

Mammoths only come out in one biome night, and our [Weather and Night Events](/guides/weather-and-night-events) guide lists what else shares that night.


**Snow Biome only.** There is no second location, and the biome is not guaranteed to generate on every map — Snow competes with Volcanic for the outer ring slot. See [Snow Biome](/locations/snow-biome) for the freeze meter and how to survive long enough to work in there.

Things that make finding one harder than it looks:

- **It is one of the hardest spawns in the game**, alongside the Bear. It is not a common sight even in the right biome.
- **A campfire gate is reported at level 4+.** That comes from a single source, so treat it as likely rather than certain.

**The good news: the Mammoth is neutral until attacked.** Unlike the Polar Bear, it does not open the fight for you. That means you can walk up to it and start the flute mini-game **without fighting it first** — which is exactly what you want, because a **damaged animal cannot be tamed**. Do not hit it, do not let your pet hit it, and clear nearby hostiles before you begin.

## The Tame, Step by Step

1. **Carry the whole bill before you start** — 10 Pumpkin and 5 Cake in your sack, not "most of it". Leaving mid-tame means the animal wanders, or night arrives.
2. **Clear the area.** A hostile interrupting the mini-game can reset your progress or kill the target.
3. **Start the mini-game** and keep the marker in the green band. Apex animals have the **narrowest band** in the game, so this is the stage where practice genuinely pays off — the Zookeeper minigame perk that older guides mention has been replaced, and practice is now the only thing softening the band (see [Zookeeper class](/classes/zookeeper-class)).
4. **Feed it 2 Pumpkin + 1 Cake** when it asks. One face turns happy.
5. **Repeat four more times.** Five happy faces flip to hearts and it is yours.

**A known glitch:** players report the Mammoth sometimes refusing food at a feeding step. If a prompt will not accept an item, disengage and come back rather than burning items trying to force it.

## Keeping It Alive

This is the part that matters most, because a Mammoth represents an enormous amount of farmed food.

**Do not send it into every fight.** The consistent advice is to pull pets away from dangerous groups and keep them out of environmental hazards. Two specific dangers:

- **Lava Mines.** Tamed animals can trigger them, which makes volcanic areas genuinely hazardous for companions — relevant if you tame the volcanic variant.
- **The Deer.** It does not care about your pet. Taming, or fighting, near nightfall is how people lose companions. See [The Deer](/entities/the-deer).

**On pet death, sources disagree and you should assume the worst.** One guide states a pet lost in combat is **lost permanently**; another says defeated pets **recover** and can be used again. We could not resolve it. Follow the same logic as everything else with asymmetric stakes: play as if the Mammoth is gone for good, because being wrong about that costs you ten Pumpkins and five Cakes, while being wrong the other way costs you nothing.

What is not disputed: **pets do not carry between runs**, a **2-pet limit** applies by default, and the **[Beastmaster](/classes/beastmaster-class)** class raises it to 5. The caps are on [Best Pet](/taming/best-pet), and the class comparison is on [Best Class](/classes/best-class).

## Variants With the Same Bill

Sources describe Mammoth variants in the other biomes, and the useful part is that **they share the exact same requirement** — 2 Pumpkin + 1 Cake per cycle. Same flute, same five cycles, same 10 + 5.

| Variant | Biome | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Mammoth** | Snow Biome | Neutral — the one this page is about |
| **Lava Mammoth** | Volcanic Biome | **The same animal as the Hellephant** — players used this name first. See [How to Tame a Hellephant](/taming/how-to-tame-a-hellephant) |
| **Mossy Mammoth** | Jungle Biome | Reported by one source — it spawns inside Jungle structures two at a time, see [Jungle Biome](/locations/jungle-biome) |

The Hellephant is **hostile** rather than neutral and has **less HP** than the Mammoth (500 against 800), so it is a genuinely harder tame despite the identical food bill. Its threat side — the charge, the ragdoll, and what killing one drops — is on [The Hellephant](/entities/the-hellephant).

So if you have already built the Cake farm, you have not built it for one animal. You have built it for all of them.

Taming costs shift with patches, and our [update log](/guides/updates-patch-notes) is where the changes are logged.

## FAQ

**What flute do I need?**
The Strong Taming Flute (Tier 3). A Good or Old flute cannot start the tame.

**How many cycles?**
Five.

**How much food in total?**
10 Pumpkins and 5 Cakes. That is 2 Pumpkin + 1 Cake after each of the five successful mini-games.

**Where do I find one?**
The Snow Biome, nowhere else. It is one of the rarest spawns in the game.

**Do I have to fight it first?**
No. The Mammoth is neutral until attacked — and it must be undamaged to be tamed, so do not attack it.

**What is the hardest part?**
The Cake. Pumpkin scales with Farm Plots; Cake does not keep up as easily.

Crops that are not on the Farm Plot list come from Seed Packs — our [Seed Packs](/items/seed-packs) page explains which.
**Can I tame it without the Beastmaster class?**
Yes. The class raises your pet limit and starts you with a flute, but it is not a requirement.

**What is the Mammoth actually good for?**
Tanking and hauling. It has the highest HP of any animal; the Polar Bear hits harder.
