The **Bear** is the first **apex** pet most players go for — a walking damage-soak with the same ~350 HP it has as an enemy. It is a much bigger project than the [Wolf](/taming/how-to-tame-a-wolf): a **Strong Flute**, **five cycles**, and a large food stack. This page is the practical how-to; stats and drops live on [The Bear](/entities/the-bear), and the full per-animal food table is on [Taming Guide](/taming/taming-guide).

Last verified: **2026-09-30**.

A bear asks for the Strong Flute and a hunt; [Kiwi](/entities/the-kiwi) inverts that completely, taking the Old Flute and one Berry with Campfire Level 6 standing in for everything the hunt would have cost.

Berry Seeds keep a berry line alive between forages — see our [Seed Packs](/items/seed-packs) page.
Five Steak plus Pumpkin and Salmon is less than it looks, because a Bear itself drops 3 to 4 Steak, and our [Steak](/items/steak) page works out that offset for every tame.

Every flute tier starts at the Animal Shelter for the free Old one, per our [Cabins](/locations/cabins) page.

Steak and Ribs are hunted, and the renewable half of a pet diet is the [Fairy](/entities/the-fairy)'s, covered on her page.

## What You Need Before You Start

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Strong Taming Flute** | The Tier 3 flute — the Old and Good flutes will NOT tame a Bear |
| **5 Steak + 5 Pumpkin + 5 Salmon** | 1 of each per cycle × 5 cycles (the most-cited set) |
| **A free, undamaged forest bear** | Not a night-spawned bear, not a guardian mid-fight |

The XP road to the Strong Flute starts with Bunnies and Wolves, and our [Bunny](/entities/the-bunny) page is the cheapest first step on it.

## Step 1 — Get the Strong Taming Flute

The Strong Taming Flute is the **Tier 3** flute. You do not start with it — you earn it by taming lower-tier animals first (Bunnies and Wolves build XP, then an Alpha Wolf or two), then take the flute to the **Upgrade Station** (the Workshop). The full tier ladder is on [Taming Guide](/taming/taming-guide). Practically: tame Wolves → upgrade to Good → tame an Alpha Wolf → upgrade to Strong. The [Taming Flute](/items/taming-flute) page covers the three tiers, what each unlocks and the pet slots each one buys.

Taming a bear instead of killing six of them is how you skip the Grey Key, the shortcut our [Keys and Locked Doors](/guides/keys-and-locked-doors) guide points at.

## Step 2 — Find the Right Bear

A tameable Bear shows up in the deeper **Forest** (once the campfire is around Level 5–6), at the **Cultist Hunter Camp**, and inside **Locked Caves** (as a guardian). Two will not take the flute:

- Bears that **spawn on you during a night raid**.
- A **guardian Bear** you have already started fighting — it must be undamaged.

Find a calm, undamaged bear and **do not damage it before you start**.

## Step 3 — Play the Mini-Game

Walk into the **green circle** around the bear. A vertical bar appears with a light-green target zone and a red marker; hold to push the marker up, release to let it fall, keep it inside the green zone until the bar fills. A Bear is a **hard** mini-game — the green zone is small and the timing is tight, much stricter than the [Wolf](/taming/how-to-tame-a-wolf). Older guides credited the **Zookeeper** class with softening this, but that perk has been replaced — see [Zookeeper class](/classes/zookeeper-class) for what the class does now.

Each completed attempt pacifies the bear long enough to feed it and flips one face above its head.

Salmon is the one ingredient on that list you cannot reliably hunt, which is why established teams angle for it instead. [**Fishing**](/guides/fishing-guide) is its only renewable source, and the water you want is not the cold one.

The Bear's bill is Steak and Morsel, so the tame that could have been fed entirely on [Carrot](/items/carrot) is not this one.

Bears do not take [Berries](/items/berry), but the Kiwi does, and one is the whole bill.

## Step 4 — Feed It

After each successful mini-game, the bear asks for food. The most-cited set is **1 Steak + 1 Pumpkin + 1 Salmon**; feed that, then repeat. Five cycles total:

| Cycle | Food |
|---|---|
| 1 | 1 Steak + 1 Pumpkin + 1 Salmon |
| 2 | 1 Steak + 1 Pumpkin + 1 Salmon |
| 3 | 1 Steak + 1 Pumpkin + 1 Salmon |
| 4 | 1 Steak + 1 Pumpkin + 1 Salmon |
| 5 | 1 Steak + 1 Pumpkin + 1 Salmon |
| **Total** | **5 Steak + 5 Pumpkin + 5 Salmon** |

One guide (sportskeeda) reports a **Steak + Stew + Pumpkin** variant instead of Salmon — same 1-of-each-per-cycle × 5 structure. When every face flips to hearts, the bear is yours.

## Rules That Break Taming

- **The bear must be undamaged when you start.** If it has already taken a hit, the flute will not work.
- **You need the Strong Flute.** An Old or Good Flute cannot start a Bear tame — do not show up with the wrong tier.
- **Match the prompt exactly.** The food a bear asks for can vary (Salmon vs Stew), so always read the speech bubble in your own run before you commit your stack.
- **Bring the whole stack.** Five cycles means five full food sets; turning up short means a reset.

## The Two- (or Three-) Pet Limit

You can keep **two tamed pets** by default, **three** as Zookeeper or **five** as [Beastmaster](/classes/beastmaster-class). A tamed Bear is a long-term investment — most players tame one Wolf early for cheap fight help, then swap to a Bear once the Strong Flute is ready. See [Best Pet](/taming/best-pet) for whether the Bear is worth the food versus other animals.

Limits like that have changed since launch, and our [update log](/guides/updates-patch-notes) tracks when.

## Sources

The **Strong Flute + 5 cycles + Steak/Pumpkin/Salmon** setup is consistent across bloxodes, mmojugg and pockettactics, all checked 2026-09-30. sportskeeda reports a Steak + Stew + Pumpkin variant. Exact food can shift with balance patches, so always confirm with the on-screen prompt in your own run.
