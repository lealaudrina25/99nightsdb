## What Codes Give You

Codes in 99 Nights in the Forest are released by the developers around updates and seasonal events. They do not give you weapons, pets or Robux — anyone promising that is lying. What they do give is currency:

- **Diamonds** — the main currency. Spent on permanently unlocking classes.
- **Gems** — a secondary currency used for shop purchases and some event rewards.
- **Flames** — spent at the supply crate to change which events run during a night.

Because Diamonds are the only way to unlock classes, codes are the fastest free route to a better class. Do not spend what you claim immediately — save toward one class that fits how you play rather than half-buying two.

## How to Redeem Codes

The redeem box is inside the shop, not in a separate menu, which is why many players cannot find it.

1. Load into 99 Nights in the Forest and wait until you are fully spawned in.
2. Open the **Currency Shop** — tap your Gems counter or the shop icon.
3. Click the **Codes** button in the bottom-right corner of that window.
4. Type the code **exactly as written**. Codes are case-sensitive, so capitalising a letter will fail.
5. Press **Redeem**. The reward lands instantly.

Each code is one-time per account. If the game tells you the code is already redeemed, that is not a bug — you or someone on your account has claimed it before.

## Tips and Common Mistakes

**Check back after every Saturday update.** The developers ship patches roughly every one to two weeks, and new codes almost always drop alongside them.

**Redeem before you need the Diamonds.** Codes can disappear between the moment you read them and the moment you log in. There is no advantage to waiting.

**Do not trust code generator sites.** There is no such thing as a working generator for this game. If a site asks you to log in or complete a task to unlock a code, leave.

**Save for one class, not two.** Diamonds only unlock classes. Thirty Diamonds will not buy you a good class and a backup class — it buys one. Pick the role you want to play and commit.
