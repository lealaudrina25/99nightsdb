Almost every food guide in 99 Nights in the Forest files the Berry under the same heading as the Carrot: pick it off the ground, one fork of hunger, keep walking. That is accurate, and it is also the least interesting true thing about the Berry.

The Berry is worth exactly **one fork** — the smallest restore in the game, with no heal and no buff. But it is the only item that does three unrelated jobs at once. It is food. It is the entire purchase price of the [cheapest tame in the game](/entities/the-kiwi). And it is one of four foods that walk through the Hard Mode rot clock untouched. Read those three facts together and they all say the same thing: **the Berry is the food you should not eat.**

This page prices the Berry against the four foods that already have pages — [Carrot](/items/carrot), [Chili](/items/chili), [Pumpkin](/items/pumpkin) and [Steak](/items/steak) — and against the one pet it buys.

Last verified: **2026-10-08**.

## Quick Answer

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What it is | A bush-grown raw food. **One fork** of hunger, no health, no buff |
| Where it comes from | **Berry bushes**, scattered across the map in every biome — a standing wild population, no planting needed |
| Where the seed comes from | Grocery Store, the Fairy, Chests (Good and Legendary), the Food Warehouse, Farmer Cletus |
| Regrows? | **No.** A picked bush does not come back. [Berry Seeds](/items/seed-packs) are the renewable route, one in-game day to grow |
| Who wants it | **The [Kiwi](/entities/the-kiwi)** — the whole taming bill is **one Berry, one cycle, on the Old Flute** |
| Can you cook it? | **No.** The [Crock Pot](/locations/crafting-bench) rejects berries and peppers outright |
| Hard Mode, 60% Corruption | **Never rots.** The Berry is one of four foods (with carrot, strawberry and apple) on the never-rot list |
| Worth carrying? | Yes — but as a tame currency and a reserve, not as a meal |

## What One Berry Actually Does

The Berry has the shortest stat line in the game, and rblxguide is unusually blunt about it: "a single hunger icon and a flat None for effects, so there is no healing, no buff and no hidden upside."

On our own [Hunger](/guides/hunger) page's fork scale, one fork is "a snack" — the same rung as a carrot, an apple, a cake slice, and most raw meat. Nothing about the Berry beats any of them for the slot it occupies. It is the entry-level item in the food chain, and every external crop table agrees on the number:

| Food | Hunger | Extra effect | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Berry** | **1 fork** | none | rblxguide, dinokid.wiki, our Hunger page |
| [Carrot](/items/carrot) | 1 fork | none | our Hunger page |
| [Chili](/items/chili) | 1 fork | short speed boost | our Hunger page |
| [Pumpkin](/items/pumpkin) | 4 forks | none | our Hunger page |

So if the Berry were only a snack, this page would not exist. It exists because the snack is the wrong thing to spend the Berry on.

## The Three Jobs of a Single Berry

A Berry in your hand is worth three different amounts depending on what you do with it, and the amounts are not close.

| Use | What one Berry is worth |
|---|---|
| **Eat it** | One fork — a sixth of what a Pumpkin gives you for the same slot |
| **Tame with it** | **A pet.** One Berry plus one cycle on the Old Flute is the whole Kiwi bill |
| **Bank it (Hard Mode)** | **Indefinite storage.** It is on the never-rot list, so it survives the 90-second rot clock that eats almost everything else |

Eating is the only one of the three that wastes the item. The rest of this page is the arithmetic behind the other two.

## The Berry That Buys a Pet

There is exactly one animal in the game whose entire taming bill is a Berry, and it is the cheapest tame you can name.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Animal | [Kiwi](/entities/the-kiwi) |
| Flute | **Old Taming Flute** — the one you spawn with |
| Stages | **1** |
| Food, the whole bill | **Berry ×1** |
| Where | The map edge, behind Campfire Level 6 |

Eurogamer, 99-nights.wiki and our own [Taming Guide](/taming/taming-guide) all print the same row: Kiwi, 1 Berry, one stage, Old Flute. Our [Pet Food](/taming/pet-food) page lists Berry as the Kiwi's ingredient, and our [Kiwi](/entities/the-kiwi) page is where the catch lives — the tame is cheap, the **door** is the most expensive step in the game, because the Kiwi sits behind Campfire Level 6.

That changes what a Berry is worth, but it does not change the point. Compare the two cheapest bills in the game:

| Pet | Flute | Bill |
|---|---|---|
| [Bunny](/entities/the-bunny) | Old | 1 Carrot, 1 cycle |
| [Kiwi](/entities/the-kiwi) | Old | **1 Berry**, 1 cycle |

The Bunny and the Kiwi are the only two one-stage Old-Flute tames in the game, and the only difference in the food is Carrot versus Berry. A Wolf, by comparison, wants three cycles of six Steak. So one Berry is not a snack — it is a pet, and it is the cheapest pet in the game that is not a Carrot.

## Where Berries Come From

Berry bushes are one of the map's standing resources, not a spawn you wait on. rblxguide describes them as "berry bushes scattered across the map" that "can be replanted from berry seeds," and the 99nightsintheforest.net food guide rates Berry Bushes as a **Common** gathering source "especially for beginners." Our own [Hunger](/guides/hunger) page lists the source as "across the map, and from Berry Seeds."

The practical read: any biome, any day, walk a bush line and you will find some. There is no campfire level, no biome and no tool between you and a Berry — which is exactly why it is the item nobody starves for on Day 1, and exactly why it is easy to undervalue.

## Does a Berry Grow Back?

No. This is the one hard rule about the Berry, and it is the reason Berry Seeds matter at all.

dinokid.wiki states it in its crop table without hedging: Berry "does not regrow on its own; plant Berry Seed Packs for a renewable supply." Our own [Chef Class](/classes/chef-class) page says the same thing — "around the map and berry seeds, does not regrow on its own" — and our [Hunger](/guides/hunger) page repeats it for both berries and chili: "berries and chili do not grow back on their own, you need their seeds."

So a run has a **finite wild berry stock**. Clear the bushes near your base and they are gone for good. The only way to keep a berry line running is the seed route, and the seed route has a one-day lead time.

| Step | Cost | Who agrees |
|---|---|---|
| Find a Berry Seed Pack | Found, bought or traded | bloxodes, 99-nights-in-the-forest.com, our [Seed Packs](/items/seed-packs) |
| Use it | Free, one action | bloxodes, dinokid.wiki |
| Wait | **1 in-game day** | bloxodes, 99-nights-in-the-forest.com, dinokid.wiki |
| Harvest | Berries, back on bushes | 99-nights-in-the-forest.com |
| Regrowth after harvest | **None** — plant again | dinokid.wiki, our Seed Packs page |

## Every Route to Berry Seeds

Berry Seeds are the standard Food-category packet: they grow Berry plants, they take one in-game day, and they solve hunger rather than a buff. The seed is the bottleneck, so it is worth knowing all the doors.

| Route | What it takes | Repeatable | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Grocery Store** | Buy with Mossy Coins | Yes | bloxodes, our [Seed Packs](/items/seed-packs) |
| **The Fairy** | Trade flowers for packets | Yes, if you keep picking flowers | bloxodes, 99-nights-in-the-forest.com |
| **Chests** | **Good** Chests carry Berry Seeds; **Legendary** Chests carry Berry, Flower and Chili Seeds | Unbounded by luck | our [Chest Tiers](/items/chest-tiers); bloxodes |
| **Food Warehouse** | Loot the building; it holds boxes of Berry Seeds | One-time per run | 99-nights-in-the-forest.com; our Seed Packs page |

Note the shape of that table. Every other seed route in the game is a repeatable action; the Food Warehouse is a one-time structure, and the [Fairy](/items/seed-packs) wants flowers you have to pick first. The Grocery Store is the simplest door if you have Mossy Coins, and Chests are the only route that does not cost you a resource at all — Good Chests are the common tier, so a Berry Seed Pack is one of the cheaper things a chest can hand you.

## Why the Crock Pot Rejects It

The [Crock Pot](/locations/crafting-bench) is the one machine that turns basic food into better food, and it has an explicit blacklist. dinokid.wiki spells it out: the Crock Pot "explicitly excludes Berries — some older write-ups list them as a stew ingredient, but the current bench does not accept them." Our own [Crafting Bench](/locations/crafting-bench) and [Chef Class](/classes/chef-class) pages say the same in one line: the Crock Pot "rejects berries and peppers."

That is a genuinely unusual position for a food. The Carrot goes into Stew. The Pumpkin goes into Stew and Pumpkin Soup. Steak and Morsel and Rib go into the meat dishes. The Berry is the only basic food in the game that **cannot be improved**. What you pick is what you get — one fork, forever.

For a food page that usually reads as a downside. For the Berry it is the point: because it cannot be cooked away, the Berry stays available for the two uses that beat eating, and it never disappears into a recipe you did not plan.

## Hard Mode: The Four Foods That Never Rot

The Hard Mode Corruption ladder eventually turns storage into a lie. Our own [Hard Mode](/guides/hard-mode) page sets the rungs: Hungry Deer from 15%, reduced passive healing at 25%, stronger raids at 35%, **Food Rot Spores at 60%**, extreme raids at 75%, healing stopped at 80%, poison spores at 100%.

At the 60% step the fandom Hard Mode page splits every food in the game into three buckets:

- Food dropped by normal animals **rots after 90 seconds**.
- Food grown in Farm Plots **rots after 90 seconds**.
- Food that spawns on the map — **carrots, berries, strawberries, apples** — **never rots**.

The Berry is on the third list, and it is the member of that list you are most likely to actually be holding. A map Carrot is usually spent on the [Bunny](/entities/the-bunny) tame the moment you get it. An Apple is a Grocery Store item, not a foraged one. A Strawberry is contested — some sources treat it as a separate plant, and it is not one of the nine seed packs. The Berry, by contrast, is common, foraged, and bankable.

So in Hard Mode the Berry flips from "worst food" to "**the safest food to carry**." When the spores are up and every cooked meal in your Sack has a 90-second fuse, the item that survives the walk home is a bush Berry. Eat the Pumpkin first — it is going to rot anyway. Save the Berry.

## Where Sources Disagree

Six places where the published material contradicts itself or us. We list them rather than average them; this is the state of the sources as of 2026-10-08.

1. **Does a Berry regrow?** dinokid.wiki and our own Chef Class and Hunger pages say no — "does not regrow on its own." But our [Chili](/items/chili) page describes carrot, berry and apple as "found food" with "a standing population that the map maintains," which reads as though berries keep coming back somewhere. The honest reading: the map starts with a standing stock, but a picked bush does not respawn — the two statements are about different things and only one of them is about your run.
2. **Is a Berry a Farm Plot crop?** Our own [Pet Food](/taming/pet-food) page says one row that Berry Seeds "grow in Farm Plots," while the Farm Plot crop list we use elsewhere is Carrot, Corn, Pumpkin and Cake — and Berry is not on it. Same page, two answers. The seed-packet route is the one every source agrees on.
3. **How much hunger is a Berry worth?** Every table we trust — rblxguide, dinokid.wiki, and our own [Hunger](/guides/hunger) page — puts it at **one fork**. The 99nightsintheforest.net food guide puts Berries at **20%** of the bar, which is the same site our [Seed Packs](/items/seed-packs) page already flags as unreliable for its twelve-crop seed list and three-to-seven-day growth times. We use the fork figure.
4. **Where can you get Berry Seeds?** bloxodes and our Seed Packs page list the Grocery Store, the Fairy and Chests. The 99-nights-in-the-forest.com Berry Seeds page lists the Food Warehouse, the Fairy, a Good Chest and a Legendary Chest — and **no Grocery Store at all**. The Grocery Store route is the one you should not plan around if the store is far.
5. **Is Strawberry a separate item?** The never-rot list names strawberries alongside berries, and the Breeze wiki's six-outcome seed packet includes them — but the game's nine seed packs have no Strawberry pack, and the only place a strawberry ever appears for sale is our [Pelt Trader](/items/pelt-trader) page's note that the Fairy sells strawberry seed boxes. Whether Strawberry is a distinct plant or an older name for the Berry is unresolved in the sources.
6. **How many things can one seed packet place?** Fandom's older Chili article says one packet places one of **four** — Chili, Flowers, Fireflies, Berries. The Breeze wiki lists **six**, adding Strawberries and Trees. If your packet lands on a Flower, you did not get a Berry.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Where do I get Berries?**
Pick them off **berry bushes scattered across the map** in any biome. They are a common foraging item and need no tool, no plot and no campfire level. For a supply that keeps going, plant a **Berry Seed Pack** and wait one in-game day.

**Do berries grow back after I pick them?**
No. A picked bush does not respawn, so a run's wild berry stock is finite. Keep a line going with [Berry Seeds](/items/seed-packs) — the packet is the renewable route.

**What does one Berry give me?**
**One fork of hunger**, nothing else — no health, no buff. On our [Hunger](/guides/hunger) page's scale that is "a snack," the same as a carrot or an apple.

**Why would I save a Berry instead of eating it?**
Because one Berry is the entire taming bill of the [Kiwi](/entities/the-kiwi) — one Berry, one cycle, Old Flute — and it is also one of only four foods that never rot at Hard Mode 60% Corruption. Eating it for a sixth of a Pumpkin wastes both uses.

**Can I cook berries into stew?**
No. The [Crock Pot](/locations/crafting-bench) rejects berries and peppers outright. Some older guides list berries as a stew ingredient, but the current bench does not accept them.

**Can I use berries to craft anything?**
One recipe, and it is single-source: the fandom wiki lists a Medkit as **2 Berries plus 1 Cloth** for 50 HP, and no other source we found publishes a Medkit recipe at all. Treat it as a lead, not a rule — as our [Healing Guide](/guides/healing-guide) says, the strongest heal in the game is otherwise a pure find.

**Do berries rot in Hard Mode?**
No. Berries are on the never-rot list — map-spawned food (carrots, berries, strawberries, apples) never rots, even at 60% Corruption when everything else gets a 90-second clock. That is what makes them worth banking in a hard run.

**Is the Berry the same as the Strawberry?**
Not confirmed. The never-rot list treats them as separate foods, but the nine seed packs include no Strawberry packet, and no external crop table we trust lists Strawberry as its own plant. We treat them as related but unresolved.
