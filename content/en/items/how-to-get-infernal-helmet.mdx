The Infernal Helmet is the best head slot in 99 Nights in the Forest and the only way to get one is to craft it — which means killing a boss you have to summon on purpose, plus six scorpions.

Last verified: **2026-09-30**.

## Quick Answer

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Recipe | **6 Scorpion Shells + 1 Cultist King Antler** |
| Crafted at | The **Anvil in the Tool Workshop** |
| Hard part | The Antler — it only drops from the Cultist King |
| What it does | Reduces damage taken, grants burning immunity |
| Worth it? | Yes, if you are spending real time in the Volcanic Biome |

## The Recipe

Every source that gives a number gives the same one:

| Material | Amount | How to get it |
|---|---|---|
| **Scorpion Shell** | 6 | Killed Scorpions, Volcanic Biome only |
| **Cultist King Antler** | 1 | The Cultist King boss — the only source |

Five independent sources agree on both the materials and the counts, which is unusually clean for this game. Take **8–10 Scorpion Shells rather than 6** if you are farming them in one trip: the shells are the easy half and running short after you already have the Antler is annoying.

## Where to Craft It

Craft it at the **Anvil, which sits in the Tool Workshop**. This is worth stating plainly because guides split the answer between "the Anvil" and "the Tool Workshop" — they are describing the same place, and at least one guide makes that explicit by telling you to unlock the Anvil *in the Tools Workshop area*.

To use it you need the Anvil repaired first: clear the cultists guarding the Tool Workshop, gather the scattered anvil pieces inside, and place them on the blueprint. One source adds that the Tool Workshop itself requires **campfire level 3 or higher**; that is a single-source claim, so treat it as likely rather than certain.

For more on the building itself — it is the large stone-brick structure with the anvil icon, also called the Anvil Building, with parts scattered across floors — see [How to Get the Chainsaw](/weapons/how-to-get-chainsaw), which crafts at the same station.

**One genuine outlier:** a Meteor Anvil recipe list includes the Infernal Helmet. The Meteor Anvil is a separate, event-only station that appears after a Meteor Shower from day 5 onward, so either the recipe is available at both stations or that list is wrong. If the normal Tool Workshop Anvil does not offer it to you, that is the second place to look.

## Farming Scorpion Shells

Scorpions spawn **only in the Volcanic Biome**. They are not hard to kill one at a time; they are dangerous in groups, because their attack applies a **poison damage-over-time** that stacks up fast.

- **Run the outer edges of the biome.** Multiple sources describe the perimeter as where scorpions are most common, and looping it is faster than hunting them one by one.
- **Kill them from range.** Their threat is the poison, not the burst damage. A ranged weapon removes the problem entirely.
- **Bring healing.** If you pull a nest, the poison will out-damage your health regen.

Scorpion Shells have a second use worth knowing: the **Big Game Hunter** class can consume them for a permanent damage buff. If you play that class, shells are not purely helmet fuel — see [All Classes](/classes/all-classes).

## Getting the Antler

This is the real gate. The **Cultist King Antler** drops from the Cultist King and from nothing else, and he has to be summoned:

1. Collect **4 Cultist Gems** — cultist kills or Volcano sacrifices. A [Cultist Stronghold](/locations/cultist-stronghold) run is the fastest way to gather cultists in one place.
2. Find the **Cultist King Base** in the Volcanic Biome.
3. Place one gem on each of the **four altars**; he spawns on the fourth.
4. Beat him and take the Antler.

Full walkthrough, tactics and his complete drop table are on [The Cultist King](/entities/the-cultist-king). He can reportedly be summoned repeatedly, so multiple helmets are possible if you want one per teammate.

## What It Actually Does — Read This Part

The Infernal Helmet **reduces incoming damage** and grants **immunity to burning**. The fire immunity is the reason to build it: the Volcanic Biome is full of enemies whose damage is fire and lava, and Hot Chests sit in the middle of lava pools.

**But the immunity does not cover everything, and sources disagree on how much.**

- **Two sources are explicit and pessimistic:** the helmet protects you from lava damage caused by **enemy attacks only**. If you step, fall or swim in **environmental lava**, you still take fatal damage.
- **One source is more generous**, saying stepping into lava pools no longer applies burning at all.

**Assume the pessimistic version.** If you believe the generous one and it is wrong, you die in a lava pool with your best helmet on. If you believe the pessimistic one and it happens to be generous, you only lose a little convenience. The downside is asymmetric, so treat terrain lava as lethal regardless.

Two smaller details from single sources: the helmet reportedly **cannot be transferred** to other players once equipped, and it is said to pair especially well with the **Fire Bandit** class. Verify both yourself before relying on them.

## Is It Worth Building?

**Yes, if the Volcanic Biome is where you are spending your time.** It is the difference between farming the biome comfortably and losing health to every encounter. The Cultist King fight is the barrier, not the scorpions.

**Skip it if you only dip into the biome occasionally.** You can loot the biome's Hot Chests without the helmet if you are careful about where you stand, and the Anvil, gems and boss fight add up to a real investment.

Either way, do not do it purely for the helmet: the same boss drops Infernal weapons, and the Antler is the reason to fight him regardless. See [How to Get the Infernal Sword](/weapons/how-to-get-infernal-sword) for that side of it.

## FAQ

**Can I find the Infernal Helmet in a chest?**
No. Every source agrees it is craft-only.

**Is 6 Scorpion Shells definitely the number?**
Yes for every source that states one. Nobody publishes a different count.

**Which station — Anvil or Tool Workshop?**
The Anvil inside the Tool Workshop. Guides naming either are describing the same station.

**Does it make me immune to lava?**
To lava damage from enemy attacks, yes. To standing in lava — probably not, and the sources that address it directly say you still die. Plan around that.

**Do I need a high campfire level?**
One source says campfire level 3 for the Tool Workshop. If wood is the bottleneck, start with [How to Get Wood Fast](/items/how-to-get-wood-fast).
