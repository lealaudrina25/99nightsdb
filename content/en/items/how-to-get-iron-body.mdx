The Iron Body is the **standard mid-game armor** of 99 Nights in the Forest: the first real step up from the Leather Body, and the piece most players are wearing on the night they start pushing into the Cultist Stronghold. The catch is that it is never crafted — you find it, or you trade a Bear Pelt for it.

## What It Actually Does — Read This Part

The Iron Body's job is simple: it **reduces the damage you take**, and it does so noticeably more than the Leather Body that most players spawn with or pick up early. Sources describe it as protecting the wearer "greatly," and it is the tier the game's own loot tables use as the mid-game target.

Two things about it are worth knowing before you go hunting:

**1. It has no special effect — and that is fine.** Unlike the [Thorn Body](/items/how-to-get-thorn-body), which reflects damage, or the Alien Armour, which feeds a tech meter, the Iron Body is a plain protection upgrade. For most of the game that is exactly what you want.

**2. Armor is permanent — so an Iron Body is a commitment.** The fandom wiki and rblxguide both state the same rule: **once a piece of armor is equipped it cannot be unequipped — not even by dying and reviving — and it can only be swapped for a *better* piece.** Practically, that means:

- If you are still on Leather and you can reach an Iron Body, **take it** — it is a strict upgrade and it will not lock you out of anything (the Alien and Thorn bodies are both above it).
- If you already have something better, an Iron Body is a **downgrade you cannot take**, so do not waste a Bear Pelt on it.

## Where the Iron Body Sits

There are **seven wearable armor variants** in the game (plus the held [Riot Shield](/items/how-to-get-riot-shield)). The Iron Body sits in the middle:

| Tier | Armor | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Early | Leather Body | What most players start in (Brawler spawns with it) |
| — | Vampire Cloak | Only from the Vampire class or the Hedge Maze; **not** better than Iron |
| — | Poison Armour | Poisons melee attackers; Frog Invasion / Poison Master |
| **Mid** | **Iron Body** | **This page** — the standard target for days 10–20 |
| Mid-high | Alien Armour | Above Iron; feeds Alien Tech Energy; Cyborg spawns with it |
| High | Thorn Body | Above Iron *and* Alien; reflects melee damage |
| End | Obsidiron Body | Highest; AoE explosion; Meteor event only |

So the honest summary is: **the Iron Body beats the Leather Body and the Vampire Cloak, and loses to the Alien, Thorn and Obsidiron bodies.** The Poison Armour is a specialist side-grade rather than a straight step on the same ladder. If you are choosing whether to take an Iron Body, ask what you are giving up — usually nothing.

## How to Get It

Three routes. You only need one.

### Route 1 — Loose in the Forest

The Iron Body can spawn as world loot, sitting out in the Forest rather than inside a chest. Both the fandom and Miraheze wikis list it alongside the chest sources. This is the luckiest route and the one you cannot plan for — but it is why you should **open every chest and check every structure**, not just the ones you were farming.

### Route 2 — Gold and Legendary Chests

The Iron Body is a documented result from both **Gold Chests** and **Legendary Chests**. Our own [Morningstar](/weapons/how-to-get-morningstar) page lists it independently: when you open a Gold Chest chasing the Morningstar, "Iron Body" is one of the other possible results alongside the Strong Axe and Giant Sack.

That means the Iron Body is effectively a **free by-product of chest runs you were already doing**. The most repeatable source of Gold and Legendary Chests in the game is the **Cultist Stronghold** loop — see [Cultist Stronghold](/locations/cultist-stronghold) for how it resets.

### Route 3 — The Pelt Trader's Fourth Trade

This is the only *guaranteed* route, and the one worth planning around.

The **Pelt Trader** — the fur-hatted NPC who appears near your campfire — runs a fixed **four-trade chain**, one trade per pelt type, each rarer than the last. **The Iron Body is tied to the fourth and final trade: the Bear Pelt.** Along with the Strong Flashlight (offered only if you do not already own one) and the Strong Axe, it is one of your three options at that trade.

Here is the full ladder, because the order matters:

| Trade | What he asks for | What you can pick |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | **Bunny Foot** | Good Axe · Good Sack · Old Flashlight |
| 2nd | **Wolf Pelt** | Spear · Revolver (a Leather Body appears on some lists here too) |
| 3rd | **Alpha Wolf Pelt** | Rifle · Strong Flashlight (a Giant Sack if you already own a flashlight) |
| 4th | **Bear Pelt** | Strong Flashlight (if needed) · **Iron Body** · Strong Axe |

**The trap: the Bear Pelt can only be spent once.** You pick *either* the Iron Body *or* the Strong Axe — not both. That is a real decision, because the Strong Axe is the one-hit-per-tree logging upgrade that makes a sapling farm work (see [How to Get Wood Fast](/items/how-to-get-wood-fast)). If your base is still starved for wood, the axe may be the better pick; if you are dying to cultists, take the Iron Body.

Three practical details about the trader:

- **He arrives on Day 2**, then returns **every 3–4 days**, and **leaves at nightfall** — so do the trade during daylight.
- **Each player has their own offers.** The trader is client-sided, so in multiplayer every player can get their own Iron Body; you are not competing for one.
- **The fourth trade sometimes does not trigger.** A known bug: after you hand over an Alpha Wolf Pelt, the trader may not ask for the Bear Pelt at all, cutting the chain short. If that happens, you wait for the next visit, or fall back to a chest.

**Hunter class bonus:** reaching **Hunter level 3** unlocks a **fifth trade**, one more reward on top of the usual four.

## Is It Worth Getting?

**Yes — it is the right piece to wear through the middle of a run.** It is the standard mid-game armor for a reason: it is a clear upgrade over Leather, it is easy to find as a by-product of chest farming, and it does not block you from upgrading into an Alien, Thorn or Obsidiron Body later.

**The only time to hesitate** is on the Bear Pelt trade. If you are still swinging a basic axe and wood is your bottleneck, the Strong Axe may pay off more than the armor — see [How to Get Wood Fast](/items/how-to-get-wood-fast). Everything else about the Iron Body is a straightforward "yes."

## FAQ

**Is the Iron Body craftable?**
No. There is no recipe. You find it (loose in the Forest, or in Gold / Legendary Chests) or you trade a Bear Pelt for it at the Pelt Trader.

**Which Pelt Trader trade gives the Iron Body?**
The fourth — the **Bear Pelt** trade. It is one of three options there, alongside the Strong Axe and the Strong Flashlight.

**Can I get the Iron Body *and* the Strong Axe from one Bear Pelt?**
No. You pick one. The Bear Pelt is spent once, so if you want both you need another Bear Pelt, another visit, or a chest.

**Is it better than the Leather Body?**
Yes, clearly — the Leather Body is the starter tier and the Iron Body is the first real upgrade above it.

**Is it better than the Thorn Body?**
No. The Thorn Body gives greater protection than the Iron Body, so if you can reach one, that is the better armor.

**Which classes start with it?**
None. Unlike the Leather Body (Brawler), Poison Armour (Poison Master), Alien Armour (Cyborg) and Vampire Cloak (Vampire), no class spawns wearing an Iron Body.
