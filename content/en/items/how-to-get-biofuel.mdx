**Biofuel** is the campfire fuel that does not care about the weather. Where a stack of logs sputters out in the rain, one Biofuel holds your fire at max intensity for minutes — and that is what keeps a deep run alive past night 50. This page covers how to build the **Biofuel Processor**, what to feed it, and how it stacks up against plain wood.

Last verified: **2026-09-29**.

## What Biofuel Actually Is

Biofuel is a crafted **fuel item** made at the **Biofuel Processor**. Its only job is keeping the campfire alive — but it does that job about **twice as well as a log**, and unlike logs it is **not weakened by rain**. That combination is why late-game bases run on it.

## How to Build the Biofuel Processor

The Processor is a placeable station. The fandom mirror (breezewiki) and gamermovil agree on the craft:

- **Biofuel Processor:** 12 Wood + 12 Scrap (per breezewiki and gamermovil, both checked 2026-09-29).

Place the Processor next to your campfire and feed it. Some guides list the Processor blueprint behind a puzzle (an Old Factory or similar); if your crafting menu does not show it, go find the blueprint first.

## What to Feed It

The Processor turns **food** into fuel. Per the fandom mirror:

- **Any food works** — carrots, corn, steak, even raw meat. Uncooked food works as well as cooked; pelts actually give *more* than one unit each.
- Drop food in, wait a moment, collect Biofuel.

One Biofuel keeps the campfire at **max intensity for ~5 minutes** regardless of weather or current fuel level. That is the "pro strategy" for surviving the harder raids after night 50, when a dead fire means a dead run.

### Where Sources Disagree

A few guides give a *different* recipe — for example "1 Corn + 1 Rotten Meat = Biofuel." We do **not** use that version: it comes from a site on our untrusted list (99nightsintheforest.wiki, which has a history of fabricated mechanics), and it is not corroborated by the fandom mirror or gamermovil. Until a second reliable source confirms a Corn/Rotten-Meat recipe, treat the "feed any food to the Processor" method as the consensus.

## Biofuel vs Logs

| Fuel | Fuel value | Rain-proof? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Log | 1× | No — rain drains it faster | Near-infinite if you grow saplings (see [How to Get Wood Fast](/items/how-to-get-wood-fast)) |
| Biofuel | ~2× | **Yes** | Costs 12 Wood + 12 Scrap for the Processor; then food → fuel |

Early game, logs are fine — you are next to a forest and can grow saplings. Biofuel becomes worth it once raids get serious and weather turns; a rain-proof fire you do not have to babysit is the difference between a clean night 60 and a wipe.

## How It Fits the Base Loop

Biofuel is the back end of the [Best Base Design](/guides/best-base-design) fuel loop: Farm Plots grow food → Processor turns surplus food into Biofuel → campfire stays maxed → deeper map stays open. The 12 Scrap for the Processor comes from the loot structures covered in [How to Get Scrap Fast](/items/how-to-get-scrap); the 12 Wood from your sapling farm in [How to Get Wood Fast](/items/how-to-get-wood-fast).
