A Seed Pack is the game's way of letting you order a plant that is not already on the map. You use it, the crop, food, flower, firefly or potion ingredient appears somewhere on the forest floor after one in-game day, and you walk out and harvest it. Every other food in the game is either already growing or drops from an animal; seed packs are the only thing that asks you to plan a day ahead.

That makes them the quiet backbone of a long run. Berry and Chili packs solve hunger and movement. Flower and Firefly packs feed the Fairy loop and the dark. The other five — Dripleaf, Moonflower, Stareweed, Cavevine and Mandrake — are Fairy-only packets that grow potion ingredients you cannot get any other way. This page is the hub for all nine, and each kind links out to the page that covers its crop.

## Quick Answer

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What it is | An item that plants a crop, food, flower, firefly or potion ingredient on the map |
| How many kinds | **Nine** — 2 food, 2 flower/light, 5 potion ingredients |
| How long to grow | **One in-game day** after you use the packet |
| Where they come from | Grocery Store, the Fairy (for Flowers), Chests; the 5 potion packets come **only** from the Fairy |
| Farm Plot? | **No** — packets plant on the map; Farm Plots are a separate passive system (see [Crafting Bench](/locations/crafting-bench)) |
| Do they regrow? | **No** — once harvested there is nothing left; plant another packet to get more |

## The Nine Seed Packs

| Seed Pack | Grows | Main use | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chili Seeds** | Chili plants (speed boost) | Food / movement | Grocery Store, Fairy, Chests |
| **Berry Seeds** | Berry plants | Renewable food | Grocery Store, Fairy, Chests |
| **Flower Seeds** | Flowers | Fairy purchase currency | Grocery Store, Fairy, Chests |
| **Firefly Seeds** | Fireflies | Light, flashlight charging | Grocery Store, Fairy, Common Chests |
| **Dripleaf Seeds** | Dripleaves | Potion brewing | Fairy (beside ponds) |
| **Moonflower Seeds** | Moonflowers | Potion brewing | Fairy (harvest at night) |
| **Stareweed Seeds** | Stareweeds | Potion brewing | Fairy (Flashlight to harvest) |
| **Cavevine Seeds** | Cavevines | Potion brewing | Fairy (Locked Caves, Mineshafts) |
| **Mandrake Seeds** | Mandrakes | Potion brewing | Fairy (Trick-or-Treat houses) |

Every packet grows its output one in-game day after planting. The food and flower packets plant their output across the map; the potion packets have a placement rule you have to respect (see [How Planting Works](#how-planting-works)).

## Where to Get Seed Packs

The routes are not equal. Some are repeatable every run, one is a one-time structure, and the five potion packets are locked behind the Fairy.

| Route | What it takes | Gives | Repeatable | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Grocery Store** | Mossy Coins | Chili, Berry, Flower, Firefly packs | Yes | Bloxodes, 99nightsintheforest.net, Deltia's |
| **The Fairy** | Trade Flowers for packets | All nine — the only source for the five potion packs | Yes, if you keep picking flowers | Fandom Chili; Bloxodes |
| **Chests** | Open Chests | Common → Flower/Firefly; Good → Berry; Legendary → Berry/Flower/Chili | Unbounded by luck | Our own [Chest Tiers](/items/chest-tiers) |
| **Food Warehouse** | Loot the building | Boxes of Berry, Flower, Firefly, Chili Seeds | One-time per run | Fandom Food Warehouse |
| **Farmer Cletus** | Sells seed boxes for Mossy Coins | Chili pepper and berry seed boxes | Yes | Fandom Locations thread |

The Fairy is the anchor. She sits outside the Mushroom House once your campfire reaches level 2, and she is the only door to the five potion packets. The flower trade is spelled out on our [Pelt Trader](/items/pelt-trader) page: pick about twenty flowers — a circle of eight counts as one group, and the Fairy's Home itself holds around ten — then hand them over. Twenty flowers is roughly a morning's picking, so this is the most reliable route if you reach her early.

The Food Warehouse is the interesting one, and the most fragile claim on this page. The fandom Food Warehouse page is blunt: "The Food Warehouse is the only structure to give Seed Packs." That would make it the single best stop for a bulk seed order, and no other source we found mentions the building at all. Treat it as a strong single-source lead, not a rule.

## How Planting Works

Using a packet is one action, and the wait is fixed.

1. **Use the packet.** It plants its output on the map.
2. **Wait one in-game day.** Bloxodes and our [Chili](/items/chili) page both give the growth time as one in-game day, with no conditions about biome, campfire level or placement.
3. **Harvest.** Walk the map and pick the output.

### Placement rules for the potion packets

Three of the five potion packets only grow in the right part of the map, so check before you use them:

- **Dripleaf Seeds** grow beside ponds.
- **Cavevine Seeds** grow inside Locked Caves and Mineshafts.
- **Mandrake Seeds** grow near Trick-or-Treat houses.

Moonflower and Stareweed can grow across the map, but their harvest has a rule: **Moonflower Bulbs can only be harvested at night**, and you must **shine a Flashlight on Stareweed** before collecting its petals. Pack the light and schedule the trip before the plant is ready.

### A packet is not a guarantee

Note what a "Chili Seed Pack" does not promise. Fandom's older Chili article says one packet places one of four things — Chili, Flowers, Fireflies, Berries. The Breeze wiki lists six, adding Strawberries and Trees. If your packet lands on a Tree, you did not get Chili. Budget at least two packets per crop you actually need, and plant in a known area so you know where to walk on harvest morning.

## Food Seeds

The two food packets are the ones a survival run cannot ignore.

**Chili Seeds** grow Chili — one fork of hunger plus a short movement-speed boost — after one in-game day. Chili does not spawn on the map by itself, so its entire supply runs through this packet; the full route, the Arctic Fox bill it feeds, and the Hard Mode rule that strips Chili of any wild fallback are on our [Chili](/items/chili) page.

**Berry Seeds** grow Berries — one fork of hunger, renewable — after one in-game day. Berries do not grow back on their own in the wild, so the packet is how you keep a berry line alive for the [Kiwi](/entities/the-kiwi) tame and for cooking. Berries are also forageable across the map, which is why a Berry pack is the safer first buy than a Chili pack.

## Flower and Light Seeds

**Flower Seeds** grow Flowers, which are the Fairy's purchase currency — harvested Flowers buy more seed packets, so a Flower pack quietly pays for itself if you keep trading. **Firefly Seeds** grow Fireflies, which give the map extra light and recharge flashlights on contact; on a dark run that is worth more than the hunger value of the food it costs.

Both come from the Grocery Store, the Fairy and Chests (Firefly also drops from Common Chests), so they are the easiest packets to stock without committing to the Fairy loop.

## Potion Ingredient Seeds

These five are Fairy-only packets that grow potion plants. None drop from Chests or the Grocery Store, and none grow in a Farm Plot.

- **Dripleaf Seeds** — Dripleaves beside ponds; a potion ingredient.
- **Moonflower Seeds** — Moonflowers across the map; bulbs harvested at night.
- **Stareweed Seeds** — Stareweeds across the map; shine a Flashlight before harvest.
- **Cavevine Seeds** — Cavevines in Locked Caves and Mineshafts.
- **Mandrake Seeds** — Mandrakes near Trick-or-Treat houses.

Spend Flowers on the output your route can actually use. Buying a cave plant when nobody plans to brew potions is a fine way to grow decorative homework.

## Where Sources Disagree

Six places where the published material contradicts itself or us. We are not resolving these; they are the state of the sources as of 2026-10-05.

1. **Growth time — one day or three to seven?** Bloxodes and our Chili page say one in-game day, flat. The 99-nights-in-the-forest.net fan guide lists "3–7 days" and twelve crop types — Forest Carrot, Glimmerberry, Shadow Wheat, Moonlit Mushroom, Fire Pepper, Ancient Herb — that do not match the game's actual crops (Carrot, Corn, Pumpkin, Chili, Berry, Apple). We treat the twelve-type, 3–7-day list as unreliable and print the one-day figure.
2. **Is there a Carrot Seed?** Our [Pet Food](/taming/pet-food) page lists Carrot among the plot crops grown from seeds. No published seed list we can find — Bloxodes' nine, Pocket Tactics' nine — includes a Carrot Seed. Open.
3. **Farm Plot versus seed pack.** Farm Plots grow Carrot, Corn, Pumpkin and Cake (rblxguide); seed packs plant Chili, Berry, Flower, Firefly and the five potion plants. Whether a plot also grows Chili is contested: our Pet Food page says yes, Sports Rant's crop table, Rblxguide's plot list and the Breeze write-up say no. The seed-pack route is the only one every source agrees on.
4. **Does one packet plant only its crop?** Fandom's older Chili article says a Chili Seed Pack places one of four things; the Breeze wiki says six. So a packet is not a guaranteed crop.
5. **Is the Food Warehouse the only structure giving Seed Packs?** Fandom says yes, bluntly. No other source mentions the building, so this is a single-source lead we will not promote to a rule.
6. **What a Chili is worth.** Every external food table puts Chili at one fork plus a boost; our [Hunger](/guides/hunger) page puts it at none — "speed boost instead." This changes whether a Chili seed is a snack or pure utility, and it is argued out on the Chili page.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long do Seed Packs take to grow?**
One in-game day after you use the packet. No biome, campfire or placement condition changes it.

**Where do I get Seed Packs?**
The Fairy sells all nine for Flowers. Chili, Berry, Flower and Firefly packs also appear through the Grocery Store and Chests. The five potion packets come only from the Fairy.

**Which Seed Packs grow food?**
Berry Seeds grow Berries for hunger, and Chili Seeds grow Chili that gives a temporary speed boost. The other seven grow flowers, fireflies or potion ingredients.

**Do Seed Packs work in a Farm Plot?**
No. A packet plants its output on the map; a Farm Plot is a separate passive system that grows Carrot, Corn, Pumpkin and Cake on its own clock. Use a packet when the crop you want is not on the plot list.

**Where do I plant the potion packets?**
Dripleaf beside ponds, Cavevine in Locked Caves and Mineshafts, Mandrake near Trick-or-Treat houses. Moonflower and Stareweed grow anywhere but have harvest rules (night; Flashlight).

**How do I harvest Moonflowers and Stareweed?**
Moonflower Bulbs can only be harvested at night. Shine a Flashlight on Stareweed before collecting its petals.

**Is there a Carrot Seed?**
Not in any published seed list we can find. Our own Pet Food page disagrees and lists Carrot among the seeded crops, so treat this as open.
