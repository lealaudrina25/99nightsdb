## First, the Naming Trap

You will see **Diamonds** and **Gems** used interchangeably, and it confuses almost everyone. Several guides claim Gems are a separate, weaker currency. **The evidence says they are the same pile under two names.**

Every number we can cross-check lines up exactly once you assume that:

| | Called "Diamonds" | Called "Gems" |
|---|---|---|
| Ranger class | 70 | 70 |
| Assassin class | 500 | 500 |
| Cyborg class | 600 | 600 |
| `afterparty` code pays | 15 | 15 |
| `yay fishing` pays | 2 | 2 |

We could not find a single item priced in one and not the other. The **class screen** tends to say Gems; **code rewards, chests and most guides** say Diamonds. Treat them as one balance.

Two things that follow from this and are worth knowing:

- **Diamonds are saved to your Roblox account, not to the run.** Dying never costs you any.
- **Class unlocks are permanent.** Buy once, use in every future run.

Separately, **Candy** and **Candy Canes** are genuinely different — event-only currencies for seasonal classes.

## The Cultist Stronghold Loop

This is the method that turns a trickle into a farm. Everything else is one-time or luck.

1. **Find it.** One Stronghold spawns per game, marked with a diamond icon on your map. In some runs it stays sealed until your campfire reaches **Level 4** — which is why [How to Get Wood Fast](/items/how-to-get-wood-fast) matters here.
2. **Kit out while it is closed.** It opens on a roughly **20-minute timer**. Use that window for a ranged weapon, ammo, food and fuel. Set a timer so you are at the door when it unlocks.
3. **Fight upward with range.** Clear floor by floor to the top. Shoot from cover rather than walking into archer fire. Kill crossbow cultists first, and kite melee enemies back downstairs to pick them off.
4. **Loot, then reroll the server.** The top floor hides the treasure room with the Ruby and Diamond Chest — **at least 5 Diamonds**, banked instantly.

### The tips that actually change the payout

- **Farm solo.** Enemy count scales with the number of players, but the Diamond Chest pays the same either way.
- **Never let difficulty stack.** Each clear makes the next one harder, so restarting after a clear is faster than beating a Level 3 Stronghold.
- **The Deer cannot follow you inside.** The Stronghold doubles as a panic room at night.
- **Carry a backup weapon.** Reloading in an open room with archers still alive is how most raids end.
- **Get the Good Sack early** — more carry space per trip.

Fifty Diamonds is the entry price for the only food economy that costs nothing per trip, and it pays across every run you ever play. Full breakdown: [**Fisherman Class**](/classes/fisherman-class).

The class you farm with has its own Level 2 and Level 3 to reach. Our [class levels guide](/guides/class-levels) prices every ladder by Diamonds per item of work.

## Picking a Class for Farming

You do not need the expensive class. The cheapest ones do the job:

- **Medic (40)** — two bandages and faster revives. The safest budget pick if you raid with friends.
- **Ranger (70)** — starts with a Revolver and 18 ammo. The cheapest reliable entry into ranged Stronghold farming.
- **Alien (100)** — the Ray Gun never runs out of ammo, though it overheats, so carry a revolver or rifle to cover the cooldown.
- **Cyborg (600)** — the endgame goal: [Alien Armour](/items/how-to-get-alien-armour) plus a Laser Cannon that shreds cultists in a couple of hits. Farm *toward* this one rather than starting with it.

See [Best Class](/classes/best-class) for the full comparison, and [Best Weapons](/weapons/best-weapons) for what to bring through the door.

## Daily Quests

At the spawn lobby there is a large roulette. Pull its handle and it rolls **three tasks for the day**. Completing each one during a run pays **5 Diamonds** — so up to 15 a day, every day, which adds up faster than most people expect.

The exact per-task figure comes from a single source, but the mechanism itself is reported everywhere.

Four of the cheapest badges in the game hang off one upgrade chain: the Firemaking I-IV badges at campfire level 3, 4, 5 and 6. The whole ladder is on [Campfire](/guides/campfire).

Swiftness — Day 99 in under 90 minutes — is the one worth hunting on a [speedrun in 99 Nights in the Forest](/guides/speedrun-guide), and it is a multiplier problem, not a damage problem.

Badges are the largest non-code diamond source in the game. Every one of them, with its payout and its dispute, is catalogued on our [All Badges](/guides/all-badges) page.

Four of them, worth 25 Diamonds between them, with two furniture rewards attached. [**Fishing in 99 Nights in the Forest**](/guides/fishing-guide) lists the requirements and flags the one currently reported unobtainable.

No tutorial tells you what completing a run looks like, so it is worth saying plainly: surviving to the end of Day 99 gets you a cutscene, but the true ending also needs all four children home. Every way a run can end is on [how to win 99 Nights in the Forest](/guides/how-to-win-99-nights).

## Badges and Milestones

**Badges** are one-time each, and there are roughly 35 of them, worth about 100 Diamonds in total. For a new account this is the best return available — work through the easy ones first and let the hard ones wait until you have better gear.

**Milestones** pay every run: **2 Diamonds at Day 50** and **3 more at Day 99**. Build beds and rescue the kids to push the day counter faster — see [Missing Kids Locations](/locations/missing-kids), since each rescue is a permanent +1 multiplier.

**Hard Mode pays more** for the same objectives, but a completed normal run beats an abandoned Hard Mode attempt. Switch when your group can reliably reach Day 99.

## Update Parties

Roughly 30–60 minutes before a Saturday update goes live, the developers sometimes run a party and shower the map with Diamonds. Being there can pay **20–40**.

Check the game's Roblox page for the update schedule and join early — arriving after the update has launched means you missed it. Stand in the open when they fall; big structures can block the drops.

## What Not To Do

- **Do not trust generator sites.** There is no working Diamond generator. Any site asking you to log in or complete a task is farming you.
- **Do not buy Diamonds first.** Robux bundles exist, but grinding beats them on value unless you specifically want to skip ahead.
- **Do not grind Hard Mode you cannot finish.** An abandoned run pays nothing.
- **Do not let Stronghold difficulty stack.** Reroll instead.

## A One-Hour Session

1. **5–10 min:** roll Daily Quests, kit up, check for a new code.
2. **~20 min:** first Stronghold clear.
3. **Exit and re-queue.** Repeat for a second clear.
4. **Last 10–15 min:** knock out an easy badge, claim milestones, redeem codes.

Two smooth clears beat one long messy one. Consistency is the whole trick.
