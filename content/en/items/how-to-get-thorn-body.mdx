## What It Actually Does — Read This Part

Two things, and the second one is usually oversold.

**1. It is the second-toughest armor in the game.** The fandom Armor page is explicit: the Thorn Body "grants the wearer a greater protection than the Iron Body and Alien Armour." So it sits above both the common mid-game armors, and only the Obsidiron Body is documented higher.

**2. It reflects a small portion of melee damage back at whoever hit you.** The same source calls it "a small portion," and it only fires when the wearer is hit with a **melee attack**. Arrows and ranged attacks do not reflect. This is the same mechanic family as the [Poison Armour](/items/how-to-get-poison-armour), which poisons melee attackers instead.

**Do not read "reflect" as a damage build.** A small fraction of one hit is chip damage — it will not kill anything meaningful on its own. The reason to wear the Thorn Body is the **protection number**; the reflection is a bonus that makes swarms slightly cheaper to stand in.

### The rule most players learn too late: armor is permanent

Both the fandom wiki and rblxguide state the same rule: **once a piece of armor is equipped, it cannot be unequipped — not even by dying and reviving — and it can only be swapped for a strictly better piece.**

That has two consequences:

- **Do not equip whatever you find first.** A Leather Body you put on by accident is stuck on you until something better turns up.
- **The Thorn Body is a safe thing to commit to.** Because only the Obsidiron Body outranks it, wearing it does not lock you out of the real end-game armor — you can still upgrade into that. It *does* lock you out of going back down to Iron or Alien, which is fine, because Thorn is better than both.

## Where the Thorn Body Sits Among All Armors

The fandom wiki counts **seven wearable armor variants** (plus the Riot Shield, which is a *held* item rather than worn). Here is the whole ladder, from weakest to strongest:

| Armor | Protection | Special effect | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Leather Body** | Lowest | — | Spawn (Brawler class), the Forest, Good / Iron Chests, Pelt Trader |
| **Vampire Cloak** | ~Leather, below Iron | Blood Meter heals ~25% HP at night (pauses in combat) | Vampire Chests at the end of the Hedge Maze (Carnival event); Vampire class spawns with it |
| [**Poison Armour**](/items/how-to-get-poison-armour) | Side-grade | Poisons anything that melee-attacks you | Frog Chests / Frog Invasion event / Mother Temple Jungle Chest; Poison Master class spawns with it |
| **Iron Body** | High | — | The Forest, Legendary / Gold Chests, [Pelt Trader](/items/how-to-get-iron-body) |
| **[Alien Armour](/items/how-to-get-alien-armour)** | Higher than Iron | Regenerates Alien Tech Energy when hit | Broken UFOs, [Mothership UFOs](/locations/the-alien-mothership), Alien Chests (Alien Invasion); Cyborg class spawns with it |
| **Thorn Body** | **Higher than Iron *and* Alien** | Reflects a small slice of melee damage | **Ruby Chests + Tool Workshop (this page)** |
| **Obsidiron Body** | Highest | Fills a damage bar, then an AoE explosion (~50–60 damage, knockback) | Obsidiron Chests only, during the Meteor event |

Two practical reads on that table:

- **If you are a Cyborg, you spawn with Alien Armour** — which the Thorn Body beats, so it is a genuine upgrade rather than a side-grade. The same applies to a Vampire, whose Vampire Cloak is *worse* than Iron Body.
- **Only the Obsidiron Body is above you.** Everything else is a step down. That is what makes Thorn a comfortable long-term wear.

**One source adds numbers.** The Namu wiki publishes a damage-reduction table (Leather ~22%, Poison ~39%, Iron ~62%, Alien ~69%, Thorn ~81%, Obsidiron ~82%) and lists the same order of strength. **Treat those exact percentages as unverified** — no other source we checked gives them, so use the *ordering* and ignore the precise figures. The same page also notes armor can be upgraded further with an **Armour Trim Kit** (a Meteor-event craft), and mentions a "Corrupted Armor" variant found in Corrupted Ruby Chests — both single-source and unconfirmed.

## How to Get It

Two independent routes, and you only need one.

### Route 1 — Ruby Chests

The Thorn Body is a possible **Ruby Chest** drop. Sources that name a specific farm point to the **Cultist Stronghold**, which is also the most repeatable supply of Ruby Chests in the game — the full loop, and how the Stronghold resets, is on [Cultist Stronghold](/locations/cultist-stronghold).

It is a loot roll, not a guarantee. Expect to open several before one pays out.

### Route 2 — Craft It at the Tool Workshop

The Thorn Body is a Tool Workshop recipe. So is its sibling, the [Riot Shield](/items/how-to-get-riot-shield). Build it at the **Anvil inside the [Tool Workshop](/locations/tool-workshop)** — the large stone-brick building with vines and an anvil icon on the map. The setup work is the same as for the [Chainsaw](/weapons/how-to-get-chainsaw): defeat the cultists guarding the building, collect the scattered anvil pieces from its floors, and place them on the blueprint.

**A disagreement worth knowing about.** Most recipe lists present the workshop as a menu you pick from. Two sources instead describe the anvil as offering its **high-tier items one at a time** — and notably, both name the *same four items* (Chainsaw, Riot Shield, Tactical Shotgun and Thorn Body). It is possible this is simply a garbled version of the widely-repeated rule that the anvil **crafts only one item at a time**, and the menu reading is the majority. If the Thorn Body does not appear as a recipe for you, that is the explanation to test.

## The Recipe

Every source that publishes a Thorn Body recipe publishes the same one:

| Material | Amount | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **Cultist Gem** | 4 | Cultist kills — see [Cultist Stronghold](/locations/cultist-stronghold) |
| **Bear Pelt** | 4 | Bears — see [The Bear](/entities/the-bear) |
| **Alpha Wolf Pelt** | 2 | Alpha Wolves — see [The Alpha Wolf](/entities/the-alpha-wolf) |
| **Scrap** | 10 | Ground-up metal — see [How to Get Scrap](/items/how-to-get-scrap) |

**The Alpha Wolf Pelts are the bottleneck, not the gems.** Four [Cultist Gems](/items/cultist-gem) is a short Stronghold run and ten Scrap is a few minutes at the Grinder, but the two Alpha Wolf Pelts mean hunting [Alpha Wolves](/entities/the-alpha-wolf) specifically — the toughest regular wolf, not the common ones. Do that hunt first; the rest is easy.

Worth noting: the **Chainsaw and the Tactical Shotgun use the exact same 4 Cultist Gem + 4 Bear Pelt + 2 Alpha Wolf Pelt + 10 Scrap recipe.** So if you are already farming those materials for one of them, you are most of the way to the Thorn Body too.

## Is It Worth Building?

**Yes — it is the best armor you can realistically aim for short of the Meteor event.** It is a straight upgrade over the Iron Body most players are wearing, it costs only a Ruby Chest run or one workshop trip, and because nothing below it can replace it, it is a decision you make once. Which tier it belongs to, and what else a Ruby Chest can roll, is on [Chest Tiers](/items/chest-tiers).

**The one time to hesitate:** if you are planning to chase the Obsidiron Body, remember you can only ever move *up*. Wearing the Thorn Body does not block that — Obsidiron is above it — so there is no real downside. The only armor decision that can hurt you is equipping something weak early.

For the rest of the toolkit that pairs with a frontline build, see [Riot Shield](/items/how-to-get-riot-shield), [Best Weapons](/weapons/best-weapons) and [All Classes](/classes/all-classes).

## FAQ

**Is the Thorn Body a weapon?**
No. It is wearable body armor. You cannot attack with it.

**Is it better than the Iron Body?**
Yes — the fandom wiki states it gives greater protection than both the Iron Body and the Alien Armour.

**Does it damage everything that hits me?**
Only **melee** attackers, and only a small portion of the damage. Ranged hits, arrows and fire do not reflect.

**Can I take it off later?**
No. Armor cannot be unequipped once worn, and can only be replaced by a strictly better piece. Since only the Obsidiron Body outranks the Thorn Body, wearing Thorn does not lock you out of anything.

**Do I have to craft it?**
No. It is also a Ruby Chest drop, so a lucky chest can save you the whole 4 / 4 / 2 / 10 recipe.

**Chest or craft — which is faster?**
Craft, if you can already clear the Tool Workshop. The chest route is free but random; the recipe route is guaranteed once you have the pelts.
