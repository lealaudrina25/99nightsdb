Scrap is the one material that quietly gates your whole late game. Wood you can always chop. Diamonds you can grind. But the moment you try to upgrade your Crafting Bench past Tier 2 — or build the Chainsaw — you hit a wall of "needs 10 / 20 / 50 Scrap" and realize you never saved any. This guide shows where scrap reliably comes from, what it actually costs you, and the loop that fills the gap fastest.

## What Scrap Actually Is

Scrap is the game's generic **metal crafting material**. You don't produce it at a bench the way you process wood — you pull it out of the world's structures, and (late game) out of spare gems. Almost every mid-to-late-game recipe asks for at least a little.

The trap: because it isn't renewable like wood, players burn through it on early defenses and then stall at the bench upgrades. Treat scrap as the scarce resource and wood as the abundant one.

## The Fastest Way to Get Scrap: Loot Structures

The consensus across location guides is clear — **structures are the #1 source of scrap**, not enemies and not chests. The reliable, repeatable ones:

- **Sheds & Garages** — the most dependable scrap-and-gasoline spots. Small sheds often cough up 1 scrap/fuel item per door; garages add better tools and the occasional Revolver.
- **Barns** — bigger hauls of both wood and scrap metal, a real mid-game spike.
- **Ruined Houses / Cabins** — high-tier loot; some scrap sits alongside the top weapons.
- **Metal chairs** in medic/clinic buildings — reported to break down into scrap (see the disagreement note below on the exact amount).

The practical rule: as your map expands, every *new* structure is a fresh scrap pull. Keep pushing the exploration radius instead of re-clearing old ones.

## The Recycler: Turn Spare Gems Into Scrap

This is your late-game safety valve, and it's well documented across multiple guides. The **Recycler** unlocks at **Crafting Bench Tier 5** and costs **40 Wood + 40 Scrap + 1 Forest Gem** to build. Once placed, feed it unwanted gems:

- **Gem of the Forest → Cultist Gem**
- **Cultist Gem → Scrap**

So a surplus Forest Gem on a 99+ night run becomes a steady scrap trickle. You generally won't build the Recycler *for* scrap early (it costs 40 scrap to make) — it pays off once you're gem-rich but scrap-poor. The Forest Gems for it come from [Cultist Stronghold](/locations/cultist-stronghold) raids and chests.

## What Scrap Is Used For

Two uses are rock-solid because multiple named sources agree on the numbers:

**1. Every Crafting Bench tier upgrade costs scrap** (verified upgrade ladder):

| Upgrade | Scrap | Other cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bench 1 → 2 | 1 | 5 Wood |
| Bench 2 → 3 | 10 | 15 Wood |
| Bench 3 → 4 | 20 | 30 Wood + 2 Cultist Gems |
| Bench 4 → 5 | 50 | 50 Wood + 1 Forest Gem |

**2. The Chainsaw needs 10 Scrap** — see [How to Get the Chainsaw](/weapons/how-to-get-chainsaw).

Beyond those two, scrap feeds a long tail of defenses, tools, and base structures (bear traps, reinforced walls, the tier-5 machines themselves all draw on it). Exact per-item amounts for those aren't officially published, and guides disagree on the figures, so treat any specific number you see elsewhere as unverified rather than gospel.

## The Fastest Farming Loop

1. **Upgrade your campfire first** so the map keeps opening — new structures = new scrap.
2. **Run a shed/garage circuit** near your base each day; don't skip the small sheds, they're free scrap.
3. **Hit a barn whenever one is on the route** — best wood-and-scrap ratio.
4. **Bank it.** Resist spending scrap on early spike traps; the bench ladder needs 81 scrap total (1+10+20+50) and the Chainsaw needs 10 more.
5. **Late game, lean on the Recycler** to convert spare Forest Gems once your bench is at Tier 5.

## Where Sources Disagree

- **Metal chair scrap value.** Some guides claim a metal chair breaks down into 5 scrap; others just list "scrap" with no number. The amount is unconfirmed — verify in-game before you plan a chair-only route.
- **Scrap respawn.** No official respawn timer is published. Treat each structure as a one-time pull and keep exploring outward rather than camping one spot.
- **Class bonuses.** A few guides claim the Scavenger class boosts scrap drops from chests. This isn't officially confirmed, so don't build your route around it.

## FAQ

**Wood or scrap — which should I prioritize?**
Wood is effectively infinite (chop trees); scrap is the bottleneck. Hoard scrap, spend wood freely. See [How to Get Wood Fast](/items/how-to-get-wood-fast).

**Can I buy scrap anywhere?**
There's no shop that sells it. You loot it from structures or convert it via the Recycler. Anyone claiming a "scrap code" is wrong — codes don't grant materials.

**I'm stuck at Bench Tier 4. What do I need?**
20 Scrap + 30 Wood + 2 Cultist Gems. The gems come from [Cultist Stronghold](/locations/cultist-stronghold) raids.

**Does the Recycler need scrap to build — isn't that circular?**
Yes, it costs 40 scrap. It's a *late-game* converter for when you have spare gems but not enough scrap, not an early-game source.
