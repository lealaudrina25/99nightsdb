The Gem of the Forest is the last material in the game. It is not food, it is not a weapon, and nothing at an NPC will trade it to you. It exists for one purpose: it is what tier 5 of the Crafting Bench asks for, and every machine that comes out of tier 5 asks for it again.

What makes it a page of its own is not how rare it is. It is that **no source collects its routes in one place**. The fandom's Materials page mentions it in one sentence. Recipe round-ups list it in a cost column. The Stronghold guide mentions a fragment as an aside. If you want to know which of the eight places a fragment can actually come from, which of them still works on night 40, or how many you need before you can afford one of everything, you have to assemble it yourself — which is what this page does.

Last verified: **2026-10-05**.

|---|---|

| How you merge it | Drag the four fragments into each other. **No bench, no Grinder, no crafting station** |
| What it feeds | **Crafting Bench tier 5** and the tier-5 machines. Nothing else |

| Bench 4 → 5 cost | **50 Wood + 50 Scrap + 1 Gem of the Forest** |

## Four Names, One Item

Before the routes, settle the naming, because the naming is what makes guides contradict each other. There is one item with four published names.

| Name | Who uses it | On this site |
|---|---|---|
| **Gem of the Forest** | The fandom Materials page, rblxguide, Bloxinformer, SimpleGameGuide | Yes — most pages |
| **Forest Gem** | The Miraheze items page, Deltias Gaming | Yes — two pages only ([Scrap](/items/how-to-get-scrap), [Blacksmith](/classes/blacksmith-class)) |
| **High-Tech Component** | Three third-party crafting round-ups, describing the tier-5 gem | Referenced in passing on [Weather and Night Events](/guides/weather-and-night-events) |
| **Cultist Gem** | The fandom Cultist Gem page | **Not used for this item** — see the Cultist Gem page |

Our reading: it is one item, and the fandom Materials page is the one that states it plainly — wood, scrap, Cultist Gems and Gems of the Forest are the game's four material types, listed side by side, and the Forest Gem is defined as what tier 5 of the crafting table costs. **Forest Gem and Gem of the Forest are the same object.** High-Tech Component is a schema name from recipe aggregators that map the tier-5 bench onto their own material labels — the same thing our own Weather page caught when it noticed that three sources call the Gem of the Forest a High-Tech Component. And the Cultist Gem is simply the wrong item, which is the confusion that costs runs.

One practical consequence of the naming: if you read a guide that says tier 5 takes a **Cultist Gem**, it is describing the gate one rung down. Tier 4 takes 2 Cultist Gems and no Forest Gem. Tier 5 takes the Forest Gem and no Cultist Gem. The two rungs are named after different materials, and sources that mix the names are usually off by one rung.

## What the Merge Actually Does

The merge is the cheapest step in the whole route and the one guides describe most loosely.

Four **Gem of the Forest Fragments** dragged onto each other become one **Gem of the Forest**. Deltias Gaming's version adds the useful detail that they pop over each other in your inventory and merge on their own — **you do not need to put them on the bench or grind anything**. VGtimes describes the same action as throwing the four pieces into one spot. Either way, no station is involved, which matters because it means **merging early costs you nothing but inventory space**, and leaving four loose fragments in a pack is strictly worse than a merged gem.

The gem itself has **no hunger value and no combat value**. It is a pure crafting material; you cannot eat it, throw it, or sell it. That is worth stating because the item's shape — a cracked green sphere — reads like a resource you are meant to use directly.

What it does not do is unlock tier 4. That is the Cultist Gem wall, and it is [Cultist Gem](/items/cultist-gem)'s job to argue about.

The [Pumpkin](/items/pumpkin) page is the reference for this one, including why the old advice to look for pumpkins under trees stopped working after the Halloween wild-spawn nerf.

## Where the Fragments Come From

Eight documented routes. This is the table no source publishes whole, and the "repeatable" column is the one that decides your route.

| Route | Pays | Repeatable? | The gate on it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Jungle temples** — each of the four | **2 fragments guaranteed** per temple | No — **one-shot per run** | 1 Crystal Skull Key per temple; see [Jungle Biome](/locations/jungle-biome) |
| **Volcano altar** | 1 fragment on **every 8th charge** | **Yes** — the cycle repeats | Campfire **Level 4+** to enter; see [Volcanic Biome](/locations/volcanic-biome) |
| **Bone Pond** fishing | Fragment from Campfire Level 6 water | **Yes** — the water does not dry up | **Campfire Level 6**; see [Fishing](/guides/fishing-guide) |
| **Cultist Stronghold**, full clear | A fragment alongside 2 Cultist Gems, a Ruby Chest and a Diamond Chest | **Yes** | Bench tier 4; see [Cultist Stronghold](/locations/cultist-stronghold) |
| **Ice Temple** — Frozen Beam | The beam is documented as holding **both** gems | No | Snow Biome on the map; see [Ice Temple](/locations/ice-temple) |
| **Gold Chest** | Fragment in the loot table | No | Chest tier; see [Chest Tiers](/items/chest-tiers) |
| **Ruby Chest** | Fragment in the loot table | No | Chest tier; see [Chest Tiers](/items/chest-tiers) |
| **Nighttime raid, last cultist** | A fragment from the last kill — **but only on raids past night 99** | **Yes** | The last-kill rule; see [The Cultist](/entities/the-cultist) |

Three of those are worth pulling apart, because the counting is where runs fail.

**The Jungle is the biggest single payout and it is a one-shot.** Four temples at two fragments guaranteed is **8 fragments — half a gem — from one loop**, and the loop also gives you 12 Cultist Gems across the same four visits. The catch is that the temples are one-shot per run and the loop is gated behind four keys, which means the Jungle has to be on your map and has to be scheduled. Our [Keys and Locked Doors](/guides/keys-and-locked-doors) guide is the scheduling problem, and the campfire levels that gate the keys are on [Campfire Level Gates](/guides/campfire-level-gates).

**The volcano is the repeatable one and its first payout is a fragment.** Every 8th charge pays — but the cycle's other stops are not fragments: charge 16 gives 3 Cultist Gems and charge 24 gives the Infernal Sword or Crossbow. Bring **Sacrifice Totems** rather than farming twenty-four cultist bodies, because a totem is 2 charges. The full cycle, and why the sword is the third payout rather than the first, is on [Volcanic Biome](/locations/volcanic-biome).

**The last-kill route is the one people plan around and should not.** A fragment from the last cultist of a raid is not a fragment you can farm by killing cultists; it is a fragment you get if you happen to be holding the belt at the right moment, three nights into a run past 100. Worse, it is the same last-kill rule that makes the Stronghold a lottery — see disagreement 4 below.

The pond route deserves one line: the Bone Pond at **Campfire Level 6** is the only water that yields both the fragment and the Old Car Engine, and it is the cheapest route on this table if your campfire is already Level 6. This is why the fishing route shows up as a **campfire** requirement rather than a day one, and it is the hinge for the next section.

## The Day-Gate Fight

Four sources give four different answers to "when do fragments start dropping", and three of them are about the same route.

| Answer | Who says it |
|---|---|
| **Day 90** | Deltias Gaming: "Fragments only drop from Cultists that spawn after Day 90" |
| **Past night 99 / Day 100+** | Our own [Cultist](/entities/the-cultist) page, rblxguide, the Miraheze items page, Quest Codes, 99-nights.wiki |
| **No day at all — a fully upgraded campfire** | VGtimes, which says fragments "can be discovered deep in the forest when the campfire is fully upgraded" |
| **No odds exist anywhere** | Quest Codes declines to publish a drop rate, and calls every YouTube or Reddit farm estimate a tactic signal rather than a number |

**Our reading: they are describing two different routes, and only one of them has a day gate on it.** The 90 / 99 / 100 spread all attaches to the **raid** route, and the spread is real — a guide saying "after day 90" and a guide saying "past night 99" are both describing cultist raids, just at different levels of precision, and the honest answer is that the exact night is unsettled. The **campfire** answer belongs to the **pond** route, which is gated on Campfire Level 6 and never mentions nights at all.

That split has a practical use: if a source says fragments need **Day 100+**, it means the raid, and you should not plan your pond route around day 100. If a source says **fully upgraded campfire**, it means the pond, and you should not wait for a night number that does not exist. And if you want a fragment on night 40, none of the three day-based answers apply to you — go to the Jungle, the volcano, the pond or a full Stronghold clear, all of which work before night 90.

What no source gives you is a rate. Every route on this page is either guaranteed (Jungle, volcano cycle stops, full clear) or chance-based (chests, pond, raids). Plan around the guaranteed ones.

## What the Gem Buys: The Spend Plan

Five sinks, and the ordering matters more than the count, because the first gem you merge decides which machines exist for the rest of the run.

| Spend | Gems | Rest of the recipe | What it solves |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Crafting Bench tier 5** — the upgrade itself | **1** | 50 Wood + 50 Scrap | Unlocks every row below |
| [Respawn Capsule](/guides/death-and-respawn) | 1 | 40 Wood + 40 Scrap | A death with no teammate to carry you |
| [Weather Machine](/guides/weather-and-night-events) | 1 | 40 Wood + 40 Scrap | Rain and thunder, for 3 days once charged |
| [Temporal Accelerometer](/guides/speedrun-guide) | 1 | 40 Wood + 40 Scrap | One skipped day/night cycle |
| **Recycler** | 1 | 40 Wood + 40 Scrap | A gem-to-scrap and gem-to-gem conversion bench |

**The bench first, always.** Our [Crafting Bench](/locations/crafting-bench) page states it as the one rule that cannot be argued with: do not craft toward tier 5 until the gem is already in your pack. Rushing tier 4 to reach 5 does not skip the gem, it just spends 50 Scrap before you have to find one anyway.

After that, spend against what is actually ending your runs:

- **Runs that die to the dark** — the Respawn Capsule. It is the only non-Robux way back from a death, and the arithmetic of it is argued on [death and respawn](/guides/death-and-respawn).
- **Runs that drown in weather** — the Weather Machine. Its recipe name fight, and the fact that three sources call the same gem a High-Tech Component, are on the [weather](/guides/weather-and-night-events) page.
- **Runs where the day counter is the enemy** — the Temporal Accelerometer. Our own speedrun page says build it last and probably not in a speedrun, because it sits one tier above the Giant Bed you already need and competes for the same gem.
- **Runs with a gem surplus and a scrap shortage** — the Recycler. Build it last for exactly that reason: it costs a gem, so it is not a gem generator.

**The Blacksmith does not help here.** This is the most common mistaken reading of the class. The Blacksmith skips the most expensive bench rung for free, and every page that describes it agrees that rung is **3 → 4** — the one carrying 2 Cultist Gems. The 4 → 5 rung carries a Gem of the Forest and the class does not touch it. **The Blacksmith is a Cultist Gem class, not a Gem of the Forest class.**

## The Arithmetic Nobody Publishes

Five gems is our addition, and it is worth doing because the published total is wrong in the same way the Cultist Gem total is wrong.

**Our arithmetic, from the table above:**

- The tier-5 upgrade: **1** gem
- Four tier-5 machines: **4** gems
- **One of everything: 5 gems.**
- At four fragments each, **one complete tier 5 costs 20 fragments.**

**The published total disagrees, and it is off by exactly the upgrade.** Two guides print the same figure — that crafting everything at least once takes **6 Cultist Gems and 4 Gems of the Forest**. The 6 is our bench number for the Cultist Gem side (tier 4's upgrade plus four tier-4 structures). The 4 is the same four machines, with the tier-5 upgrade itself not counted, because that upgrade is what the gem *buys* rather than what the gem sits *in*. So the calculator figure is a machines-only number wearing a whole-tier label.

If you are budgeting: 4 fragments per gem, 5 gems for one of everything, and the first gem's only job is to make the other four buildable.

## Where Sources Disagree

Five live contradictions. Two of them change your route and one of them changes your total.

**1. When do fragments drop?** Day 90, night 99, Day 100+, or a fully upgraded campfire — four answers, analysed in the section above, with our reading that they describe two different routes.

**2. Is a Gem of the Forest Fragment a Gem of the Forest?** No, and it is not a 25% discount either. Four fragments make one gem; one fragment is a quarter of one. The fandom's own Materials page treats the two as distinct rows, and our [Jungle Biome](/locations/jungle-biome) FAQ answers it the same way for the temple's 3-gem / 2-fragment payout. The failure mode is real: a Jungle loop returns **8 fragments, not 4 gems**, and a run that reads those as gems is four fragments short of a bench upgrade.

**3. Does the tier-5 machine take a Gem of the Forest or a Cultist Gem?** The fandom contradicts itself between two of its own pages — the Cultist Gem page says you spend Cultist Gems to activate the Respawn Capsule, Temporal Accelerometer and Weather Machine, while its Materials page and four recipe round-ups say 1 Gem of the Forest each. Our own [speedrun](/guides/speedrun-guide) page raises the same fight from the other direction: our bench page says 1 Gem of the Forest, fandom trivia says 1 Cultist Gem. We follow the majority — merged Gem of the Forest, one per machine.

**4. What does the Ice Temple beam actually hold?** Our own [Ice Temple](/locations/ice-temple) page documents the Frozen Beam as holding **Gem of the Forest and Cultist Gems**, and it is the one place on this list that could be a repeatable-ish Forest Gem route. But the same page's comparison section argues the temple is steadier than the Stronghold *for Cultist Gems*, and its own FAQ hedges that the last-kill rule may or may not police a container. **We cannot resolve it**, and it matters: if the beam only ever yields Cultist Gems, the Ice Temple is not a Forest Gem route at all and the table above should lose a row.

**5. How many tier-5 machines are there — three or four?** Most round-ups list three: Weather Machine, Temporal Accelerometer, Respawn Capsule. Fragtop lists four, adding the Recycler. Our [Scrap](/items/how-to-get-scrap) page puts the Recycler at Tier 5 costing 40 Wood, 40 Scrap and 1 Forest Gem, which would make **five** gems for one of everything. This single disagreement moves your fragment budget by one gem — 16 or 20 fragments — so pick your number deliberately and do not let a guide's three do it for you.

## FAQ

**Is a Gem of the Forest Fragment the same as a Gem of the Forest?**
No. Four fragments merge into one gem. The Jungle temple returns 2 fragments guaranteed; the volcano pays one fragment per 8-charge cycle stop; neither gives you a gem.

**Can I reach tier 5 with Cultist Gems?**
On one fandom page, yes. Everywhere else — and on our own [bench](/locations/crafting-bench) page — no. Tier 5 takes a merged Gem of the Forest.

**Do I need a Gem of the Forest before tier 4?**
No. Tier 4 is the 2-Cultist-Gem wall. This gem enters at the rung above it, which is why the [Blacksmith](/classes/blacksmith-class) class skips something you do not need yet.

**What is the fastest first gem?**
It depends on your map, not on your skill. If the Jungle is on it, the four temples give 8 fragments in one scheduled loop and nothing beats that. If it is not, the volcano is the only repeatable route that does not require a biome you may not have.

**Can I farm fragments before night 90?**
Yes — from the Jungle, the volcano, a full Stronghold clear, the Bone Pond at Campfire 6, or chests. Only the raid route waits for late nights.

**Does the Recycler help me get more gems?**
No. It runs Gem of the Forest **to** Cultist Gem and Cultist Gem to scrap. It is the escape hatch for a merged gem you needed Cultist Gems instead — see the [Cultist Gem](/items/cultist-gem) page.

**Do fragments have a drop rate I can plan around?**
No source we could find publishes a stable one. Plan around the guaranteed routes: Jungle temples, volcano cycle stops, and full Stronghold clears.
