The Frog King is the headline boss of the **Frog Invasion** update (22 August 2025) and one of the few fights in 99 Nights in the Forest that is locked behind a **gate you have to farm**. He is not a single enemy you stumble into — he is the last of a **four-wave frog gauntlet**, and the reason to run it is the Frog Chest, which holds the [Poison Spear](/weapons/how-to-get-poison-spear) and the [Poison Armour](/items/how-to-get-poison-armour).

Last verified: **2026-10-02**.

## How to Reach Him

The whole boss is gated behind **eight keys**, and there is no shortcut:

**1. Collect 8 Frog Keys.** Each key sits in a different **pond**, shown as a **glowing key marker in the water**. You fish it out — so bring a rod (the Fishing Cabin hands you the basic one). Not every pond has a key, so you are canvassing the map, one pond at a time. One key per pond.

**2. Wait for the portal pond.** After roughly **three to four in-game days**, a **large pond appears near your campfire** with a **whirlpool** at its centre. It is guarded by ordinary frogs plus one **Purple Frog** — clear them before you dive.

**3. Jump into the whirlpool.** It pulls you down into the **Frog Cave**.

**4. Use all 8 keys at once.** Inside the cave is a huge door with **eight keyholes**. Place all eight or the mechanism will not trigger — if you are one short, you have wasted the trip.

**A minority note on the keys.** The standard route is fishing the key-marked ponds. Several sources also say frogs can **drop** Frog Keys on death, so killing them is a secondary source — see [Frogs](/entities/the-frog). Treat the pond route as the reliable one and treat frog drops as a bonus.

## The Fight

The boss room is a **wave-based gauntlet**, several guides compare its structure to the Alien [**Mothership**](/locations/the-alien-mothership) event: clear each wave, and the next spawns. Four waves, weakest to strongest.

| Wave | Enemies | What to know |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Green Frogs** | Small, low HP, minor damage. The only tier that does **not** split — it just drops a morsel. Use this wave to save ammo |
| 2 | **Blue Frogs** | Tougher, more damage. On death each splits into **two Green Frogs** |
| 3 | **Purple Frogs** | The largest regular frogs: most HP, heavy damage. On death each splits into **two Blue Frogs** — which then split again |
| 4 | **Frog King** | The boss. Biggest health pool, strongest attacks, and a **poison attack** that damages you over time |

**The mechanic that kills people is the splitting.** Any frog above Green, when killed, spawns **two frogs of the next weaker type** — so a Purple Frog becomes two Blues becomes four Greens if you let it. Swinging into a crowd lets it multiply out of control. The fix, per RBLXGuide: **focus-fire one chain at a time** and funnel the splits into a choke point rather than fighting in the open.

**On the King himself:** he fights with poison, and he is not a puzzle — he is a damage race you win by **staying out of range**. One guide (Backyard Drunkard) adds that he **summons extra frogs if the fight drags on**, so slow fights get punished; treat that as a single-source detail rather than a guaranteed mechanic. Sources also name him the **Red Frog King** or the **Orange Frog**, so if you see those labels, they are the same boss.

### Why you will not find his HP here

**No source publishes the Frog King's health or damage numbers.** Every guide we checked describes him qualitatively — "very high health," "the strongest attacks in the game" — and stops there. Rather than invent a figure, we are telling you there is not one to quote.

The only concrete number in the frog family is the **common frog**: RBLXGuide puts it at about **70 HP and 5 damage per tongue lash**. That is the baseline Green Frog, not the boss — do not scale it up and assume you have his stat line.

## Tactics

The tactics below are named by multiple independent guides, which is why they are the ones to trust.

- **Use ranged, not melee.** Ray Gun, Crossbow or Bow, with far more ammo than feels reasonable — you will spend a lot of it on the waves before the King even appears. See [Best Weapons](/weapons/best-weapons) for what to bring.
- **Keep distance and keep moving.** Do not stand still and trade hits. Circle and kite; the poison sprays are what punish a stationary fight.
- **Heal before you need to.** Every guide says the same thing: the poison chips you constantly, so top up early rather than waiting for low health. Carry cooked meat, bandages and medkits.
- **Wear your best armour.** Armour dampens both the physical hits and the poison ticks — and this is exactly the fight where the [Iron Body](/items/how-to-get-iron-body) or [Thorn Body](/items/how-to-get-thorn-body) earns its slot.
- **Spend cheap on waves 1–3.** Save your best weapon and your healing for the King. Clearing the early frogs with basic attacks keeps your good ammo for him.
- **The Alien class is a popular pick.** One guide (Noleep) recommends Alien with a Ray Gun — ranged pressure with no ammo management. See [All Classes](/classes/all-classes).
- **Retreat and reset if it goes wrong.** Every guide says the same: if you are low with no heals, leave the cave and restock rather than dying. Better a reset than a lost run.
- **Bring friends if you can.** Group play lets one player kite while the others deal damage — Backyard Drunkard frames it as a role-based fight (DPS, tank, healer) for a reason.

## What He Drops

Beating the King opens the **Frog Chest**, and the drops are consistently reported across every guide we checked:

| Item | Drop rate |
|---|---|
| [Poison Spear](/weapons/how-to-get-poison-spear) | **3%** |
| [Poison Armour](/items/how-to-get-poison-armour) | 1% |
| Blowdart | 1% |
| Frog Boots | 1% |

Two things follow from that table. First, the rates are **low** — the Poison Spear at 3% is roughly a 1-in-33 clear, so this is a farm loop, not a one-and-done. Second, the chest also gives **a large amount of meat**, which is why some players run it as a food farm even after they have the gear.

**After a win, the cave ponds open up.** You can fish them using the **same loot pool at the same rates** as the chest, so a chest roll that misses a piece can be filled in by fishing before you leave.

**What you will not get:** one site claims the Frog King drops a **"Beastmaster Badge"**, and the same site invents a whole set of frog crafting recipes (frog armour "from 10 Frog Skins + 5 Poison Glands," and so on). **No other source matches any of that**, and no frog-themed armour or spear in this game has a recipe — see the crafting note on [How to Get the Poison Spear](/weapons/how-to-get-poison-spear). Treat the badge and the recipes as fabricated.

## FAQ

**How many Frog Keys do I need?**
Eight, one per pond, and all eight must be placed at once to open the door.

**Where are the Frog Keys?**
In ponds that show a glowing key marker in the water. Fish the marker to pull the key. Not every pond has one.

**How do I get into the Frog Cave?**
Wait for the large portal pond near your campfire (roughly three to four in-game days in), clear its frogs, and jump into the whirlpool in the middle.

**Can I fight him more than once?**
Yes. There is no one-time lock — it is a repeatable farm loop for the low-rate chest drops.

**What is the best way to beat him?**
At range. Bring your strongest ranged weapon, keep moving, heal early, and wear armour so the poison does not stack up on you.

**Does he drop the Poison Spear?**
Yes — 3% from the Frog Chest, the rarest item in it.

**Is the fight soloable?**
It is possible with strong range and good kiting, but every guide treats it as easier with a group, where one player can kite the King while the others deal damage.
