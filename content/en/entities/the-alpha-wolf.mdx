The **Alpha Wolf** is what a [Wolf](/entities/the-wolf) turns into once the run gets serious — bigger, tankier, hits harder, and from Day 15 onward it starts showing up and leading packs. It is a genuine threat at night, but it also drops the **Alpha Wolf Pelt** that trades for a Rifle, and it is a strong combat pet if you can tame it. This page covers its stats, where it appears, what it pays out, and how to befriend it.

Last verified: **2026-09-30**.

## The Alpha Wolf at a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Hostile, killable, tameable |
| **HP** | ~115–125 (sources range 90–150 — see below) |
| **Damage** | ~25 per hit (sources range 22–35) |
| **Walk speed** | ~26 |
| **Tameable** | Yes — a **Good Taming Flute**, 4 cycles |
| **Active** | Night; Snow biome, Forest with Level 3–5 campfire, Locked Caves, Cultist Hunter Camp |
| **Drops** | Morsel (2–3), Steak (1), Alpha Wolf Pelt (0–1), Blue Key (guardian) |

**On the stats:** guides disagree hard on the exact HP and damage. The two most specific numbers are 115 HP (dedicated entity wiki) and 125 HP (pockettactics); other guides list 90, 150, or a 100–150 range. Damage is reported between 22 and 35. Treat the precise figures as **run- and patch-dependent** and read the on-screen values in your own game. What every source agrees on: it is much tougher and harder-hitting than a normal Wolf, and it is fast.

Four of them guard the second rescue, which is the second +1 in the [speedrun in 99 Nights in the Forest](/guides/speedrun-guide) ladder.

## How It Behaves

- **It appears later.** Most guides put the first Alpha Wolves around **Day 15+**, or any time once your campfire is Level 3–5. They spawn at night and vanish at dawn.
- **It leads packs.** Alpha Wolves often show up with normal Wolves and act as the pack leader, so a single encounter can spiral.
- **It teleports home.** Like every animal, if you lure it too far from its spawn it vanishes and returns to post. Kill it where it lives.
- **It is repelled by a high campfire.** A lit campfire at Level 5+ keeps them out of its radius — your safe zone (see [How to Get Wood Fast](/items/how-to-get-wood-fast)).

- **The better Steak farm.** [Chef and Cook](/classes/chef-class) — the Alpha drops the same Morsel and Steak as the Wolf, and both feed the station.

What it drops feeds you as well as your pets — Steak at one fork raw, two cooked. See [**Hunger in 99 Nights in the Forest**](/guides/hunger).

## What It Drops — and Why You Hunt It

| Drop | Amount | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| **Morsel** | 2–3 | Food (cook it) |
| **Steak** | 1 | Food (cook it) |
| **Alpha Wolf Pelt** | 0–1 | Trade at the **Pelt Trader** for a Rifle or Strong Flashlight (see [Best Weapons](/weapons/best-weapons)) |
| **Blue Key** | 0–1 | From **guardian** Alpha Wolves — gates deeper Locked Caves |

The pelt is the real prize: it is one of the few things that buys you a Rifle from the [Pelt Trader](/items/pelt-trader). The **Blue Key** comes from a guardian Alpha Wolf in a Locked Cave and opens later cave progression — see [Missing Kids Locations](/locations/missing-kids).

## How to Fight It

- **Ranged is safest.** A [Rifle](/weapons/how-to-get-rifle) or [Revolver](/weapons/how-to-get-revolver) keeps you out of its bite; a Wolf pet can body-block it.
- **Strafe and circle.** Like a normal Wolf it pathing-locks on rocks and trees — kite it around geometry and take free hits.
- **Don't fight a pack.** One Alpha Wolf is manageable; a pack is not. Pull it away from the others before you engage.
- **[Bear Traps](/items/bear-trap) work.** They chunk it hard; stack a few if you are farming pelts and keys.

The reported fish variant of this requirement — Mackerel and Salmon in place of Stew and Corn — is why carrying a rod pays off before a long tame. [**Our fishing guide**](/guides/fishing-guide) covers where each comes from.

## How to Tame It

The Alpha Wolf is the first **Good Flute** pet — a real step up from the [Wolf](/entities/the-wolf) and a strong combat companion once tamed.

- **Tool:** a **[Good Taming Flute](/items/taming-flute)** (Tier 2). The Old Flute will not work.
- **Cycles:** **4** angry faces above its head.
- **Food:** the most-cited version is **1 Steak + 1 Stew + 1 Corn per cycle** (×4 = Steak ×4, Stew ×4, Corn ×4). Several other guides report a **fish variant: 1 Steak + 1 Mackerel + 1 Salmon per cycle** (×4 = Steak ×4, Mackerel ×4, Salmon ×4).
- **Rule:** the Alpha Wolf must be **undamaged** to start the mini-game, and you must not attack it beforehand.

The flute tiers and the full per-animal food table are on [Taming Guide](/taming/taming-guide). The two food sets above are the only ones reported — read the speech bubble in your own run to see which one you got.

## What NOT to Do

- **Don't assume Wolf food works.** The Alpha Wolf wants a Good Flute and a different (bigger) food stack than a normal Wolf.
- **Don't engage a whole pack.** Pick one off and separate it.
- **Don't try to tame a guardian mid-fight.** It must be undamaged, and guardians are usually surrounded.
