The **Bear** is the heavy hitter of 99 Nights in the Forest — the killable entity with real weight behind it. It drops the food and pelts you need, it guards the keys that open deeper caves, and — unlike [The Wolf](/entities/the-wolf) — it is a late-game taming project. This page covers its stats, where it shows up, what it pays out, and the two ways to deal with it: shoot it or befriend it.

Last verified: **2026-09-30**.

## The Bear at a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Hostile, killable, tameable |
| **HP** | ~350 |
| **Damage** | ~40 per hit |
| **Walk speed** | ~28 |
| **Tameable** | Yes — a Taming Flute (most guides say Strong or higher; tier varies by run) |
| **Active** | Night; deeper Forest, Snow biome, Cultist Hunter Camp, Locked Caves |
| **Drops** | Morsel (6), Steak (3–4), Bear Pelt (0–1), Yellow Key (1, guardians), Grey Key (1, guardians) |

The HP and damage figures above are consistent across rblxguide and the fandom mirror (breezewiki), both checked 2026-09-29.

Bears guard two of the four caves, so they are on the critical path of any [speedrun in 99 Nights in the Forest](/guides/speedrun-guide).

## How It Behaves

A Bear is slower than [The Deer](/entities/the-deer) but hits far harder, and it has a long detection radius — once it locks on, it chases for a long time and will not simply give up.

- **Where it spawns:** deeper **Forest** regions (away from the starting camp), the **Snow** biome at night, the **Cultist Hunter Camp**, and inside **Locked Caves** (as a guardian).
- **It teleports home.** If you lure it too far from where it spawned, it vanishes in a puff of smoke and returns to its post. You cannot permanently drag a Bear away — kill it where it lives.
- **It gets stuck on geometry.** Like [The Wolf](/entities/the-wolf), a Bear pathing-locks on rocks, trees and building corners. Use that to kite it safely.

- **Ribs and Steak from one animal.** [Chef and Cook](/classes/chef-class) — the Chef's BBQ Ribs and Steak Dinner both start on this drop table.

Among the drops is Steak, worth one fork raw and two cooked, and the raw version costs health. Fork values are on the [**hunger**](/guides/hunger) page.

The guardian bears are also the lock: they carry the Yellow and Grey keys, and our [Keys and Locked Doors](/guides/keys-and-locked-doors) guide counts what all four coloured keys cost in kills.

The Bear Pelt is the rung that closes the trader's chain and pays out Iron Body, while the Wolf Pelt in the middle of that chain is what the bandage recipe eats; our [Healing, Bandages and Medkits](/guides/healing-guide) guide draws the line.

Where the Wolf pays one, the Bear pays 3 to 4, which is enough that taming one costs about as much meat as another Bear would have dropped, and that is the arithmetic our [Steak](/items/steak) page runs.

## What It Drops — and Why You Hunt It

| Drop | Amount | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| **Morsel** | 6 | Food (cook it); also Bear taming food |
| **Steak** | 3–4 | Food (cook it); core Bear taming cost |
| **Bear Pelt** | 0–1 | Trade at the **[Pelt Trader](/items/pelt-trader)** — his fourth rung, where a Bear Pelt buys a **Strong Axe, [Iron Body](/items/how-to-get-iron-body) or Strong Flashlight** |
| **Yellow Key** | 0–1 | From **guardian** Bears in Locked Caves — gates deeper cave progression |
| **Grey Key** | 0–1 | From **guardian** Bears in Locked Caves — gates deeper cave progression |

The pelts fuel your gear, but the **keys are the real prize**: a guardian Bear in a Locked Cave drops one Yellow and one Grey Key, and those are what open the later missing-kids caves. See [Missing Kids Locations](/locations/missing-kids) for the rescue chain.

The gate above is only half of it. The other half is the win condition itself — all four rescues completed before the run resolves. Both halves, with the campfire levels they depend on, are on [how to win 99 Nights in the Forest](/guides/how-to-win-99-nights).

The Bear is a fight; the Deer it guards against is not, and our [Bosses](/guides/boss-guide) guide draws that line.

## How to Fight It

- **Ranged is strongly preferred.** A Rifle or [Laser Cannon](/weapons/how-to-get-laser-cannon) keeps you out of its 40-damage reach. Revolver / Ray Gun are too weak for a 350-HP target.
- **Bear Traps insta-kill a Bear in 4 hits.** Place 4 traps in a 2×2 square, aggro the Bear, and lead it through. This is the best farm method for pelts and keys. The step order, the reuse rule, and how to run the same kill with a team are all on our [Bear Trap](/items/bear-trap) page.
- **Circle terrain.** Kite it around rocks and trees; it locks on geometry and you get free hits.
- **Melee is possible but risky.** Strafe sideways out of its swing arc; never stand in front of it. Only attempt melee with good gear and healing.
- **Don't bring pets unless you have spare Alpha Wolves.** A Bear will switch targets to your pet and can delete it.

## How to Tame It

The Bear is a late-game pet — tougher and more durable than [The Wolf](/entities/the-wolf), and a genuine damage-soaker once tamed (same ~350 HP and ~40 damage on your side).

- **Tool:** a Taming Flute. Most guides point to a **Strong Flute or higher**; the exact tier shifts between runs, so check your own — the [Taming Flute](/items/taming-flute) page explains why.
- **Cycles:** ~5 angry faces above its head (varies by run).
- **Food:** **varies by run.** The most commonly reported set is **Steak ×5 + Pumpkin ×5 + Salmon ×5** (rblxguide); sportskeeda lists a **Steak + Stew + Pumpkin** variant. Read the speech bubble in your own run — the Bear's requirement is one that shifts between runs, like the Wolf's.
- **Rule:** the Bear must be **undamaged** to start the mini-game. A Bear that has already taken a hit cannot be tamed.

Full mechanics, flute tiers and the per-animal food table are on [Taming Guide](/taming/taming-guide). For whether the Bear is worth the food versus other animals, see [Best Pet](/taming/best-pet).

## What NOT to Do

- **Don't melee a Bear with a weak weapon.** 40 damage per hit ends a run fast.
- **Don't lure it far from spawn.** It teleports back; you waste the chase.
- **Don't try to tame a guardian Bear mid-fight.** It must be undamaged, and guardians are usually surrounded.
- **Don't eat raw Morsel or raw Steak.** Same rule as every animal — cook first.
