The Fairy is the one trader in 99 Nights in the Forest who decides whether your camp eats or starves. She is the only source of the five potion seed packets, the reason a berry farm is renewable at all, and the only NPC in the game gated behind your **campfire** rather than the clock — she does not appear near camp until the fire reaches **level 2**.

She is also the most confusingly named thing in the forest, and that is the real reason this page exists. **"Fairy" is two different features wearing one word.** There is the permanent NPC at the Mushroom House, and there is the rotating Fairy Mini-Biome added in the 2026 "Botany & Magic" update. Players search one name and land on the other, and at least one wiki blurs them further by placing the trader inside the biome's cottage. This page keeps the two apart, then covers the NPC in full.

What makes her worth a page is that almost every article on this site already leans on her — [Seed Packs](/items/seed-packs), [Chili](/items/chili), [Berry](/items/berry), [Campfire](/guides/campfire) and the [Pelt Trader](/items/pelt-trader) all route through her — and none of them ever says who she is. This is that page.

## The Two Fairies: One Name, Two Features

This is the split that trips up most searches, so it comes first.

**The NPC** is a single friendly character with a permanent home. She is a specialized vendor whose entire shop is farming: you hand over Flowers, she hands back seed packets, you plant them and your camp stops depending on luck. She is not tied to any event, and she never leaves the game — once unlocked, she is on the map for the rest of the run.

**The Fairy Mini-Biome** is a region, not a person. It is a rare, magical sub-region introduced in the early 2026 "Botany & Magic" update, built around a Mother Tree world event and a separate Box O' Seeds shop. It is temporary content: per one source it was rotated out and replaced by the Bee Mini-Biome from 25 July to 1 August 2026, then rotated back in. Our own [patch notes](/guides/updates-patch-notes) log the same update and date it **20 January 2026**, while the guide that tracks the rotation puts it at **10 January 2026** — treat the exact day as unsettled, the month as not.

The two are easy to mix because the biome's own mushroom cottage looks like the NPC's Mushroom House. One wiki's mini-biome page goes as far as writing that "The Fairy" — the merchant — resides in the biome's central cottage. That is the collapse this page is here to undo: the trader you trade with is not the biome, and the biome can be gone from the map while the trader is still sitting outside her house.

## Where the Fairy Is and When

She is a landmark, not a random spawn. Every source agrees on the shape of the answer:

- **Her Mushroom House is permanent and fixed.** One guide places it **northeast or southwest of your camp**; the other calls it a permanent, fixed structure that becomes visible once the map's fog clears and is then marked for good. Either way you are not hunting a wandering NPC — you are walking to a building.
- **The campfire is the gate.** Our [Campfire Level Gates](/guides/campfire-level-gates) table files her under **level 2**, and the [Pelt Trader](/items/pelt-trader) page says the same: she "stays for the whole run once you have reached level 2 campfire." One guide softens this to "level 1-2" to clear the fog — see the disagreements below.
- **She is a daytime NPC.** The clearest behavioural line in the sources: she is only around during the day and vanishes at night. Trade before dusk.
- **You cannot hit her.** Swings near her are harmless — she takes no damage. That makes her one of the few NPCs you can stand next to during a fight without fearing a stray melee hit.

That campfire level is worth reading in context, because it is the same rung that opens the [Frog Cave](/locations/frog-cave), the first chest tiers and a dozen other things. The Fairy is not a special gate; she is one entry on a ladder you were already climbing.

## What She Sells, and What It Costs

Her shop is farming, and everything in it is a seed packet. The logic is a loop rather than a list:

1. **Buy seed packets** from the Fairy with Flowers.
2. **Plant them** in Farm Plots — a plot costs **10 wood + 5 scrap** at the crafting bench, covered on our [Crafting Bench](/locations/crafting-bench) page.
3. **Harvest** a renewable crop every run instead of foraging once.

The interesting part is what she sells and what it costs, because the sources do not agree. Our own [Seed Packs](/items/seed-packs) page says she sells **all nine** packets and is the **only** source for the five potion kinds — Dripleaf, Moonflower, Stareweed, Cavevine and Mandrake. One external guide narrows her stock to farming only, listing **Berry Seeds (5-7 Flowers)** and **Flower Seeds (3-5 Flowers)**. The [Pelt Trader](/items/pelt-trader) page, meanwhile, describes her wares as "Seed Boxes (chillies, strawberries, trees)".

What everyone agrees on is the **shape of the payment**: you spend Flowers, and "twenty flowers" is the number our pages use for a single trade — picked in a loop or clump, where a circle of eight counts as one group and the Fairy's House itself holds around ten. Whether a given packet costs five Flowers or twenty is the open question, and it matters if you are budgeting the Flower economy below.

If you only need one sentence: **she is the potion-packet shop.** Berry and Chili packs have other sources — the Grocery Store, Chests, the Food Warehouse — but the five potion packets do not.

## The Flower Economy

The Fairy is the reason Flowers are worth picking at all, and the currency has two rules worth internalising.

- **Flowers are free to collect.** They sit in colorful patches on the forest floor, and you pick them by simply walking over them — no tool, no cost. That makes the Fairy route the cheapest repeatable seed route in the game, because the input is a thing you were walking past anyway.
- **They pool for the whole team.** Per two sources, Flowers go into a shared, team-wide "cloud" inventory that every player can spend from. In multiplayer this is a big deal: one player can pick Flowers while another trades, and nobody has to hold the currency personally.

There is a catch that makes the loop self-reinforcing. **Wild Flowers do not regrow.** Once you have stripped the map's patches, the supply is gone for that run — which is exactly why you plant a **Flower plot** of your own. A Flower seed packet grows the Flowers you spend on the next packet, so a single Flower plot quietly pays for itself and keeps the Fairy trade running for the rest of the game. This is the same renewable-food logic our [Berry](/items/berry) page and [Seed Packs](/items/seed-packs) page describe for Berries, applied to the currency itself.

## The Fairy Mini-Biome

The other "Fairy" is a biome, and it is worth understanding separately because it funds a badge.

The centerpiece is the **Mother Tree**, a world event rather than a shop. Per the biome's only detailed source, you interact with vines on the forest floor, rotating and aligning them into a pipeline from the nearby hot springs to the tree's roots, while mobs spawn to interrupt you. Finishing it awards the **Botany** badge — our [All Badges](/guides/all-badges) page lists it as "Revive the Mother Tree in the fairy biome" for 4 Diamonds — and, on that same single source, unlocks Tier-2 Planters and a permanent bloom buff to nearby crops.

The biome also carries exclusive materials the rest of the game does not have — enchanted wood, glow-shrooms, golden petals and similar — and a Box O' Seeds shop distinct from the NPC. Treat the whole resource list as **single-source**: it appears on one fan site and nowhere else we can find, and much of that page reads as embellishment. What is corroborated is the broad shape — a temporary Fairy-themed sub-region tied to a Mother Tree event and the Botany badge — not every line item.

Because it is rotating content, its availability moves. If you open the map and there is no Fairy biome, that is not a bug and it does not affect the NPC; check the current rotation before planning a run around the badge.

## The Gardening Badge and the Diamonds

Buying a seed box from the Fairy for the first time is itself a badge. Our [All Badges](/guides/all-badges) page lists **Gardening** as "Buy a box of Seeds from the Fairy," and notes she appears near the mushroom house. It is one of the cheapest badges on the board — a single purchase, no fight, no run — and it is the entry rung of the whole farming chain.

The **Diamonds** it pays are where the sources part. Our badge table gives **2**; the guide that covers the trade most closely gives **10**, and frames those Diamonds as the seed money for unlocking stronger classes at the bulletin board. Two against ten is a five-fold gap on a badge almost every player will earn, so it is worth checking in-game rather than trusting either number blindly. What is not in dispute is that the badge is trivial to get and worth taking early.

## Where Sources Disagree

The Fairy is unusually well-covered for a friendly NPC, and the coverage disagrees on specifics rather than on the big picture. The open questions:

- **The campfire gate.** Level 2 (our [Campfire Level Gates](/guides/campfire-level-gates) and [Pelt Trader](/items/pelt-trader) pages, plus the fandom overview) versus "level 1-2" on one guide. We lead with **level 2**.
- **The price of a packet.** "Around twenty Flowers" (our [Seed Packs](/items/seed-packs) page) versus **5-7 Flowers** for Berry and **3-5** for Flower on one external guide. Not reconciled by any source.
- **How many kinds she sells.** All nine, with the five potion packets exclusive to her (our Seed Packs page) versus farming seeds only (one external guide) versus "chillies, strawberries, trees" (our Pelt Trader page's trader table).
- **The Gardening Diamonds.** 2 (our badge table) versus 10 (one guide).
- **The biome's launch date.** 20 January 2026 (our patch notes) versus 10 January 2026 (one guide) versus "early 2026" (the biome page). We date the month, not the day.
- **Where she stands.** Outside the Mushroom House near camp (our pages, one guide) versus inside the biome's central cottage (the biome page). We treat the camp-side Mushroom House as the NPC's home and the biome cottage as a different structure.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Where do I find the Fairy?**
At her Mushroom House, a permanent landmark that appears once your map's fog clears. One guide places it northeast or southwest of your camp. She is not a wandering NPC.

**Why is she not there?**
Two reasons, in order: your campfire has not reached **level 2**, or it is **night**. She only stands around during the day.

**Can I kill the Fairy?**
No. She takes no damage, and swings near her are harmless. She is a vendor, not an enemy.

**What does she sell?**
Seed packets, paid for with Flowers. Our [Seed Packs](/items/seed-packs) page says she sells all nine and is the only source for the five potion packets; one guide lists only farming seeds. Berry, Chili and Flower packs have other sources — the potion packs do not.

**How much does a packet cost?**
Either around **twenty Flowers** or **5-7** depending on the source. Pick Flowers by walking over their floor patches; they pool team-wide.

**Do Flowers grow back?**
Wild Flowers do not regrow. Plant a Flower plot with a Flower seed packet to keep your trade currency flowing — the packet pays for the next one.

**Is the Fairy the same as the Fairy Biome?**
No. The Fairy is a permanent NPC; the Fairy Mini-Biome is a rotating region tied to the Mother Tree and the Botany badge. The biome can be off the map while the NPC is still outside her house.

**What badge does she give?**
**Gardening**, for buying a seed box for the first time. Our table says 2 Diamonds; one guide says 10.
