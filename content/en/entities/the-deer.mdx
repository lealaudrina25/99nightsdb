The Deer is the main antagonist of 99 Nights in the Forest and the single most important thing to understand before your first night. It cannot be killed, it drops nothing, and every weapon in the game does nothing to it. This page covers when it appears, how the chase works, and the escapes that reliably end one.

Last verified: 2026-09-29. Grandma's Favourite Games patches this game regularly, so numbers below are accurate as of that date and may shift.

## The One Thing To Understand First

**The Deer cannot be damaged by anything.** Not axes, not guns, not traps. Its health reads as invulnerable because no weapon in the game registers a single point of damage against it.

That changes the entire shape of the encounter. There is no "fight" version of this page. Every strategy below is about distance, light, and geometry — never damage.

## When and Where It Spawns

- It is a **night** entity. It despawns as morning approaches.
- It spawns in the **Forest** biome. Other biomes swap it out: the **Snow** biome uses The Owl, and the **Volcanic** biome uses The Ram.
- It is effectively **harmless on day one** and stays dormant for the first few nights before becoming an active threat.
- **If the campfire goes out, it spawns immediately.** This is the single most common way a camp dies.

You will usually hear it before you see it — rustling leaves are the tell that it has spawned near you.

## How the Chase Works

The Deer is faster than a walking player and, critically, **it accelerates while it keeps chasing you**. A straight sprint across open ground loses. Sustained pursuit is how most deaths happen, not surprise attacks.

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Damage per hit | Around 25 early on, climbing sharply as the day count rises |
| Move speed | Faster than a walking player; speeds up during a chase |
| Killable | No |
| Drops | Nothing |
| Active | Night only |

Two behaviours are worth memorising:

- **It will not enter the campfire's safe zone** unless the fire dies out. Reaching the fire while being chased stuns it briefly and it runs off.
- **It has poor pathfinding.** Trees, walls, shelves and structures block it far more often than players expect.

## Escapes That Actually Work

Ranked by reliability.

**1. Get back to the campfire.** The campfire is the intended refuge and the most reliable escape in the game. Once you cross into the lit area, the pursuit ends.

**2. Use a flashlight, aimed and brief.** A flashlight beam stuns The Deer for a few seconds — long enough to break line of sight and reach safety. Two caveats: the stun duration **decreases with repeated use**, and you should only switch it on while actually aiming, to save battery. Treat it as a tool for buying seconds, not as a weapon.

**3. Break its path.** Lure it around trees, buildings or shelves. Its pathfinding is weak, and a single large object between you and it often ends the chase outright.

**4. Take high ground.** Watchtowers, treehouses and even shelves put you somewhere it cannot reach. On stairs, stand in the middle section rather than at the floor level.

**5. Use a Teleporter if you have one.** It is the only guaranteed instant return to the campfire.

## Places It Cannot Reach

Its size and pathfinding create a short list of reliable hiding spots:

- **Watchtowers and towers** — stay away from edges, where other threats can spawn.
- **Treehouses and the barn's second floor** — watch the window.
- **Shelves and raised structures** — it cannot climb to reach you.
- **Behind doors it is too large to fit through** — but always plan a second exit before entering any building.

None of these are permanent solutions. The game can spawn wolves to force you to move, so treat every hiding spot as a pause, not a win.

## The "Hungry" State

In several updates the game has announced variations of **"The Deer is hungry tonight"**, describing a faster and more dangerous state with **red eyes**, during which it runs on all fours.

Be aware that this mechanic has been patched in and out across different versions — in some patches the enraged state is replaced by glitched appearances of The Owl or The Ram instead. The practical advice holds either way: on an announced hungry night, keep a charged flashlight and stay close to camp.

## What Not To Do

- **Do not fight it.** Nothing works, and standing still while it closes distance is how players die.
- **Do not zigzag in the open.** It lengthens your route rather than breaking the chase.
- **Do not wander far at night.** Distance from camp is the variable that kills.
- **Do not let the fire drop low.** A fire about to go out is a Deer about to spawn.
- **Do not spam the flashlight.** Each use stuns for less than the last.
