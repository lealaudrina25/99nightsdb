The Cultist King is the only boss in 99 Nights in the Forest you have to summon on purpose. He does not wander the map and he does not turn up at random — you pay four Cultist Gems for the fight, and the fight is worth having because he is the only source of the Infernal gear and the Cultist King Antler.

Last verified: **2026-09-30**.

## Quick Answer

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Where | Cultist King Base, **Volcanic Biome** |
| Cost to summon | **4 Cultist Gems** (one per altar) |
| How | Place a gem on each of the four altars; he spawns on the fourth |
| Weakness | Ranged weapons, ladders, lava |
| Worth it? | Yes — Infernal gear and the Antler come from nowhere else |

## How to Summon Him

1. **Collect 4 Cultist Gems.** (Where to get them is below — this is the slow part.)
2. **Find the Cultist King Base in the Volcanic Biome.** It is a large castle-like structure, described variously as church-like and as having a red-and-black colour scheme. It is guarded by ordinary cultists, so clear them first.
3. **Go inside and find the four altars.** Sources describe the room slightly differently — four altars in front of a tinted glass panel, or four pedestals beside stairs leading up to a painting of the Cultist King. Either way there are four of them and they are obvious once you are in the room.
4. **Place one Cultist Gem on each altar.** He spawns the moment the fourth gem goes down (one guide describes the altars igniting just before he appears).
5. **Be ready before you place the last one.** He starts attacking immediately. Top up health, load healing items, and decide where you are going to stand.

**A minority report.** One guide (Gamezebo) says that after placing the gems you return to your camp and he shows up later, fighting alongside the cultists during a raid. Five other sources describe the fight as happening **right there in the base**, immediately. We go with the majority, but if he does not appear for you, waiting for the next raid is the thing to try before you assume it failed.

## Where to Get Cultist Gems

This is the real grind. Two routes are consistently named:

- **Kill cultists.** They drop gems, and the **last cultist of a raid** is the reliable dropper. Clearing a [Cultist Stronghold](/locations/cultist-stronghold) is the densest source of cultists in one place, and defending your camp during raids gives you more chances.
- **Sacrifice items to the Volcano.** Several guides call this the most reliable method, and it is the one the wiki names for farming summon gems specifically.

Two more are reported by a single source each and are worth trying if the above runs dry: **Gold Chests**, and **fishing**. Treat those as unconfirmed.

## The Fight

**What he does**

- He is much larger than a normal cultist and armed with a big golden mace.
- His damage is **close-range** — most guides say melee only, though one describes heavy area-of-effect fire attacks as well. Either way, distance is the answer.
- He has the highest health of any regularly spawned boss. One estimate puts him at **roughly 1,000 HP, about double a Hellephant** — treat that number as an estimate, not a stat sheet.

**What actually works**

The tactics below are named by multiple independent guides, which is why they are the ones to trust:

- **Shoot him, do not swing at him.** Rifle, Tactical Shotgun or a crossbow. Melee against him is how you die. See [How to Get the Rifle](/weapons/how-to-get-rifle) and [How to Get the Tactical Shotgun](/weapons/how-to-get-shotgun).
- **Fight from the doorway.** After he spawns, run back outside and shoot through the entrance. He cannot reach you and he keeps walking into your line of fire.
- **Use a ladder.** His pathfinding is simple and **he cannot climb**. Get on a ladder or elevated structure and shoot down at him.
- **Lava.** Lure him into lava and let it do the work, then finish him. This is risky but it is the cheapest kill.
- **Get him stuck.** He walks straight at you and ignores obstacles, so trees and structures will trap him.
- **Bring friends.** Every guide says the same thing: this is a group fight pretending to be optional. A [tamed pet](/taming/best-pet) helps too.
- **Do it in daylight.** The Ram spawns at night in the same biome, and fighting both at once is a bad idea.

**One genuine disagreement: is he fast?** The wiki says he can catch a sprinting player; Sportskeeda says sprinting outruns him. Sources contradict each other directly, so **do not plan a strategy that depends on outrunning him.** Use terrain or height instead — those work either way.

## What He Drops

| Drop | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Cultist King Antler** | The reason to fight him. Crafting material for the Infernal Helmet |
| **Infernal Sword** *or* **Infernal Crossbow** | One or the other per kill, not both |
| **Infernal Sack** | 20 slots, and it cooks raw food automatically |
| **Mossy Coins** | Currency for the Furniture Trader |
| **Wildfire** | Area-damage throwable |

The Infernal Helmet is not a direct drop — you **craft** it, and the Antler is the ingredient you cannot get anywhere else. One guide gives the recipe as **6 Scorpion Shells + 1 Cultist King Antler at the Tool Workshop**; another says the **Anvil**. We cannot resolve which station is correct, so check both before you burn the Antler.

### The Mace: the big rumour

**Do not count on getting his mace.** This is the single most-mangled fact about this boss, so here is exactly where sources stand:

- **Says it drops:** most "all weapons" listicles list the Cultist King Mace as a boss drop, and a couple call it guaranteed or the sole way to get it.
- **Says it does not:** the fandom wiki has a dedicated page stating it is an **admin-only item that cannot be obtained in normal play**, and says a tester confirmed this. Sportskeeda addresses the rumour head-on and states the mace only appears during Admin Abuse. The Infernal Helmet guide at Rosenberry Rooms says the same in explicit terms.

Note that several of the sites claiming it drops are the same generic weapon-list pages that copy each other, and one of them (Rosenberry Rooms) contradicts itself on its own two pages. **Our read: assume it is not obtainable.** If it does drop for you, that is upside — but do not farm this boss for the mace.

## FAQ

**Where exactly is the Cultist King Base?**
Inside the Volcanic Biome. Its position within the biome varies per run, so upgrade your campfire for full map visibility before hunting for it. Note that the Volcanic Biome is not guaranteed — one source gives it roughly a 50% chance per run, with the [Snow Biome](/locations/snow-biome) taking the slot instead.

**How many Cultist Gems do I need?**
Exactly four, one per altar.

**Can he spawn without summoning?**
No. Every source agrees he never appears randomly.

**How often can I summon him?**
Sources differ: one says once per in-game day, another says there is no cooldown and you can re-summon as fast as you can supply gems. Either way, **gems are the limit**, not a timer.

**Is he worth it solo?**
Possible with strong ranged weapons and good kiting, but this is designed as a group fight. Going in alone with melee gear is a waste of four gems.
