Cultists are the enemy you will actually fight most in 99 Nights in the Forest. Not the Deer, not a boss — these are the human followers who guard every valuable building on the map and who walk into your camp on a schedule.

That is the normal mode. Turn Hard Mode on and the same raids start pulling Juggernauts in from 35% Corruption, with Brute Cultists and Darkstrings added on top from 75%, which is what [Hard Mode](/guides/hard-mode) tabulates.

They matter for two reasons beyond survival. **The last Cultist killed in a raid drops a [Cultist Gem](/items/cultist-gem)**, the material that gates the [Cultist King](/entities/the-cultist-king) and high-tier crafting. And their corpses are a resource in their own right. This page covers every type, how raids escalate, and what you get out of them.

Last verified: **2026-10-02**. For the Stronghold itself and its difficulty ladder, see [Cultist Stronghold](/locations/cultist-stronghold).

## The Three Types You Will Actually Meet

**Melee Cultist — the common one.** Brown clothes, a dark jacket, bald, wearing a **deer skull** on its head. It carries an **Old Axe**, a **Spear** or, later, a **Morningstar**. It walks at speed **12** and dies fast up close. Individually weak; dangerous in numbers.

**Crossbow Cultist — kill this one first.** Identical to the melee variant except for a **bright crimson jacket**. It carries a **Crossbow**, stays at range, hits for **15**, and moves faster than the melee variant (speed **16** against **12**). That speed gap is the whole problem: you cannot simply walk away from it, and it chips you down while you are busy with the crowd. **Every raid guide says the same thing — clear crossbows first.**

**Juggernaut — the rare one.** Roughly **twice the size** of a normal cultist, in a red-trimmed shirt and a **metal helmet with antlers** and glowing yellow eyes. It wields a **Morningstar** and is far tankier and harder-hitting than the other two. It normally appears only in a **Level 3 or 4 Cultist Stronghold** or in a **Hard Mode night raid**. It was added on **4 July 2025** alongside the Stronghold itself.

## Weapons and Damage

A Cultist's damage depends entirely on what it is holding, which is why the same enemy feels trivial early and lethal later:

| Weapon | Damage | Who carries it |
|---|---|---|
| **Old Axe** | **7** | Early melee cultists |
| **Spear** | **15** | Mid-tier melee cultists |
| **Crossbow** | **15** | Crossbow Cultist (ranged) |
| **Morningstar** | **30** | Late melee cultists and Juggernauts |

Note that **30 is also the damage of the Morningstar you can pick up** — if a raid is hitting you that hard, they are carrying a weapon you would very much like to have.

## How Much Health They Have

Health scales with **where** you meet them, not with a fixed value:

| Where | Health |
|---|---|
| Open world and normal night raids | **100–150** |
| Stronghold level 1 | **120** |
| Stronghold level 2 | **180** |
| Stronghold level 3 | **240** |
| Stronghold level 4 | **300** |

That ladder is the reason a level 4 Stronghold feels like a different game. See [Cultist Stronghold](/locations/cultist-stronghold) for the full breakdown.

## Night Raids

**The first raid is scripted for Day 4.** The day before, the info bar flashes red: *"Something evil is approaching your campfire… it arrives tonight."* With about 20 seconds left before nightfall it changes to *"You will be attacked tonight, return to your campfire."*

Then drums, then a cutscene. One detail worth knowing: **The Deer shows up for the first raid and only watches** — it stands near the crafting bench and does not attack you. The first raid is melee cultists with Old Axes and nothing else.

**How raids escalate.** After the first, raids come roughly **every 4 days** (about 20 minutes of play) and get worse in three ways at once:

1. **Crossbow Cultists start appearing.**
2. **Melee weapons upgrade** — Old Axe → Spear → Morningstar.
3. **Numbers grow, up to a maximum of 8** cultists.

**The failure state matters.** If any cultists are still alive at morning, your **campfire depletes faster** in proportion to how many survived, and you get the warning *"Some Cultists are still alive near your fire."* Finishing the raid before dawn is not optional.

**One odd mechanic:** if you heal The Deer during a raid, it will sometimes turn on the cultists and attack them.

## What They Drop

| Drop | When |
|---|---|
| **Cultist Gem** | Dropped by the **last cultist killed** in a raid — this is the one that matters |
| **Gem of the Forest fragment** | Also from the last cultist, but only on raids **past night 99** |
| **Mossy Coins** | 0–1; currency for the Furniture Trader |
| **Revolver / Rifle Ammo** | 0–1 |
| **Morningstar** | The weapon itself, from a cultist carrying one |

**The "last one killed" rule is the important part**, and it is why raid order matters: if you are farming Gems, you cannot simply kill them as you find them — the Gem comes from whichever cultist dies last, so you control it by leaving one alive and finishing it deliberately.

Drops are per raid, not per cultist. A room full of eight cultists pays one Gem.

## Where Else You Meet Them

Beyond raids, cultists guard most of the map's valuable structures: the **Cultist Stronghold** (marked with three blue diamonds), the **Cultist Base** (also called the Military Base — four melee and two crossbow cultists), the **Cultist Laboratory**, **Cultist Church**, **Cultist Hunter Camp**, **Cultist Ritual Circle**, **Cultist Tree** and the **Dilapidated Building**. There are frozen ones in the **Ice Temple**, and the [Volcanic Biome](/locations/volcanic-biome) adds them to nearly every structure alongside the [Cultist King](/entities/the-cultist-king).

## Variants in Other Biomes

The surface roster is not the whole family. Deeper content adds its own cultists:

| Variant | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Shadow Cultist** | Caverns | ~120 HP, low damage, jagged knife, comes in groups of 2–4 |
| **Brute Cultist** ([full page](/entities/the-brute-cultist)) | Caverns, Final Cave | **400–450 HP**, 40 damage, battleaxe. About 10–12 spawn at once, but its **slow attack cooldown** makes melee viable |
| **Darkstring Cultist** | Caverns (ranged) | Fires **two shots** per volley |
| **Jungle Cultist** | Jungle Temples, Mother Temple | ~120 HP, **Poison Spear (30)** or **Blowpipe (45)**, with poison armour that punishes meleeing it |

The Cavern variants answer to [The Bat](/entities/the-bat) rather than The Deer. Shadow and Brute arrived with The Bat on 22 November 2025 — the [Brute Cultist](/entities/the-brute-cultist) page splits those figures out by source; the Jungle Cultist came with Jungle Biome Part 1 on 28 February 2026. The [Caverns](/locations/the-caverns) page covers the cave layout these three live in, the crystal-lighting mechanic you need to get through it, and the statue blessings they guard. The temple route the Jungle Cultist guards — the four skull keys, the collapse that follows taking one, and the Mother Temple they stand on — is walked through on [Jungle Biome](/locations/jungle-biome).

## The Ones You Cannot Fight

**Mega Cultist.** A giant, red-skinned version roughly the size of a large tree, dealing **over 100 damage** per hit. It is **admin-spawned only**, during Update Parties — you cannot meet it in normal play and there is no strategy for it. Treat any "how to beat the Mega Cultist" content with suspicion.

**Trick or Treat Cultist.** A Halloween-event reskin found at the Trick or Treat House, passive while inside. Just a cosmetic variant.

**Resurrected Cultists.** The [Necromancer class](/classes/necromancer-class) can raise dead cultists with the Cultist Staff (three cultist souls each). Worth knowing: a resurrected **Crossbow** Cultist attacks in melee — it cannot fire.

## Corpses Are a Resource

Do not leave the bodies lying around:

- **Campfire fuel.** Cultist corpses can be thrown into the campfire.
- **Volcano sacrifice charges.** At the [Volcanic Biome](/locations/volcanic-biome) altar, a **normal cultist body is worth 1 charge**, and a **large cultist** (Brute or Juggernaut) is worth **2**. One source puts the [Cultist King](/entities/the-cultist-king)'s corpse at **5** — flagged as a single source below. Since payouts land every 8 charges, knowing which corpses are worth double changes how many you need to haul.
- **Stronghold cultists do not leave usable corpses** for this purpose, so farm raid and open-world cultists for the altar.

## How to Fight Them

- **Kill crossbows first.** They are the only variant that punishes you for standing still, and you cannot outrun them.
- **Fight melee cultists in the open**, where you can back up. They walk slower than you.
- **Do not let a raid run to morning** — the campfire drain is the real penalty.
- **Bring ranged for Juggernauts.** They hit for Morningstar damage and take far longer to drop.
- **Control which one dies last** if you are farming Cultist Gems.

## Where Sources Disagree

**Health.** The fandom wiki and our own Stronghold research give the 100–150 open-world range and the 120/180/240/300 Stronghold ladder. One mob list gives a flat **100 HP for every type including the Juggernaut**, which contradicts the Stronghold ladder and the Juggernaut's whole purpose. We use the ladder.

**Juggernaut damage.** Sources agree it carries a Morningstar and "much more damage", but only the weapon's own **30** figure is published. Treat 30 as the floor rather than a confirmed Juggernaut-specific number.

**Raid frequency.** "Every 4 days" and "about every 20 minutes" are the same claim measured two ways — one source states both. We present them together.

**The Cultist King's sacrifice value.** The 5-charge figure comes from a single source and is not repeated on our [Volcanic Biome](/locations/volcanic-biome) page, which lists 1 for normal and 2 for large cultists. Flagged, not adopted as fact.

## FAQ

**When is the first cultist raid?**
Day 4. You get a red warning the day before, and a second one about 20 seconds before nightfall.

**How often do raids happen after that?**
Roughly every 4 days, or about 20 minutes of play. They escalate to a maximum of 8 cultists.

**Who drops the Cultist Gem?**
**The last cultist killed in the raid.** Not a random one — finish the final cultist deliberately if you are farming Gems.

**What is the crimson jacket for?**
It marks the **Crossbow Cultist** — the ranged variant. Kill it first.

**How much damage do cultists do?**
7 with an Old Axe, 15 with a Spear or Crossbow, and 30 with a Morningstar.

**Can you stop raids from happening?**
No. They are on a schedule from Day 4 onward.

**What happens if you do not finish a raid?**
Your campfire drains faster in proportion to how many cultists survived the night.

**Are cultists tameable or recruitable?**
Not in normal play. The Necromancer class can resurrect their corpses, but that is not the same thing.
