Most nights you deal with [The Deer](/entities/the-deer) by reaching your fire, and you deal with [The Owl](/entities/the-owl) by stopping dead. [The Ram](/locations/volcanic-biome) is the Volcanic equivalent, and it is the one of the three that cannot be answered by standing still or by running in a straight line — because The Ram has a charge, and charging enemies do not turn.

This page is about The Ram as an entity: how it behaves second by second, which inputs actually interrupt it, why its facing direction is a trap to read, and where the published numbers contradict each other. The biome's lava rules, the volcano altar loop and the chest tables are on [Volcanic Biome](/locations/volcanic-biome) and are not repeated here. The statue that carries its name in the [Caverns](/locations/the-caverns) is also not this — see that page for the blessing table.

Some Chests hand out seeds; the complete seed list is on our [Seed Packs](/items/seed-packs) page.
The Ram is a hit you have to survive; [Kiwi](/entities/the-kiwi) is the animal you simply walk up to, because it is immune to everything including lava.

The three outer biomes each carry one secondary monster that stands in for The Deer, and which one you get is decided before the sun goes down.

| Biome active | The Deer's replacement | How you answer it |
|---|---|---|
| Snow | [The Owl](/entities/the-owl) | **Stand still** and let the freeze meter drop |
| Volcanic | **The Ram** | **Read the wind-up and sidestep** |
| Jungle | [**The Cat**](/entities/the-cat) | Watch the ground, leave the circle early — and its counter is "never stop moving" |

Every one of these is unkillable, and none of them drops loot. What differs is the counter, and the Ram's counter is the only one that is a **movement problem rather than a stillness problem** — which is why players who are calm around the Owl still lose a running to it.

Do not read that row as "the Volcanic Biome has no killable animal". The [Hellephant](/entities/the-hellephant) is its apex animal, is a completely separate creature, and is worth killing — it drops 8–9 Cooked Steak and can be tamed. Only the Ram in this table is the one you walk away from.

One consequence worth naming: if The Ram appears, **The Deer does not spawn that night** (unless a cultist attack is running on the same night). That detail comes from a single source and nobody else states it — see the sources section for how we treat it. The practical upshot either way is the same: an announced Volcanic night already has its own threat, so you are not getting a Deer *and* a Ram.

## What It Does, Second by Second

The loop has three distinct phases and you can see most of them coming.

**1. Walk and chase.** Out of the charge, The Ram moves slower than a sprinting player, so a straight run buys you real distance. This is the phase where your flashlight matters, and it is the only phase where it does anything.

**2. Stop and telegraph.** Mid-chase it stops moving and starts winding up. The wind-up is shown on-screen — Sportskeeda describes it as **an eye with a rising red meter**, and the meter filling is the point at which the charge is already committed. The Ram is **immune to stun while it is preparing this**, so shining a light during the wind-up does nothing.

**3. Charge.** It rushes forward on a straight line and **flattens trees** in its path. It cannot be outrun during the charge — it is faster than a sprint at this point — and it cannot be killed, not even mid-charge.

The single most useful thing about phase 3 is that **the direction it faces is not the direction it runs.** Both Sportskeeda and the wiki state this independently: it can appear to be looking one way and charge at someone standing on the opposite side. So do not dodge away from where it is looking — dodge **perpendicular to the line it is committed to**, which in practice means stepping left or right the moment the meter starts to fill.

The charge is faster than a sprint, and our [Sprint Speed](/guides/sprint-speed) guide covers the boosts that come closest to matching it.

## The Turn Problem: Why Sidestepping Actually Works

The Ram's charge is a straight line with a **poor turning radius**. That is the whole counter, and it is why the advices that sound interchangeable are not:

- **Running straight away does nothing.** Once the charge starts it outpaces you, and it also has a longer reach than you expect. Straight-line running is the version that gets you clipped.
- **Stepping left or right does.** It overshoots, and the overshoot is the gap you use to get back toward your camp.
- **The turn happens early or not at all.** Because the turn radius is bad, the useful window is the moment the meter fills — not the moment it is about to fill.

Two secondary effects of the charge are worth planning around. First, **it destroys trees**, so a charge through your escape route can remove the cover or the path you were counting on. Second, the charge is what does the damage: the walking bite is reported at 25, and the charge is the part that "eradicates" you per one source. Do not read the wind-up as a warning to run — read it as a warning to **pick a side now**.

If it does not stop, the next question is what happens after the hit lands. The downed window and every way back are on the [**death and respawn**](/guides/death-and-respawn) page.

## What Stops It

| Input | Works? | Where the claim comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **Any weapon** | **No.** No health bar exists | Consistent across rblxguide, Sportskeeda and the wiki |
| **Flashlight, while walking/chasing** | **Yes** — it winces or stalls | Sportskeeda, BloxSwaps and the wiki agree |
| **Flashlight, during wind-up or charge** | **No — fully immune** | Sportskeeda and the wiki agree |
| **Campfire safe zone** | **Yes**, same as Deer and Owl | BloxSwaps states this explicitly |
| **Lava pool** | **Reported** — one source only | See the next section |

The flashlight rule is the one that catches people: game guides list "flashlight stun" as a general weakness of the unkillable monsters, and that is only true *outside* the charge. Sportskeeda's phrasing is the precise one — use the light **while it is chasing you**, not while it is winding up.

Collisions are the whole trick with it, and our [Scythe](/weapons/how-to-get-scythe) page covers the melee weapon tied to the event it appears in.

## The One Claim Nobody Has Corroborated

Exactly one source we found — a thin aggregator guide — claims you can **lure The Ram into a lava pool to temporarily remove it from the session**, and that this is the only way short of surviving to dawn to get rid of it.

We are publishing it as a single source and nothing more. Here is what we can and cannot say about it:

- **What the source says:** it lists the lava-pool lure as a survival tip and answers "can you kill it permanently?" with "no, except by temporarily removing it via lava traps."
- **What no other source says anything about:** not one of the four other guides we checked mentions lava removing a monster. The same four all describe lava only as a **hazard to players and a shredder for dropped items**.
- **What our own Volcanic Biome page documents:** lava damages and ignites you, and permanently destroys items you drop into it. **Nothing we found states that lava removes monsters of any kind.**
- **Why it is plausible but unproven:** lava does affect entities in this game, and the Ram is Volcanic-flavoured, so it is not an absurd claim. But plausibility is not a source.

If you test it, treat the outcome as unsourced either way. Do not plan a route around it.

## The Numbers Dispute

The Ram's stat line is not settled, and the disagreement is narrow and easy to read.

| Stat | Figure | Source count |
|---|---|---|
| **Walk speed** | **30** | rblxguide — one source |
| **Walk speed** | **45** | exclusively from `99nightsintheforest.wiki`, the mirror site we otherwise reject wholesale |
| **Damage** | **25** | rblxguide and Sportskeeda — two sources, agreeing |
| **Damage** | **25+ "varies depending on day count"** | the same rejected mirror, alone |
| **Health** | N/A, unkillable | three sources agree |
| **Drops** | Nothing | two sources agree |
| **Added** | **20 September 2025**, second half of the Volcanic Biome update | BloxSwaps gives the date; Sportskeeda agrees on the "second part" framing |

**Where we land:** take **25 damage** and treat **walk speed as the open question**. We list **30** because it is the figure from a source we otherwise trust, and we flag **45** rather than silently dropping it, because a 50% speed difference is large enough to change whether a straight sprint works — and the slower number is the one that makes the escape sound. The mirror site is the only source with the day-scaling damage line, and since we have rejected that site's other claims on this entity (it is the same source that supplies the "The Ram has woken up" cutscene below), we do not carry its numbers as fact.

One internal check that does hold up: our [Volcanic Biome](/locations/volcanic-biome) page dates the **biome itself to 13 September 2025**. A monster arriving a week later, described by two sources as arriving in the *second* half of the Volcanic Biome update, is consistent with that — the Ram is Part 2 of a September biome, not a separate event.

It replaces the Deer as the night's hazard, not as a boss, which is exactly the split our [Bosses](/guides/boss-guide) guide draws.

## Does It Replace The Deer, and What Does That Cost You?

Yes, on Volcanic nights The Ram stands in for The Deer. Our [The Deer](/entities/the-deer) page and our [The Owl](/entities/the-owl) page both already record that swap, so we are not re-litigating it here.

The part that is genuinely new to most players is the one-source claim that **when The Ram is out, The Deer is not** — with the stated exception of nights that also run a cultist attack. That would make a Volcanic night a **different night**, not a harder one: the most rigid, best-understood threat in the game is simply absent, replaced by something with a narrower but more violent counter.

Treat that as unconfirmed until someone else publishes it. The safe planning assumption is the boring one: a Volcanic night has a Ram, and the Ram cannot be fought.

## What It Never Drops

**Nothing.** No pelts, no gems, no chest, no pelt-trader currency. Two independent sources agree that there is no reward for engaging with it, which is why the correct mental model is not "boss" but **weather**. You do not farm weather.

That is worth stating plainly because a lot of entity guides are built around drops, and players arrive expecting The Ram to be worth something. It is worth exactly one thing: knowing how to leave.

## Does It Come Inside?

The Ram is a night creature like the Deer and Owl, and the [campfire](/locations/all-locations) is a safe zone against it, in the same way it is against the other two. Nobody we found states flatly whether it can path all the way to your fire and stand there — every source we have describes the fire as a boundary it respects rather than a place it reaches.

Practically: run toward the fire the moment you see the eye icon, because arriving there early is the difference between a wild night and a short one.

## FAQ

**Can you kill The Ram?** No. It has no health bar, and it stays unkillable even mid-charge. Three sources agree.

**Is it faster than you?** Walking, no — you outrun it. Charging, yes — and that is the phase that matters.

**Why can't I just stun it with my flashlight?** You can, but only while it is walking and chasing. During the wind-up and during the charge it is immune.

**The way it's facing isn't where it's going — is that a bug?** Reported by two sources as normal behaviour. Use it: the Ram commits to a line and turns badly, so the counter is always a sideways step, never a straight run.

**Does it drop anything?** Nothing at all. There is no reason to engage with it beyond survival.

**Can I dodge by running around a tree?** No — the charge destroys trees in its path, so cover does not reliably survive contact.

**Is the lava-pool trick real?** One source claims it; four others say nothing about it and describe lava purely as a player hazard and loot shredder. Untested as far as we can tell.

**Why is there a Ram statue in the Caverns?** Different thing. The Caverns' green gem offers four statues — Deer, Owl, Ram and Bat — and picking the Ram one grants a **reduced chance of lightning** blessing. That is on [The Caverns](/locations/the-caverns) and has nothing to do with the monster itself.
