The **Polar Bear** is the snow-biome bruiser — the direct upgrade to [The Bear](/entities/the-bear) and, with 500 HP and 50 damage per hit, the single highest-damage killable entity in the game. It guards the Polar Bear Cave, drops a pelt for warm clothing (not for the Pelt Trader), and — if you bring a Strong Flute — becomes a top-tier cold-weather pet. This page covers its stats, where it lives, what it pays out, and the two ways to deal with it.

Last verified: **2026-09-30**.

| | |

| **HP** | 500 |
| **Damage** | 50 per hit (highest of any killable entity) |
| **Walk speed** | 28 |

| **Drops** | Morsel (7–8), Steak (3–4), Polar Bear Pelt (0–1) |

The HP and damage are consistent across the fandom wiki, rblxguide and 99nightsinforest, all checked 2026-09-30. The Polar Bear is widely called the "strongest killable entity" alongside the Mammoth (800 HP but lower damage).

## How It Behaves

- **It is a snow specialist.** It spawns in the **Snow Biome** once your campfire is Level 4+, most reliably at the **Polar Bear Cave** (two bears inside). It used to also appear in the normal Forest on a snowy patch, but that was pre-Snow-Biome.
- **It teleports home.** Like every animal, lure it too far and it vanishes back to its post — kill it where it lives (climbing on top of the cave and shooting down works, or 6 Bear Traps in a 2×3 grid).
- **It jumps terrain.** Big as it is, it can unexpectedly hop over geometry, so use elevation and safe spots.

## What It Drops — and What the Pelt Is For

| Drop | Amount | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| **Morsel** | 7–8 | Food (cook it) |
| **Steak** | 3–4 | Food (cook it) |
| **Polar Bear Pelt** | 0–1 | **Does NOT trade at the Pelt Trader.** It crafts warm clothing — 2 pelts make the Polar Bear Hat — which is what you brave the snow for |

Worth flagging: unlike the [Alpha Wolf Pelt](/entities/the-alpha-wolf) or [Bear Pelt](/entities/the-bear), the **Polar Bear Pelt is not wanted by the Pelt Trader**. Its value is cold-resistance gear, so hunt it when you need to survive the snow, not to fuel trades.

## How to Fight It

- **Ranged is the only safe answer.** A [Rifle](/weapons/how-to-get-rifle) or [Tactical Shotgun](/weapons/how-to-get-shotgun) keeps you out of its 50-damage reach. Melee is not recommended — one hit can drop an unarmored survivor.
- **Bear Traps.** 6 traps in a 2×3 grid chunk it hard; it pathing-locks on rocks and structures, so lure it into a corner.
- **Use elevation.** Climb the Polar Bear Cave and shoot down, or hide under the Research Station near the blue boxes, to take zero damage.
- **Don't bring pets.** A Polar Bear will delete a pet; keep your companions away.

## How to Tame It

The Polar Bear is a late-game pet — the strongest cold-weather companion you can field.

- **Tool:** a **Strong Taming Flute** (Tier 3). Good or Old will not work.
- **Cycles:** **5** angry faces above its head.
- **Food:** the most-cited set is **1 Rib + 1 Stew + 1 Swordfish per cycle** (×5 = Rib ×5, Stew ×5, Swordfish ×5). A second group of guides reports **1 Rib + 2 Stew per cycle** (×5 = Rib ×5, Stew ×10), with no Swordfish. Both are reported; read the speech bubble in your own run.
- **Rule:** the Polar Bear must be **undamaged** to start the mini-game.

The flute tiers and the full per-animal food table are on [Taming Guide](/taming/taming-guide). If you want the regular bear first, [How to Tame a Bear](/taming/how-to-tame-a-bear) covers the easier Strong-Flute tame.
