The Scorpion is the [Volcanic Biome](/locations/volcanic-biome)'s answer to the Frog: a small, fast hostile whose real threat is not the hit, it is the **poison** it leaves behind. It is also the gate on the [Infernal Helmet](/items/how-to-get-infernal-helmet) — its shell is one of only two ingredients — and, unusually for a hostile, something you can recruit.

That combination makes it one of the few entities in the game worth a dedicated page: it is a threat, a crafting source and a pet all at once.

Scorpions sit behind a level 2 campfire — the same upgrade that starts revealing the map. The ladder those gates hang off is on [Campfire](/guides/campfire).

## Where It Spawns

**The Volcanic Biome, and nowhere else.** Every source agrees on that. Sources also tie their spawn to a **Level 2+ campfire**, which is the same upgrade that unlocks the map and gives you access to the volcanic region in the first place.

Within the biome there are two structures:

| Location | Scorpions | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Scorpion Nest** | ~4 | Dry bushes double as nests — walk into range and one spawns immediately |
| **Scorpion Pit** | **7** | All seven can swarm at once; contains a Blue Spiked Chest and three resources |

The Pit is the one to prepare for. Seven Scorpions stacking poison on you at the same time will end a solo run that walked in blind.

## How It Behaves

- **It wanders** until you enter its detection radius or damage it, then it runs at you and attacks in range.
- **It gives up** if every player leaves its detection radius — **or if it gets too close to a lit campfire.** That is your panic button: Scorpions will not chase you onto a lit campfire.
- **It teleports home** if it strays too far from its spawn point without a target, which is why nests keep producing them.
- **Poison is a chance, not a guarantee.** Like Frogs, a Scorpion attack *may* apply poison damage over time. The hit itself is only 20; the bleed is what kills you.

Poison ticks on top of lava, and the guide's own advice is to bring bandages; our [Healing, Bandages and Medkits](/guides/healing-guide) guide prices that advice.

Fifty health dies to almost anything, which is why it is the only animal the calc flips order on. Every weapon against every enemy, in our [weapon damage and time to kill guide](/guides/weapon-damage-and-time-to-kill).

## How to Fight It

**Use ranged, always.** This is the single most repeated piece of advice across every source, and the reasoning is the same everywhere: melee range is where the poison lands, and poison is what actually drains you. A [Rifle](/weapons/how-to-get-rifle) or [Tactical Shotgun](/weapons/how-to-get-shotgun) keeps you clean.

- **Pull them one at a time.** The threat scales with numbers, not with the individual Scorpion.
- **Do not wade into a Pit blind.** Back into a choke point and let them come to you.
- **Do not fight in tight volcanic terrain** with no escape route.
- **Retreat to a lit campfire** if things go wrong — aggression drops.
- **Bring bandages.** Between poison ticks and lava, this biome punishes sloppy routing more than any other.

At 50 HP a single decent hit or two drops one, so the fight is never the problem. The problem is standing where three of them can reach you.

The Scorpion drops no Steak at all, and our [Steak](/items/steak) page lists exactly which animals do.

## Drops

| Item | Quantity | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Morsel** | 0–1 | The common drop |
| **Scorpion Shell** | 0–1 | **Rare** — the one you are here for |

**Scorpions have one of the lowest drop rates of any entity in the game**, which is the whole reason the Infernal Helmet is a grind rather than a purchase. Plan for a lot of kills.

**What the shell is for:**

- **[Infernal Helmet](/items/how-to-get-infernal-helmet)** — the recipe is **6 Scorpion Shells + 1 Cultist King Antler**. Take 8–10 shells on a farming trip rather than 6; running short after you already have the Antler is the annoying version of this grind.
- **A permanent damage buff** — the [Big Game Hunter](/classes/big-game-hunter-class) class can consume Scorpion Shells for one. If you play that class, shells are not purely helmet fuel. See [All Classes](/classes/all-classes).

If you want the whole crop in one place, [Pumpkin](/items/pumpkin) covers where pumpkins come from now, what a farm plot yields every two days, and which item really gates an apex tame.

The Scorpion wants a Volcanic Biome trip; [Kiwi](/entities/the-kiwi) wants the map edge near a Level 6 campfire, which is a longer road for the same Berry.

The Scorpion is billed in Steak, and the one tame anywhere in the game billed in [Chili](/items/chili) is the Arctic Fox, on our Chili page.

## How to Tame One

Yes — and it is cheap. The Scorpion is one of the six animals the starting **Old Taming Flute** can handle (see the [Taming Flute](/items/taming-flute) page for the full tier table).

| | |
|---|---|
| **Flute** | Old Taming Flute (level 1 — the one from the pot in the Domestication Cabin) |
| **Stages** | **2** (one source says 3) |
| **Food per stage** | 1 Steak + 1 Morsel |
| **Total** | **2 Steak + 2 Morsel** |
| **Where** | Volcanic Biome — nests and pits |

The loop is the standard one: play the flute, **win the 30-second minigame** (keep the red bar inside the green zone, same as fishing), feed the food it asks for, repeat until the faces above its head turn into pink hearts. Full mechanics on the [Taming Guide](/taming/taming-guide).

**Is it worth a pet slot?** It is rated as **biome-specific utility** rather than a combat pet — see [Best Pet](/taming/best-pet). At 2 Steak and 2 Morsel it is one of the cheapest tames in the game, and if you are already in the Volcanic Biome farming shells, recruiting one costs you almost nothing extra. It is not the pet you bring to a boss fight.

One hundred scorpions is the Pest Control badge, filed among the event badges on our [All Badges](/guides/all-badges) page.

## The Pest Control Badge

Killing **100 Scorpions** earns the **Pest Control** badge, which also unlocks a **Volcano Mini** furniture item. At a 0–1 shell drop rate and a nest-based spawn, expect this to arrive on its own while you are farming for the Infernal Helmet rather than as a target you chase deliberately. Difficulty is rated 5.

## Where Sources Disagree

**Health: 50 or 75?** Four sources say **50**; one structured wiki lists **75**. We use 50 as the majority reading, and note that at 50 HP the fight is trivial anyway — the poison is the threat either way.

**Drops.** Four sources say **Morsel (0–1) and Scorpion Shell (0–1)**. One structured wiki instead lists "Morsel 2–3x guaranteed, Steak 1x guaranteed" and does not mention the shell at all. That reading contradicts four others and conflicts with the Infernal Helmet recipe, so we go with the majority.

**Taming stages: 2 or 3?** Most sources say **2** (2 Steak + 2 Morsel total). One says **3**. Our [Taming Guide](/taming/taming-guide) lists it as "2–3" to cover both; budget for 2 and carry a spare.

**Walk speed.** 20 is the quoted figure, with sources agreeing they move faster around nests and pits. No source gives the exact faster number.

## FAQ

**Where do Scorpions spawn in 99 Nights in the Forest?**
Only in the **Volcanic Biome**, at **Scorpion Nests** and the **Scorpion Pit**. A Level 2+ campfire is required.

**How much damage do they do?**
20 per hit, plus a chance to apply poison damage over time. The poison, not the hit, is the danger.

**Do they drop Scorpion Shells every time?**
No. The shell is a **rare** 0–1 drop, and Scorpions have among the lowest drop rates in the game.

**What is the Scorpion Shell for?**
The [Infernal Helmet](/items/how-to-get-infernal-helmet) — **6 shells plus 1 Cultist King Antler**. The Big Game Hunter class can also eat them for a permanent damage buff.

**Can you tame a Scorpion?**
Yes. Old Taming Flute, 2 stages, **2 Steak and 2 Morsel** in total.

**How do you stop them attacking?**
Get to a **lit campfire** or leave their detection radius entirely. Both break aggression.

**How many Scorpions are in a Scorpion Pit?**
Seven, and they can all engage at once. Clear them from a choke point.
