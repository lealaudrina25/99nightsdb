The Ice Sword is the Snow Biome's melee weapon and one of the few items in 99 Nights in the Forest that no recipe can produce. You have to open the right chest in the right cave and get lucky. This page covers exactly which chests, which two places they spawn, what the sword actually does once you have it, and whether it beats the Morningstar.

Some Chests hand out seeds; the complete seed list is on our [Seed Packs](/items/seed-packs) page.
Last verified: **2026-09-30**.

## Quick Answer

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Where | **Snow Biome only** — Polar Bear Cave and Ice Temple |
| Chest | **Ice Chest** (some guides also call it the "Silver Ice Chest" — see the naming note below) |
| Damage | **35** base (45.5 as [Brawler](/classes/brawler-class), 56 as fully boosted Berserker) |
| Swing rate | **0.5 seconds** |
| Special | **Frost Effect** — slows enemies on hit |
| Can you craft it? | **No recipe exists** |
| Worth chasing? | Yes — it ties the Morningstar on paper and brings crowd control |

Thirty-five damage twice a second should beat seventy damage once a second, and it does — until an enemy's health sits just above two hits. The exceptions are tabulated in our [weapon damage and time to kill guide](/guides/weapon-damage-and-time-to-kill).

## What It Does

The Ice Sword is a melee weapon with a slow attached, which is a combination nothing else in the melee tree offers at this damage level.

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| **Damage** | 35 (45.5 with Brawler, 56 with a fully boosted Berserker) |
| **Swing rate** | 0.5 seconds |
| **Effect** | Applies the **Frost Effect** — enemies hit are slowed and emit frost particles |

Two caveats on those numbers. The damage figure is **35** across three separate sources we checked; an older revision of the fandom wiki lists **30** (39 with Brawler), which we read as a pre-adjustment value, so treat 35 as current. Swing rate is listed as **0.5 seconds** by two sources and **0.6 seconds ("same as an axe")** by the fandom wiki — a difference small enough that it changes nothing in practice.

**The Frost Effect is the real selling point.** Enemies you hit are slowed rather than just damaged, which turns it into a kiting and crowd-control tool. The clearest practical expression of it: as the **Brawler** class, the Ice Sword kills a **Wolf or an [Arctic Fox](/entities/the-arctic-fox) in two hits**. Against packs — Cultists, Wolves, foxes — that slow is worth more than raw damage, because it lets you disengage from a fight you were losing.

Every Ice Sword starts in cold weather or a frozen biome, and our [Weather and Night Events](/guides/weather-and-night-events) guide separates the two.


There is no recipe and no vendor. The Ice Sword comes from **Ice Chests**, and those spawn in exactly two places, both deep in the Snow Biome:

| Location | What to Expect |
|---|---|
| **Polar Bear Cave** | The cave entrance is guarded by [Polar Bears](/entities/the-polar-bear) — two inside, per our Polar Bear page. You are opening chests inside a bear's house |
| **Ice Temple** | The Cultist-held structure that is also one of the steadier [Cultist Gems](/items/cultist-gem) routes in the game |

Both locations are Snow Biome only, and the biome itself is not guaranteed to generate — the map splits into outer sectors and Snow competes with Volcanic for the slot. See [Snow Biome](/locations/snow-biome) for the cold mechanic, the freeze meter, and how to survive long enough to loot anything.

One naming note, because it trips people up: guides also write this chest as the **Silver Ice Chest** or the **Fancy Ice Chest**, but neither name appears in any published chest table — every table lists only the **Ice Chest** and the separate, weaker **Cold Chest**. So **Ice Chest** is the container we can actually verify, and the other two names are unverified aliases rather than a separate chest. Our Snow Biome page also lists the Cold Chest as a possible spawn, so check both types when you are in there. The temple side of this, including which floor the Ice Chest sits on, is on the [Ice Temple](/locations/ice-temple) page.

**Expect to open several.** The Ice Chest loot table is shared — the same chests also produce the Ice Axe, Frozen Shuriken, Snowball and Ice Bow — so the sword is one roll among several rather than the chest's purpose. There is no way to improve the odds; the only lever you have is visiting more chests across more runs. Ice Chests are one rung of the ladder laid out on [Chest Tiers](/items/chest-tiers).

## Ice Sword vs Morningstar

This comparison comes up constantly in community tier discussions, and the numbers explain why it is genuinely close rather than a matter of taste.

On the listed stats, **both weapons produce about 70 damage per second**:

| Weapon | Damage | Swing rate | Damage per second | Special |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ice Sword** | 35 | 0.5s | ~70 | Frost slow |
| **[Morningstar](/weapons/how-to-get-morningstar)** | 70 | 1.0s | ~70 | Explosion bar (area damage when it fills) |

That is straightforward arithmetic on the published figures, not a claim from any single guide — and it is why "many players consider the Ice Sword better than the Morningstar depending on how you use it" is a defensible position rather than forum noise. Same output, plus a slow, and the Ice Sword is meaningfully easier to find: Ice Chests versus Gold Chests, which are the rarest chest tier in the game.

Where the Morningstar still wins is burst: 70 in one swing ends a threat faster than two 35s, and its explosion bar adds area damage the Ice Sword has nothing to answer. If you fight single tough enemies, the Morningstar. If you fight packs, the Ice Sword.

Both sit in the **S tier** on our [Best Weapons](/weapons/best-weapons) ranking, along with the [Obsidiron Hammer](/weapons/how-to-get-obsidiron-hammer), Infernal Sword, [Poison Spear](/weapons/how-to-get-poison-spear) and [Flamethrower](/weapons/how-to-get-flamethrower).

## Who Should Use It

- **Brawler** — the standout pairing. Damage scales to 45.5, and wolves and [Arctic Foxes](/entities/the-arctic-fox) die in two hits.
- **Berserker** — **56** damage when fully boosted, the highest number the sword reaches. That number is *reported*, not derived: 56 ÷ 35 divides out as ×1.6, but our [Berserker](/classes/berserker-class) page sizes the full bonus at roughly **+25–30%** for a single source, and warns that the larger factor only appears while the level-2 buff window is open. Use 56 as a published figure, not as a rule for scaling other weapons.
- **Anyone farming the Snow Biome** — you are already in the caves for Cultist Gems and pelts, so the chests are a free roll rather than a detour.

If you are heading in purely for the sword, bring warm clothing. The freeze meter will stop you sprinting, and a Polar Bear you cannot run from is how runs end. The [Polar Bear](/entities/the-polar-bear) page covers the elevation trick that lets you clear the cave without taking damage.

## FAQ

**Can I craft the Ice Sword?**
No. Every source agrees there is no recipe. Chests only.

**Can I get it outside the Snow Biome?**
No. Both spawn points — Polar Bear Cave and Ice Temple — are Snow Biome locations.

**Which chest exactly?**
The **Ice Chest**. A few guides call it the Silver Ice Chest, but that name is not in any chest table — see the naming note above. Our Snow Biome page lists a Cold Chest as a possible additional spawn.

**Is it better than the Morningstar?**
On damage per second they are tied at roughly 70. The Ice Sword adds a slow and is easier to obtain; the Morningstar has better burst and area damage.

**What class makes it strongest?**
Berserker at 56 damage fully boosted — reported by the same sources that give the 35 base, though no source states what multiplier produces it. The [Berserker class](/classes/berserker-class) page puts the whole bonus at roughly +25–30% on one source's word, with our own stacked estimate landing near ×1.6 for the 30 seconds after a revive. Brawler is still the better all-round fit because of the two-hit breakpoints against wolves and [foxes](/entities/the-arctic-fox).

**Is there a fire version?**
Yes — the **Infernal Sword** is explicitly its counterpart, trading the Frost slow for Burning damage over time. Same idea, opposite biome. See [How to Get the Infernal Sword](/weapons/how-to-get-infernal-sword).
