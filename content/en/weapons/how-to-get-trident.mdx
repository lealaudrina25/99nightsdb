The **Trident** is the only weapon in 99 Nights in the Forest that you cannot loot, craft, trade or buy — you **fish** for it. It is also the rarest single item in the game, tied with the [Morningstar](/weapons/how-to-get-morningstar), which is why a lot of players are not even sure it is real. It is: it comes out of **Skeleton Lakes** (also called **Bone** Lakes), and the odds are genuinely punishing.

This page covers the numbers, the exact fishing route, the mini-game, and the one thing that actually raises your chances — **which lake you cast in**.

Last verified: **2026-09-30**.

## What It Does

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| **Damage** | 35 base; 56 with a fully boosted Berserker |
| **Damage per second** | ~58 |
| **Swing rate** | ~0.6 seconds |
| **Reach** | Long — spear-class |
| **Effect** | None — it is raw damage rather than a status effect |

**On the damage number, sources split.** Deltia's Gaming gives **35 base and 58.3 damage per second**, rising to **56 (93.3 dps)** fully boosted. rblxguide instead lists the Trident at **37.5**. We lean on **35**, because Deltia's own pair of figures is internally consistent, and **56 is 1.6 × 35** — the same ratio our [Berserker](/classes/berserker-class) page reaches when both of that class's level bonuses stack. That ratio is an **estimate, not a published rule**, and the class page puts the Level 3 bonus on its own at roughly +25–30%. So 56 may well have been measured with the level-2 buff window open. Take **56 as the reported number, not as a multiplier to carry into other weapons** — 37.5 → 62 simply does not reconcile either way. And **58.3 dps at 35 damage implies a 0.6-second swing**, which is where that swing figure comes from.

That puts the Trident in the game's **top damage band, just below the Ice Sword**: same ballpark per hit, but a slightly slower swing. What it does not have is a special effect — no slow like the [Ice Sword](/weapons/how-to-get-ice-sword), no burn like the [Infernal Sword](/weapons/how-to-get-infernal-sword), no explosion like the Morningstar. It is a spear that hits hard.

Deltia adds two useful breakpoints: the Trident **kills a Wolf in two headshots and an Alpha Wolf in four**.

## The Only Route: Fishing a Bone Lake

There is no second door. Every source agrees the Trident comes from fishing, and almost all of them narrow it to one type of water.

**1. Get a fishing rod.** Collect an **Old Rod** from the **Fishing Hut**, or start a run as the **Fisherman** class, which spawns with one. Better rods help in general, though no source promises a rod tier that biases the Trident specifically.

**2. Find a Skeleton / Bone Lake.** These are the lakes with **dark purple water**, and they only appear once your **campfire is Level 6**. On a map where the highest-tier chests sit at Level 4, that makes the Bone Lake one of the last things to unlock in a run — so a Trident attempt is a late-run activity, not a day-one one.

**3. Cast into the ripples.** Equip the rod and click the lake to fish. Look for **white, transparent ripples** in the water: those are the **"fast" spots**, and casting into them shortens the wait for a bite.

**4. Win the mini-game.** When something bites, a bar appears. **Click when the moving red bar lines up with the green rectangle** to fill the progress meter. As the rarest catch, the Trident is at the far end of that difficulty curve — expect many attempts that end in ordinary fish.

**Practical read:** you are rolling a very low drop rate on a late-game, one-shot-per-cast activity. If your goal is a Trident specifically, the honest advice is to fish Bone Lakes **while you are doing something else nearby**, not to stand at the water for a whole run.

### Why the Bone Lake Matters

Not every lake gives the same odds. Sources agree the Trident is **most likely in Skeleton / Bone Lakes** and rarer everywhere else — so casting in an ordinary Forest pond is not a slower route to the same prize, it is close to a wasted cast. Save the attempts for the purple water.

## Classes and Boosts

The Trident scales with the same melee bonuses as everything else:

- **Berserker** — the pick the guides name. It takes the Trident to its **56** ceiling when fully boosted, and the class's whole kit is built around surviving a fight rather than avoiding one, which suits a no-effects melee weapon.
- **Brawler** — also boosts it (Gamezebo notes both), with the usual trade-off of no ranged weapons. See [All Classes](/classes/all-classes) for the costs.

## The Seasonal Shortcut

rblxguide lists a second, non-fishing source: the **gold presents from the 2025 Christmas event** could drop a Trident. That is a **single-source, seasonal** note — worth remembering if a winter event comes back, but not a route you can use today. The same source also carries a recurring caution about the Trident generally: the fishing odds are "extremely low", so treat any catch as a bonus rather than a farm target.

## Is It Worth It?

**Keep it if you catch one — do not build a run around getting one.**

The Trident's problem is not its damage; 35–56 with long reach is a genuinely good melee weapon. The problem is that the only way to get it is a low-percentage roll on a late-game fishing minigame, and unlike the Morningstar — which falls out of Gold Chests you were opening anyway — you have to go out of your way for it. That is exactly why our [Best Weapons](/weapons/best-weapons) page lists it under special sources rather than on the early-game ladder: it is a rare reward, not a progression step.

If you want a strong melee weapon you can actually plan around, farm the [Morningstar](/weapons/how-to-get-morningstar) or the [Ice Sword](/weapons/how-to-get-ice-sword) instead. Fish the Bone Lake for the Trident when the run is already going well.

## FAQ

**Can I craft or buy the Trident?**
No. Fishing is the only route. It is not in any chest tier, and no class starts with one. Every chest tier, and what it holds, is on [Chest Tiers](/items/chest-tiers).

**Which lake exactly?**
The **Skeleton / Bone Lake** — the one with dark purple water. Ordinary ponds are far worse odds.

**What campfire level do I need?**
**Level 6** for the Bone Lakes to appear on the map.

**Is it real?**
Yes — it is just rare enough that a lot of players have never seen one. Several guides open by addressing the rumour directly.

**What is its damage?**
**35** base (some sources say 37.5), **56** fully boosted, at roughly a **0.6-second** swing.

**Does it have a special effect?**
No. It is a high-damage, long-reach melee weapon and nothing more.

**Any other way to get one?**
rblxguide lists the **2025 Christmas gold presents** as a past event source. Treat it as seasonal, not permanent.

**How good is it compared to the Ice Sword?**
Similar per-hit damage and longer reach, but a slower swing and no slowing effect — so slightly lower damage per second and less utility.
