The **Obsidiron Hammer** is the melee weapon that comes out of the Meteor Shower event — and statistically it is a **clone of the Morningstar**: same 70 damage, same one-second swing, same explosion bar. The only reason to chase one over the other is *how* you get it, because this one ends in a chest you craft yourself rather than a chest you happen to find.

It is also, for now, an **event weapon**: the whole chain only exists while a Meteor Shower is running. This page covers the full chain, the numbers, and the one detail — the Obsidiron armour set — that makes the explosion bar charge faster.

Last verified: **2026-09-30**.

## What It Does

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| **Damage** | 70 base; ~70 damage per second at a 1-second swing |
| **Swing rate** | 1 second |
| **Special** | Explosion bar — fills as you fight, then detonates around you |
| **Explosion damage** | roughly **50–60** to everything in range (sources give 50–60 and ~60) |
| **Hidden synergy** | The Obsidiron Body and Boots make the bar fill faster |

**70 damage is the top of the obtainable-melee table**, level with the [Morningstar](/weapons/how-to-get-morningstar) and the disputed Cultist King Mace. Both the fandom wiki and rblxguide state the three are stat-identical, which is the cleanest example in the game of "same numbers, three different doors".

### The Explosion Bar

The Hammer has a meter that fills **every time you deal damage** — and, per Deltia's Gaming, **every time you take damage while wearing the Obsidiron Body and Boots**. When it tops out, an explosion goes off around you and hits everything nearby for roughly 50–60. rblxguide notes the same bar can also be sped up with the **Obsidiron Boots, Obsidiron Body and the obsidian shield** the Brute class unlocks at level 3.

That is the practical reason the set matters: the Hammer and the Body drop from the **same chest**, so a couple of lucky runs hand you the whole package.

Full stats, the class breakpoints and the damage-per-second comparison are on [Best Weapons](/weapons/best-weapons) and [How to Get the Morningstar](/weapons/how-to-get-morningstar) — treat this page as the *acquisition* guide.

## How to Get It — The Meteor Chain

There is exactly one route, and it is a five-step chain that only opens during a **Meteor Shower**. Every step is a known stopping point, so plan them in one session.

**1. Catch a Meteor Shower.** The event drops the **Meteor Anvil** onto the map, highlighted so you can see it. Our [Infernal Helmet](/items/how-to-get-infernal-helmet) page records the anvil appearing **from day 5 onward**; the event itself was added with the **Meteor Shower Update on 27 September 2025**.

**2. Repair the Meteor Anvil.** It arrives broken: put the **broken pieces back on the big anvil** to activate it. Only then does it offer the Obsidiron recipes.

**3. Mine the materials.** The chest recipe needs three ingredients:

| Material | Amount | How to get it |
|---|---|---|
| Meteor Shard | 10 | Mining the **red crystals** at meteor crash sites, or killing **Meteor Crabs** |
| Gold Shard | 5 | A **rare drop** from those same red crystals |
| Obsidiron Ingot | 3 | Refined from **Raw Obsidiron Ore**, mined from the **black crystals** — dip the raw ore in **lava**, then dunk it in **water** |

**4. Craft the Obsidiron Chest.** At the Meteor Anvil, select the Obsidiron Chest recipe, lay the materials down and wait for the craft to finish. You get a **blueprint** — carry it back to your **campfire** and place the chest like any other furniture.

**5. Open it.** The chest is a **random roll**, not a guaranteed Hammer. Deltia's wording is "if you are lucky, you may get the Obsidiron Hammer on your first attempt — otherwise rinse and repeat". The chest also yields the **Obsidiron Body**.

**Cost note:** step 5 is the annoying one, because you may craft several chests before the Hammer appears — and every chest costs the full 10 / 5 / 3. Budget the shards accordingly, and keep mining red and black crystals every Shower rather than only when you decide you want the weapon.

## Other Sources (Seasonal)

The fandom wiki records two event routes beyond the Meteor chain: it was obtainable during the **Thanksgiving event**, and it can currently drop from **saving the elves** — as a chance reward **alongside 5 candy canes (6 with the Tier 3 Santa's Helper class)**. That is a **single-source** note about seasonal content, so treat it as "worth checking during a holiday event" rather than a reliable farm.

## Obsidiron Hammer vs Morningstar

The two are the same weapon wearing different hats:

| | [Morningstar](/weapons/how-to-get-morningstar) | Obsidiron Hammer |
|---|---|---|
| Damage / swing | 70 / 1s | 70 / 1s |
| Special | Explosion bar | **Same** explosion bar |
| Source | Gold Chest (random) | **Obsidiron Chest** — crafted from 10 / 5 / 3 during a Meteor Shower |
| The catch | Chest RNG | **Event-gated** — no Shower, no route |

**Neither is strictly better.** The Morningstar is available on any run and you trip over Gold Chests while doing other things; the Obsidiron Hammer removes the chest lottery *but only exists during the event*. If a Meteor Shower is live, craft the chest; if not, farm Gold Chests and let the Morningstar find you. Both are S-tier on [Best Weapons](/weapons/best-weapons).

## FAQ

**Can I craft the Obsidiron Hammer directly?**
No. You craft the **Obsidiron Chest**, then open it — the Hammer is one of its rolls.

**Is it guaranteed from the chest?**
No. Repeat the craft until it drops.

**What is the chest recipe?**
10 Meteor Shards, 5 Gold Shards and 3 Obsidiron Ingots. (A single-source figure — see below.)

**Where do I get Obsidiron Ingots?**
Mine **Raw Obsidiron Ore** from the black crystals at a meteor site, dip it in lava, then dunk it in water.

**Can I get it outside a Meteor Shower?**
The fandom wiki lists holiday-event drops (Thanksgiving, and the elves reward). Otherwise, no — the chain is event-only.

**Is it the same as the Morningstar?**
Statistically yes — 70 damage, 1-second swing, same explosion bar.

**What does it pair with?**
The **Obsidiron Body and Boots** (and the Brute class's obsidian shield), which make the explosion bar fill faster.
