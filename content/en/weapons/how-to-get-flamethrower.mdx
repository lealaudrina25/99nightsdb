The **Flamethrower** is the Pyromaniac's signature weapon and one of the best crowd-control tools in 99 Nights in the Forest — a continuous stream of fire that burns everything in front of you and **auto-cooks the meat of anything it kills**. It is also, like the [Katana](/weapons/how-to-get-katana), **class-exclusive**: there is no chest, no recipe and no drop. The only way to hold one is to buy the **Pyromaniac class for 600 Diamonds**.

Some Chests hand out seeds; the complete seed list is on our [Seed Packs](/items/seed-packs) page.
This page covers what it does, the three Pyromaniac perks, and the part that actually decides whether you enjoy it — the **fuel economy**.

Last verified: **2026-09-30**.

## Quick Answer

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| How do I get it? | **Buy the Pyromaniac class (600 Diamonds)** — you spawn with it |
| Any other route? | **No.** No chest, no craft, no drop, and it **cannot be traded** |
| Damage | **8.18** per tick, **0.05-second** fire rate (~160 damage per second while on target) |
| Ammo | **Fuel Canisters** and **Oil Barrels** — 150 capacity |
| Special | **Burning** on hit, **area damage**, and kills drop **cooked** meat |
| Range | **Short** — this is a melee-range crowd weapon, not a rifle |
| Best for | Night waves, base defence, rescuing the Missing Children |
| Worth it? | Yes if you want area control — but budget for the fuel grind |

## What It Does

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| **Damage** | 8.18 per tick |
| **Fire rate** | 0.05 seconds between ticks (continuous stream) |
| **Ammo capacity** | 150 fuel |
| **Effect** | Sets enemies on **fire** (damage over time) |
| **Area** | Hits **everything** in the cone, not one target |
| **Bonus** | Animals killed by the flame drop **cooked** meat |

The damage number looks low next to the 70 of a [Morningstar](/weapons/how-to-get-morningstar), and that is the point to understand: **it is a per-tick figure, not per swing.** At a 0.05-second tick that works out to roughly **160 damage per second** while the flame is on target — which, on the published numbers, is the highest sustained figure of any weapon we can put a number to. (That is our arithmetic on the tick figures, not a sourced claim.)

Where it loses is **range**. The Flamethrower is described as **short-ranged** by every source that covers it, so you are trading the safety of a rifle for the ability to delete a whole wave at once. As the Pyromaniac's own level-3 perk hints, you are meant to be in the middle of it.

Both arrived in the same update, which is the cleanest date anyone publishes for either. The detail behind it is in [**our fishing guide**](/guides/fishing-guide).

## The Only Route: The Pyromaniac Class

The Flamethrower is **exclusive to the Pyromaniac**, and it is stricter about it than most class weapons:

- **Cost: 600 Diamonds** — the joint-most-expensive tier, alongside Cyborg, Vampire, Necromancer and [Big Game Hunter](/classes/big-game-hunter-class).
- **You cannot drop it.** Unlike a chest weapon, the Flamethrower **cannot be handed to a teammate**, so a run only has as many flamethrowers as it has Pyromaniacs.
- **The only weapon that runs on fuel.** Every other ranged weapon uses ammo types; this one uses Fuel Canisters and Oil Barrels, which is a different supply chain entirely.

The full cost-and-roster context is on [All Classes](/classes/all-classes), and the role it fills is on [Best Class](/classes/best-class) — where it is listed as the pick for **monster defence**, clearing night-wave pressure.

The Pyromaniac was added in the **Fishing Update**, alongside the Fisherman class.

- **The other auto-cook route.** [Chef and Cook](/classes/chef-class) — flame kills drop cooked meat, but only the Chef's station turns it into a dish with a timed buff.

The first perk doubles as a food supply: animals killed by fire drop already-cooked meat, which is worth double and costs no health. See [**Hunger**](/guides/hunger).

## The Three Perks

This is where the class either clicks or does not:

| Level | Perk | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Reload with **Fuel Canisters and Oil Barrels**; **food is auto-cooked on drop** | — |
| **2** | Canisters and barrels **restore even more fuel** | Kill **150** enemies with fire |
| **3** | **Move faster for every burning enemy** | Kill **400** enemies with fire |

**Level 1 is the one that matters most**, because it fixes the Flamethrower's weakness: without a reload source, a fuel weapon is a paperweight. The auto-cook on drop is a quiet second benefit — it turns every animal you kill into food that skips the campfire entirely.

**Level 3 is the mobility payoff.** Because the Flamethrower is short-ranged, the speed boost for each burning enemy is what keeps you alive in a crowd: you set the group alight and then outrun the ones that survive.

One trivia worth knowing: the class was **shadow-buffed** after release — the Flamethrower's **range was increased** and **Oil Barrels were made usable as fuel** (originally only Fuel Canisters worked). Older guides may still describe the pre-buff version.

A flamethrower spends Fuel Canister, which is the one "ammo" in the game your campfire also wants; our [Ammo and Guns](/guides/ammo-guide) guide covers that clash.


The Flamethrower is not gated by its damage — it is gated by **what it eats**. Two items feed it:

- **Fuel Canisters** — found around the map and in chests.
- **Oil Barrels** — also found on the map and in higher-tier loot, and the **only renewable source** is the **Oil Drill**, which is slow.

Two consequences every Pyromaniac learns the hard way:

1. **Your fuel competes with your campfire.** These same canisters and barrels can go into the fire, so an early-game Pyromaniac is choosing between a warm night and a loaded flamethrower. Once your campfire is maxed, that conflict goes away and the Flamethrower gets much more comfortable.
2. **More Pyromaniacs make it worse, not better.** Fuel is a shared, finite resource on a server, so a team with several Pyromaniacs will starve each other. One Pyromaniac is a strong pick; three is a fuel crisis.

The single best efficiency trick, straight from the fandom notes: **scorch an enemy until it is nearly dead and let the burn finish it**. You spend less fuel per kill, at the cost of speed. It is slower, but fuel is the bottleneck, not time.

If campfire fuel is your other headache, the whole wood-to-Biofuel loop is on [How to Get Biofuel](/items/how-to-get-biofuel).

Both arrived in the same update, and the cheaper of the two is the one players actually end up using every run. Compare them on the [**Fisherman Class**](/classes/fisherman-class) page.

Bunny kills do not count toward either number, and our [Bunny](/entities/the-bunny) page lists every class with that exclusion.

## Leveling the Class (150 and 400 Fire Kills)

Both perks are gated behind **fire kills**, and there are three things to know that the requirement screen does not tell you:

- **Any weapon can soften them up.** Lower an enemy's health with anything and land the **finishing blow with the Flamethrower** — it still counts. You do not have to burn anything from full.
- **The Infernal Sword and [Infernal Crossbow](/weapons/how-to-get-infernal-crossbow) count too**, if your map rolled the [Volcanic Biome](/locations/volcanic-biome) — their fire also counts toward the objective.
- **Bunny kills do not count.** Skip them when you are farming the requirement.

Target **clusters** rather than single enemies: the level-2 requirement is 150 fire kills and the level-3 is 400, and the AoE flame racks those up far faster on a group than on one wolf at a time.

## Where It Sits

On [Best Weapons](/weapons/best-weapons) the Flamethrower is listed in the group **ranked S by two tier lists** — the same band as the Morningstar, Ice Sword and Poison Spear. It earns that spot for a reason none of the others can match: **it is the only weapon that hits a whole crowd at once**, and it is the only one that turns a kill into free cooked food.

What it is **not** is a general-purpose weapon. It is short-ranged, it is expensive to keep fed, and every source flags it as harder to use than a gun. Take it for what it is — a wave-clearer and a base defender — and bring something with reach for everything else.

## FAQ

**Can I get the Flamethrower without the Pyromaniac class?**
No. It is class-exclusive with no chest, recipe or drop route.

**Can I craft it?**
No. Any guide describing a Flamethrower crafting recipe is wrong — it spawns with the class and nothing else.

**Can I give mine to a teammate?**
No. The Flamethrower cannot be dropped.

**What is its ammo?**
Fuel Canisters and Oil Barrels — a different supply chain from normal ammo.

**Why does it feel weak per hit?**
Because 8.18 is a **per-tick** figure at a 0.05-second fire rate, not a per-swing number. Sustained, it is one of the highest-damage weapons in the game.

**Does it cook food?**
Yes — animals killed by the flame drop **cooked** meat, and the level-1 perk auto-cooks anything you drop.

**What class is best with it?**
It is the class weapon — you are always the Pyromaniac when you hold it. Pair it with a teammate who has range.
