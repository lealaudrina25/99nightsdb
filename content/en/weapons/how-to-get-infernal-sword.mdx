The Infernal Sword is the strongest fire melee weapon in 99 Nights in the Forest and it only exists inside the Volcanic Biome. It is also genuinely hard to get — this page covers all three ways in, and which one is actually worth your time.

Last verified: **2026-09-30**.

## Quick Answer

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Where | Volcanic Biome only |
| Best route | **Volcano sacrifice** — 8 offerings spawns a reward chest |
| Other routes | Infernal Chests (rare), Cultist King drop |
| Can you craft it? | **No** |
| Worth chasing? | Yes for damage — and any kill feeds you |

## What It Does

- **Burning on every hit.** Enemies keep taking damage after you stop swinging, which is what separates it from every other melee weapon.
- **Roughly 40–50+ damage per hit.** Sources do not agree exactly: one puts it at **40 plus Burning**, two describe it as **over 50 per hit** — enough to drop a cultist in two or three swings. Treat "around 40–50 plus burn" as the working number.
- **It cooks your food for you.** Land the killing blow on an animal and the meat drops already cooked. Over a long run that is a real hunger-bar advantage, not a flavour detail. One source also notes the same effect applies to the broader Infernal set.

One caveat worth knowing before you commit a loadout: per a single guide, **the Deer and the Owl are immune** to it. That claim is not corroborated elsewhere, so verify it yourself rather than relying on it mid-fight.

## Route 1 — Volcano Sacrifice (Most Reliable)

This is the route most guides call the better odds, because unlike chests it always produces a roll.

1. Get into the **Volcanic Biome**. One guide suggests **campfire level 2–3** before going in; the biome was added as part of the Volcanic Biome update — see [Update Log & Patch Notes](/guides/updates-patch-notes).
2. Go **inside the volcano** itself and find the **bridge marked with a star symbol** over the magma pool. The sacrifice pit sits underneath it.
3. **Drop offerings into the magma** until every point of the star symbol lights up. Valid offerings include **loot, cultist corpses, and players who did not make it**.
4. Once the symbol glows all the way around, **a reward chest appears** — and the Infernal Sword is in its loot table.

**How many sacrifices?** Two independent sources both say **eight** — one describes it as eight offerings to light the symbol, another as "feed the volcano eight corpses" for a reward. Cultists are the practical source: they spawn around the volcano itself.

Two more details: poisoning scorpions are everywhere inside, so bring healing. And there is reportedly a **"Volcanology" badge at 150 sacrifices** that improves reward tiers — that comes from a single source, so treat it as unconfirmed.

## Route 2 — Infernal Chests

The Volcanic Biome's top-tier chests. Depending on the guide you read they are called **Infernal Chests**, **Inferno Chests** or "red spiked chests" — same thing, and visually they are the redder, spikier version of the Hot Chests found around the same biome.

Reported spawn points differ a little between guides, so check all of these:

| Location | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Volcanic Church** | The chest spawns on the **roof** — parkour required. Open it fast: rewards can roll off into the lava |
| **The Furnace** | The factory-like structure in the biome |
| **Ammo Furnace** | Reported to hold a *Legendary* Infernal Chest |
| **Cultist Tower / Lavaduct / Church** | Each guarded by cultists; the last one drops a Cultist Gem |
| **The Pit** | Guarded by scorpions |

The practical distinction that matters is this: at least one source lists the **Legendary** Infernal Chest as carrying the sword-specific loot table (Infernal Sack, Infernal Crossbow, Infernal Sword), which makes the **Ammo Furnace** worth prioritising over the ordinary spawns.

Be aware the ordinary versions carry other things too — one guide lists **gasoline** in the regular Infernal Chest table. You will open several before one gives you what you came for; the drop is described as extremely rare, with one source comparing its rarity to pulling the Morningstar out of a Gold Chest.

## Route 3 — The Cultist King

The boss drops it — but he drops **either the Infernal Sword or the Infernal Crossbow per kill, never both**, so it is a coin flip and he costs four Cultist Gems to summon. Full summoning walkthrough, tactics and the complete drop table on [The Cultist King](/entities/the-cultist-king).

## Which Route Should You Use?

**Do the volcano ritual, using cultists you were killing anyway.** It converts enemies you must fight into guaranteed rolls, whereas chest routes depend on both finding the chest and winning a very low roll.

The Cultist King route is worth it **if you also want the Infernal Helmet**, because you need his Antler for that regardless — then the sword is a bonus rather than the objective. Going to him purely for the sword is poor value: four gems, a hard boss fight, and a 50/50 against the crossbow.

And if you happen to unlock the **Fire Bandit class**, note that one source reports it grants the **Infernal Crossbow** outright — which sidesteps this entire grind for the ranged version.

## FAQ

**Can I craft the Infernal Sword?**
No. Every source is agreed on this. It cannot be crafted at any bench.

**Is it only in the Volcanic Biome?**
Yes, for all three routes.

**Can I get it outside the volcano?**
Yes — via Infernal Chests elsewhere in the biome, or from the Cultist King. The volcano ritual is just the most consistent of the three.

**What is it worth trading for?**
Nothing in this game has an official trade value; treat the Infernal set as end-of-tree gear you use, not sell. See [Best Weapons](/weapons/best-weapons) for how it ranks against the rest.

**Do I need a high campfire level?**
One guide recommends campfire level 2–3 for the Volcanic Biome; if wood is your bottleneck, start with [How to Get Wood Fast](/items/how-to-get-wood-fast).
