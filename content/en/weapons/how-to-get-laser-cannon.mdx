## What the Laser Cannon Actually Does

The Laser Cannon is an **alien-tech ranged weapon** with a single, devastating trick: one shot fires an instant projectile that detonates on impact and damages **every enemy caught in the blast**.

- **Damage:** 70 per direct hit (about 87.5 if you are a Cyborg — see below).
- **Area of effect:** yes. The blast hits multiple targets at once, which is why it is the best "delete a pack" button in the game.
- **Infinite ammo:** it draws from your **Alien Tech energy**, not bullets. No reload, no ammo pickups.
- **The catch:** each shot drains roughly **75% of the energy bar**, and a full recharge takes about **8 seconds**. So it is one shot, then an ~8-second downtime where you need a sidearm.
- **Hit detection:** instant. The beam lands the moment you click — no travel time to lead.

That trade (huge blast, long cooldown) is the whole weapon. Used sparingly on a clustered group, it is S-tier. Spam-fired, it leaves you defenseless.

## How to Get It: Three Ways

**1. The Cyborg Class — guaranteed.** Buy the Cyborg from the Classes shop for **600 diamonds** and it spawns in your inventory every run. This is the *only* deterministic source. (Class-shop availability may rotate — see our [Best Class](/en/classes/best-class) page for the current state, since a reported "always-available" revamp is unconfirmed.)

**2. Broken UFOs and the Mothership.** During alien invasion events these crash sites can drop the Cannon as loot. Random, not guaranteed.

**3. Alien Chests.** Another random drop from alien-themed chests.

*(Past source: it was also a Trick-or-Treat reward in the 2025 Halloween event, which is now over.)*

The honest read: ways 2 and 3 are dice rolls. If you want the Cannon reliably, the Cyborg is the path — which is exactly why the next question matters.

## The Overheat Catch (Cyborg-Specific)

The Cannon itself just has an 8-second recharge. The scary "overheat damages you" penalty is a **Cyborg class perk**, not the gun's:

- At Cyborg Level 1, if your **Alien Tech overheats**, you take damage **and** your armor is temporarily lowered.
- Practically: one Cannon shot leaves you ~25% energy. Firing again before it recharges overheats. So you pace shots and carry a sidearm for the gap.
- A non-Cyborg player who finds the gun in a chest gets the same 8-second recharge but **no self-damage** on overheat — the gun just will not fire until it is ready.

This is the single biggest skill gap between "Cyborg is OP" and "Cyborg killed me": manage the heat.

## Is the Cyborg Worth 600 Diamonds?

Reframe the purchase. You are not buying "a gun" — you are buying a **package**:

- the **Laser Cannon** (the gun),
- **Alien Armor** (strong defense that regenerates alien-tech energy when you take hits),
- **night vision** (a real exploration and combat advantage after dark),
- and the class itself, which levels into energy-restore perks.

**Yes, if:**
- you want the Cannon *reliably* instead of gambling UFOs and Alien Chests;
- you raid the Cultist Stronghold or fight grouped enemies a lot — the AoE deletes packs;
- you want a tanky frontline that sustains itself.

**No, if:**
- you would rather roll the dice on UFO/Alien Chest drops, or you prefer a different class. At 600 diamonds the Cyborg is the **most expensive class in the game**.
- You can get ranged AoE without it: the **Tactical Shotgun** (Ruby Chest in the Stronghold) or the **Infernal Crossbow** (Infernal Chest — infinite ammo *and* a burning effect) both fill a similar role.

**How to afford it:** 600 diamonds is a grind. The repeatable core is the Cultist Stronghold loop — see [How to Get Diamonds Fast](/en/items/how-to-get-diamonds-fast) for the farming route. And whatever you do, do not spam the Cannon: the overheat damage penalty can finish you at low HP.

## Where Sources Disagree

**Energy per shot.** Two structured guides say ~75% of the bar; two others say ~60%. We used 75% as the majority reading — but your HUD is the source of truth, and the difference only changes how long your downtime is.

**Cyborg damage bonus.** Most sources quote ~87.5 (a clean ×1.25). One cites ~77 at tier 2. The base 70 is undisputed across every source we checked.

**Ignore the "farm 600 diamonds by hitting strongholds 120 times" advice.** That figure comes from a low-quality SEO post with no corroboration. Use the diamond guide above instead.

## FAQ

**How do you get the Laser Cannon in 99 Nights in the Forest?**
Buy the Cyborg class for 600 diamonds (guaranteed starter), or find it as a random drop in Broken UFOs, the Mothership, or Alien Chests.

**What damage does it do?**
70 per hit at base, about 87.5 if you are a Cyborg (a 25% alien-tech bonus). The blast hits multiple enemies.

**Is it better than the Tactical Shotgun?**
Different jobs. The Cannon wins at range and against groups (AoE, infinite ammo); the Shotgun wins up close. Both sit in the S-tier for ranged.

**Does it need ammo?**
No. It runs on Alien Tech energy — infinite, with an ~8-second recharge after each shot.

**Is the Cyborg worth 600 diamonds just for this gun?**
Not "just" the gun — you also get Alien Armor, night vision, and the class. If you want the Cannon without RNG, it is the only reliable buy. See "Is the Cyborg Worth 600 Diamonds" above.
