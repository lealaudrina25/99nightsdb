It fires 8 pellets per shot but uses only one shell, so it one-shots wolves and shreds cultist hordes. The catch is ammo: it is rare and must be crafted. This page is the practical "how do I get one and is it worth it" — its tier ranking lives on [Best Weapons](/weapons/best-weapons).

## The Two Ways to Get a Tactical Shotgun

| # | Route | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Ruby Chest** | The reliable route. Ruby Chests are the rarest chests and show up most in the **Cultist Stronghold** reward room. A Ruby Chest downgrades to a Gold Chest once opened, so be first. |
| 2 | **Workshop crafting recipe** | The Tactical Shotgun can appear as a random crafting recipe at the Workshop. Luck-dependent, but check it each run. |

The Cultist Stronghold is the realistic farm — it resets about every 20 minutes and is the single most repeatable Ruby Chest source in the game. See [Cultist Stronghold](/locations/cultist-stronghold) for how to run it. When you pick one up, it always comes loaded with **43 shotgun ammo** (3 in the tube, 40 in reserve). The Ruby Chest is the rarest tier short of the Diamond Chest - see [Chest Tiers](/items/chest-tiers).

## What It Actually Does

The numbers below are consistent across the dedicated entity wiki, deltiasgaming, breezewiki and rosenberryrooms, all checked 2026-09-30.

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Pellets per shot | 8 (uses only 1 shell) |
| Damage per pellet | 10 (20 headshot) |
| Max close-range damage | ~150–160 per shot |
| Magazine | 3 shells |
| Reload | 5 seconds |
| Firerate | 2 seconds |
| Range | Close only — damage falls off fast with distance |

The headline is **point-blank damage**: at close range it puts out up to ~150 per shot and one-shots wolves, rabbits and frogs. The downside is the 3-shell magazine and 5-second reload, so you always want a sidearm (a [Revolver](/weapons/how-to-get-revolver) or [Rifle](/weapons/how-to-get-rifle)) ready while it reloads.

Everything on this page hangs off one number, the price of a shell, which our [Ammo and Guns](/guides/ammo-guide) guide works out in full.


Shotgun ammo is the real limiter, not the gun:

- **Ammo Crate** — buy shells with [Scrap](/items/how-to-get-scrap). One box costs 20 to craft.
- **Ammo Furnace** — if you have an Oil Drill, this makes ammo. [Biofuel](/items/how-to-get-biofuel) also feeds the furnace, so a biofuel setup is a backdoor to free shells.
- **The pickup trick** — each shotgun you find comes with a fresh 43 ammo. If you find a second one in a Ruby Chest, drop your empty one and grab the new — instant 43 more. Unreliable, but it works.

## When the Shotgun Beats the Rifle

| Situation | Better pick |
|---|---|
| Crowds (Cultist Stronghold, night raids) | **Shotgun** — 8 pellets clear a pack |
| Single big target at range (Bear, Alpha Wolf) | **Rifle** — longer reach, less reload penalty |
| Close-quarters defense of camp | **Shotgun** — burst damage |

Both are S-tier. Most players carry the Shotgun for crowds and the Rifle for bruisers — see [Best Weapons](/weapons/best-weapons) for the full ranking.
