The **Kunai** is the odd one out among 99 Nights in the Forest's ranged weapons: it **never reloads**. There is no magazine, no reload animation, no ammo type to loot — you just throw. That single trait is why it is worth understanding, and it is also why the two questions people actually ask are *"where do I get one"* and *"wait, don't I already have these?"*

The closest Hard Mode equivalent is the Corrupted Thrown Axe: same never-reloads behaviour, a faster-filling meter, and a detonation somewhere between 80 and 90 percent. See [Hard Mode](/guides/hard-mode).

The short answer to the second question is yes. **The Kunai is the Assassin's throwing knife, and the Assassin already starts with 120 of them.** Most of the confusion comes from the game using two names for the same item: it is called **"Throwing Knives"** in the class menu and **"Kunai"** in your inventory.

## The Naming Trap: the Kunai *Is* the Throwing Knives

This is the single most useful thing to know about the Kunai, because it is where most guides — including a couple of aggregators — go wrong.

The item has **two names for one thing**:

- The **class menu** lists the Assassin's starter ranged weapon as **"Throwing Knives"**.
- The moment you are in a run, the same item shows up in your **inventory as "Kunai"**.

So when you buy the Assassin ([500 Diamonds](/classes/all-classes)) and spawn with "120 Throwing Knives", **you have 120 Kunai**. There is no separate unlock, no extra purchase, and no second version to chase. Several wikis spell this out directly, and it is the reason a lot of players who think they have "never found a Kunai" have actually been carrying a stack of them since the first minute of every Assassin run.

**Why we are flagging this.** One weapons table we checked lists the Kunai as *"Ruby Chests or a 25-Diamond Assassin class purchase"*. That "25-Diamond purchase" does not survive contact with the rest of the sources — every dedicated page says the class simply **starts** with the knives, and the "25" almost certainly came from a different sentence: the far more common claim that a chest drop gives you **25 more throwing knives**. We treat the 25-Diamond buy as a misread rather than a real mechanic.

Thirty-five damage per throw with no reload is the Assassin's entire damage identity in one number. Placed against every enemy on this site, it is in our [weapon damage and time to kill guide](/guides/weapon-damage-and-time-to-kill).

## What It Does

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| **Damage** | **35** per throw |
| **Fire rate** | Fast — described as around the Raygun's rate |
| **Reload** | **None** — no magazine, no reload animation |
| **Ammo** | A consumable stack; empty the stack and the item leaves your inventory |

Two properties make the Kunai genuinely different from every other ranged weapon:

1. **It never reloads.** Every gun in the game — [Revolver](/weapons/how-to-get-revolver), Rifle, Shotgun, even the alien tech weapons — stops to reload or recharge. The Kunai does not. There is no downtime window, which makes it excellent for *poking* a target while you reposition.
2. **It is consumable.** This is the trade for having no reload: instead of a magazine you refill, you carry a **stack of knives**. When the stack hits zero, the Kunai **disappears from your inventory** — it is the only weapon in the game that works this way. In practice that means a Kunai is a resource you spend, not a permanent pickup, and keeping a stack topped up matters.

**On the damage number.** Specialised pages put the Kunai at a flat **35**, and several sources instead describe it as *"damage comparable to the Revolver"*. Those are not really in conflict — the Revolver does **25** on a body shot but **37.5** on a headshot, so the Kunai sits right in that band. Treat 35 as the working figure.

## Where to Get It

### Route 1 — The Assassin Class (guaranteed)

Buy the **Assassin** class and you begin every run with **1 [Katana](/weapons/how-to-get-katana) and 120 Throwing Knives (Kunai)**. That is the only way to be *guaranteed* a stack from the first second of a run, and it comes bundled with the class's mobility kit.

If you already own the class, you never need this page again — your problem is not *getting* a Kunai, it is *not running out*.

### Route 2 — Chests (restocking)

The Kunai also drops from **Ruby Chests**, which is how a non-Assassin player can ever hold one.

Some Chests hand out seeds; the complete seed list is on our [Seed Packs](/items/seed-packs) page.
If you *are* running the Assassin, chests become your supply line:

- **5% chance** for any chest to spawn a Kunai at **class Level 1**, rising to **10% at Level 3**.
- A chest Kunai comes with **25 more throwing knives**, which is exactly the "25" that the misread above tripped over.
- The class's **5-star talent** adds a further **10%**, which stacked on the rest is why experienced Assassins describe finding a Kunai in roughly **one chest in four**.

**Not a source:** there is no crafting recipe, no trader, and no enemy that drops it. If you are not buying the class and not opening chests, the Kunai does not exist for you.

## How to Actually Play With It

The Kunai is a **utility weapon, not a damage carry**. At 35 a throw it is nowhere near the 70-damage melee ceiling, so treat it as a tool for the moments a gun would leave you standing still:

- **Open the fight at range, then close.** With the Assassin's reduced health pool, every hit you avoid is worth more than the damage you deal — the Kunai is how you soften a target before you commit.
- **Harass, don't duel.** Its fast, reload-free cadence is built to *chip* a target: poke a [Bear](/entities/the-bear) while you back off, or finish a runner that would otherwise escape into the trees.
- **Aim high.** Headshots are worth more, and the Kunai rewards them more than a slow weapon does because you throw far more of them.
- **Farm it where it works.** Sources specifically recommend it for **Cultist Stronghold** and **Ice Temple** raiding, where ranged pokes let you clear guards from behind cover. - **Watch the stack, not the health bar.** The failure mode is not *"it broke"* — it is *"I threw my last one at nothing"*. Re-stock from chests as you go, especially on Assassin runs.

One synergy note from the class itself: the Assassin's **Level 2 crit on the first hit** pairs best with a **high-damage opener** like the [Rifle](/weapons/how-to-get-rifle) or [Laser Cannon](/weapons/how-to-get-laser-cannon), not with the Kunai. The knives cover the *gaps* between those shots.

The Frozen Shuriken is the same consumable-stack idea in a cold package, and our [Ammo and Guns](/guides/ammo-guide) guide lists every ranged weapon that never touches a bullet.


The Kunai has a biome twin you should know about before you go looking for it in the cold. The **Frozen Shuriken** is the **Snow Biome** throwable: same throwable slot, but it drops from **Ice Chests** in the **Polar Bear Cave** and the [Ice Temple](/locations/ice-temple) (the same chests our [Ice Sword](/weapons/how-to-get-ice-sword) page covers) and it **applies Frost** — a slow — on hit.

So the two throwables split cleanly:

| | Kunai | Frozen Shuriken |
|---|---|---|
| Where | Ruby Chests / Assassin class | Ice Chests (Polar Bear Cave, Ice Temple) |
| Damage | 35 | Not well documented |
| Effect | None — raw damage | **Frost** (slow) |
| Reload | None | None |

If you want a throwable for its *effect* rather than its damage, the Frozen Shuriken is the one to look for in the [Snow Biome](/locations/snow-biome).

The class is bought for mobility as much as knives, and our [Sprint Speed](/guides/sprint-speed) guide ranks the sprint it carries.

## Is the Assassin Worth It for the Kunai Alone?

No — and it is worth being blunt about that. **You do not buy the Assassin to get a Kunai.** You buy it for the **mobility kit** and the Kunai comes along: the class has one of the fastest sprint speeds in the game (**+10%**), and it pays for that with a **smaller health pool (down ~15%)**.

That trade is the whole point. If you want a **cheap, reliable ranged weapon**, the [Revolver](/weapons/how-to-get-revolver) is free from the Armory and the Pelt Trader; the Kunai's value is *speed and no reload downtime*, which only matters if you are playing the fast, fragile style the Assassin is built for. The full cost-and-benefit table is on [All Classes](/classes/all-classes), the class ranking is on [Best Class](/classes/best-class), and the perks by level — including the crit and the damage-less-kill requirements — are on the [Assassin class page](/classes/assassin-class).

Bottom line: **if you run Assassin, learn to use the knives; if you do not, the Kunai is not a reason to spend 500 Diamonds** — see [How to Get Diamonds Fast](/items/how-to-get-diamonds-fast) for what that actually costs you in playtime.

## FAQ

**Is the Kunai free?**
**Yes, if you own the Assassin.** The class starts with **120 of them** — they are just labelled "Throwing Knives" in the class menu and "Kunai" in your inventory.

**How do I get a Kunai without the Assassin?**
**Ruby Chests.** You cannot craft it, buy it from a trader, or loot it from an enemy.

**What is its damage?**
**35** per throw. Some sources describe it as "comparable to the Revolver", which does 25 body / 37.5 headshot.

**Does it run out of ammo?**
It runs out of **knives**. There is no reload, but the stack is finite, and the Kunai disappears from your inventory once it is empty — restock from chests.

**Is it full-auto or does it reload?**
It does **not** reload at all — that is its defining feature and the only ranged weapon that works this way.

**How do I find more of them as the Assassin?**
Any chest has a **5%** chance at class Level 1 and **10%** at Level 3 to spawn a Kunai with **25 extra knives**; the class's 5-star talent pushes that further.

**Is it the same as the Throwing Knives I spawn with?**
**Yes.** Same item, two names.

**What is the snow version?**
The **Frozen Shuriken**, from Ice Chests in the Polar Bear Cave and Ice Temple — it applies Frost.
