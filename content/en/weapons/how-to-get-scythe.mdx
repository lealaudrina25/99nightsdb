There are **two different weapons called "the Scythe"** in 99 Nights in the Forest, and almost every guide blurs them together. That single confusion is why people who follow a tutorial to the letter still end up without the weapon they wanted.

This page separates them cleanly: the **event Scythe** (60 damage, from the Halloween Hedge Maze) and the **Vampire Scythe** (45 damage, locked to the Vampire class). Only one of the two is reliably available outside an event — and it is not the one most guides lead with.

Last verified: **2026-09-30**.

## Quick Answer

| | **Scythe** (event) | **Vampire Scythe** (class) |
|---|---|---|
| **Damage** | **60** base (78 Brawler) | **45** base (58.5 Brawler) |
| **Swing rate** | 0.7 seconds | 0.7 seconds |
| **Effect** | **Lifesteal** on hits | **Blood bar** — fills, then heals ~30% HP |
| **Where** | Hedge Maze chest (Halloween), Thanksgiving Table | **Vampire class only** |
| **Available now?** | **Seasonal** — event-gated | **Yes** — buy the class |
| **Craftable?** | No | No |

**The one-line version:** if you want a scythe *today*, buy the **Vampire class (600 Diamonds)** and you spawn with the Vampire Scythe. The 60-damage Scythe is a Halloween-event prize and is not something you can farm right now.

## There Are Two Scythes — Here Is the Difference

Both are two-handed melee weapons with a long, curved blade and a 0.7-second swing. Past that they are different items with different owners:

- The **Scythe** is the **event weapon**. It was added with the **Halloween Hedge Maze** (the item's own date field reads **1 November 2025**), it hits hardest of the two, and it drops from a chest you can only reach during that event.
- The **Vampire Scythe** is the **class weapon**. It is the starter tool of the **Vampire class**, which is permanent content — so this is the one most players can actually get.

Sources are not always careful about which one they mean, and one of them even attributes the **Vampire Scythe** to the Hedge Maze chest. We go through that disagreement below, because it changes where you should look.

## The Scythe (60 Damage, Event)

The higher-damage of the two, and a genuine endgame melee weapon.

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| **Damage** | 60 base; 78 with Brawler scaling |
| **Swing rate** | 0.7 seconds |
| **Effect** | **Lifesteal** — it heals you as you land hits |
| **Kill power** | Drops a rabbit in one hit |

Two sources describe the lifesteal slightly differently — one says it heals you **on every successful strike**, another says the heal comes **after enough hits accumulate**. Either way the effect is real and it is the reason the weapon is rated so highly: on a long run against waves of enemies, healing as you fight is worth more than a small damage edge.

**How to get it:**

1. **The Hedge Maze (Halloween event).** Play the **Carnival mini-games** — basketball, ring toss and the shooting game — and earn **one Ticket from each of the three**, for three in total.
2. Spend the three Tickets to **enter the Haunted / Hedge Maze**.
3. Follow the **red arrows** to the centre. Watch out: the [Deer](/entities/the-deer) can spawn inside the maze, so carry a **Flashlight** to scare it off.
4. Open the **big centre chest** ("Vampire Chest") for **Candy, Coins and the event Scythe**.

One practical warning that belongs here: the maze is **Halloween content**. If the event is not running, the maze — and therefore this route — is not there. Do not start a run planning around it.

**Seasonal alternatives.** The same Scythe is reported at the **Thanksgiving Table** (November), and one source adds a **Valentine's event** route. All three are event windows, not standing content.

## The Vampire Scythe (45 Damage, Class)

The permanent one — and the one you can actually go get.

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| **Damage** | 45 base; 58.5 with Brawler scaling |
| **Swing rate** | 0.7 seconds |
| **Reach** | Long — spear-class |
| **Effect** | A **blood bar** that fills as you fight; when it tops out, it is consumed and heals roughly **30% of your health** (50% once the Vampire class hits Level 3) |

**The blood bar is the whole point.** Unlike the event Scythe, which heals a little on every hit, the Vampire Scythe **banks** its healing: you keep landing hits, the meter fills, and then it pays out in one big heal. That makes it a burst-survival tool rather than a steady trickle — better for getting out of a bad fight in one piece, worse for topping yourself up continuously.

Sources describe it as the **more accessible, slightly weaker cousin** of the event Scythe, sharing the Vampire Cloak's blood meter. It also has a famously blunt in-game description: *"just a bouncing blade on a stick"*.

**How to get it — one route:** **buy the Vampire class (600 Diamonds)** and you **spawn with a Vampire Scythe and a Vampire Cloak**. The weapon **cannot be dropped or traded**, so the class is genuinely the only door. See [All Classes](/classes/all-classes) for the full cost table.

## The Disagreement: What Does the Maze Chest Actually Drop?

This is worth spelling out, because it is the trap in most scythe guides.

**Most sources say the Hedge Maze's centre chest gives the event Scythe (60 damage):**

- 99-nights-in-the-forest.com lists the Scythe's only source as the **Hedge Maze Chest**, "awarded after successfully completing the Hedge Maze during the Halloween event".
- Deltia's Gaming's update breakdown says the centre chest holds "event-exclusive items like the **Scythe**".
- rblxguide routes the Scythe through the **Vampire Chest** for completing the Hedge Maze.
- BloxSwaps lists the Scythe from the Hedge Maze chest and the Thanksgiving Table, and — importantly — lists the **Vampire Scythe as "Vampire class only, cannot be dropped"**.

**One source disagrees:** Sportskeeda's guide says the chest at the maze's centre gives the **Vampire Scythe**. That is the odd one out — every other source ties the Vampire Scythe to the class and the maze chest to the event Scythe.

**What to do with that:** if you are chasing the **Vampire Scythe**, do not rely on the maze — buy the class, which is the route the sources agree on. If a future Halloween event returns, open the maze chest for the **event Scythe**.

## The Vampire Class, In Full

Since the class is the only route to the Vampire Scythe, here is what 600 Diamonds actually buys:

- **Starter tools:** **Vampire Scythe + Vampire Cloak.**
- **Level 1:** **lifesteal on any melee weapon**, and you **run faster at night**.
- **Level 2:** improved **night vision** (requires healing 450 HP via lifesteal and dealing 5,000 damage).
- **Level 3:** **increased lifesteal healing** (requires healing 1,000 HP and dealing 15,000 damage).

Note what Level 1 really gives you: the lifesteal applies to **any melee weapon**, not just the scythe. So the class is not "the scythe class" so much as "the sustain class" — the scythe is the weapon it hands you to start, and its night-time bonuses make it a very different pick from the [Katana](/weapons/how-to-get-katana)'s pure mobility. ## Is the Scythe Better Than the Morningstar or Infernal Sword?

Two comparisons that come up constantly:

- **vs the [Morningstar](/weapons/how-to-get-morningstar):** one source puts the event Scythe's damage "similar to the Morningstar", and the published figures support it — **60 vs 70** is close, the swings are **0.7s vs 1.0s**, and both have a combat gimmick (lifesteal versus the explosion bar). The Morningstar hits harder per swing; the Scythe swings faster and heals.
- **vs the [Infernal Sword](/weapons/how-to-get-infernal-sword):** a guide calls the **Vampire Scythe** "slightly stronger than the Infernal Sword", which lines up with 45 damage against the sword's ~40–50 range — but the sword's edge is **burning**, not healing. Pick by whether you want damage over time or survivability.

## A Note on the Numbers

One aggregator lists the Scythe at **"6,078" damage**. That is not a real figure — it is the same formatting bug we have flagged before, where the base and Brawler values get run together: **60 and 78**. It shows up identically on the Ice Sword ("3,545" = 35 / 45.5) and the Katana ("2,532" = 25 / 32.5). Ignore the concatenated number; the real value is **60 (78 Brawler)**.

## FAQ

**Is there more than one scythe?**
Yes — the **event Scythe (60 damage)** and the **Vampire Scythe (45 damage)**. They are different weapons.

**Which one can I get right now?**
The **Vampire Scythe**, via the Vampire class. The event Scythe needs a Halloween (or Thanksgiving) event to be running.

**How do I get the event Scythe?**
Clear the **Hedge Maze** during the event and open the **centre chest**. You need **three Tickets** from the three Carnival mini-games to get in.

**How do I get the Vampire Scythe?**
Buy the **Vampire class — 600 Diamonds** — and you spawn with it. It cannot be dropped or traded.

**Can I craft either one?**
No. Neither has a recipe.

**Does the scythe heal me?**
Yes, both do — but differently. The **event Scythe** heals as you land hits; the **Vampire Scythe** fills a **blood bar** and then heals ~**30%** of your health in one go (50% at Vampire Level 3).

**Is the Scythe stronger than the Morningstar?**
Close. The Morningstar does **70** per swing; the Scythe does **60** but swings faster (0.7s vs 1.0s) and heals.

**What does the Vampire class give me besides the scythe?**
A Vampire Cloak, **lifesteal on any melee weapon**, faster running at night, and improved night vision at higher levels.
