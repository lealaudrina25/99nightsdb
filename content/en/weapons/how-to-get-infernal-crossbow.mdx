The **Infernal Crossbow** is the best ranged weapon in 99 Nights in the Forest that does not ask you to find ammo. It hits for **55 plus burning**, it has **infinite reserve ammo**, and any animal it kills drops **already cooked**. That combination is why it sits in the S tier on our [Best Weapons](/weapons/best-weapons) page — and why the fastest way to own one is not to farm for it at all, but to buy the **Fire Bandit** class for **200 Diamonds**, which starts you with one.

This page covers what it does, all four routes to it, and whether it is actually better than the plain **Crossbow** you can pick up in the same biome.

Last verified: **2026-09-30**.

## What It Does

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| **Damage** | **55** direct, plus a **burning** effect |
| **Ammo** | Infinite reserve — **no bolts to collect** |
| **Magazine** | 1 bolt; about a **3-second** reload |
| **Effect** | **Burning** damage over time |
| **Bonus** | Animals you kill drop **cooked** food |
| **Trajectory** | Arrows **arc downwards** over distance |

Three things make it more than "the Crossbow but stronger":

1. **Infinite ammo means no supply line.** Every other gun in the game — [Revolver](/weapons/how-to-get-revolver), Rifle, Shotgun — needs its own ammo type, and running dry mid-fight is how runs end. The Infernal Crossbow never runs out. You can burn a whole night firing and open the next day with the same weapon.
2. **It cooks for you.** Land the killing blow on an animal and the meat drops cooked, exactly like the [Infernal Sword](/weapons/how-to-get-infernal-sword) and the [Flamethrower](/weapons/how-to-get-flamethrower). Over a long run that is a genuine food source, not a gimmick — no campfire trip needed.
3. **The burn keeps working after you stop.** The 55 direct hit is only part of the story; the target keeps taking damage while it burns, which is what makes it strong against bigger animals that take several bolts.

**On the burn number, sources differ** — one puts the burn at **10 damage per second**, another describes **20 additional burn damage**, and a third says a full hit can reach **up to ~80 damage** once the burn ticks land. Treat "**55 plus a burn**" as the reliable part; the exact tick is not settled.

One mechanic worth knowing: sources say the Infernal Crossbow **does not replace the plain Crossbow** in your inventory, so you can carry both and dual-wield them. And the arrows **arc**, so you have to aim above distant targets rather than straight at them.

## The Four Routes

### Route 1 — The Fire Bandit Class (easiest)

Buy the **Fire Bandit** class for **200 Diamonds** and you **start every run with an Infernal Crossbow and a Wildfire**. This is by far the simplest route: no biome run, no ritual, no RNG. If all you want is the weapon, this is the answer, and it is one of the cheapest classes in the game.

The class is built around fire, so the crossbow is not a throwaway — it is the core of the kit. See [All Classes](/classes/all-classes) for the full class table.

### Route 2 — The Volcano Ritual (free)

Inside the **Volcano** in the [Volcanic Biome](/locations/volcanic-biome) there is a sacrifice pit beneath the star-symbol bridge. Throw offerings — cultist corpses, loot, even downed players — into the magma, and the altar pays out **every 8 charges** in a fixed, repeating order:

| Charges | Reward |
|---|---|
| 8 | A **Gem of the Forest** fragment |
| 16 | **3× Cultist Gems** |
| 24 | **Infernal Crossbow** *or* **Infernal Sword** |
| 32 | Back to a Gem fragment — the cycle repeats |

So the crossbow is the **third** payout, at roughly **24 charges** — not eight. And it is a coin flip against the sword, which shares the same slot. Bring **Sacrifice Totems** and large cultist bodies (both worth **2 charges**) instead of farming 24 individual cultists. The full charge-value breakdown is on [How to Get the Infernal Sword](/weapons/how-to-get-infernal-sword).

### Route 3 — Infernal Chests

The biome's top-tier chests — the **Red Spiked / Infernal Chests** — can contain the crossbow, alongside the Infernal Sword and Infernal Sack. Only a couple spawn per map (the Volcanic Church roof and the Ammo Furnace are the documented ones), so treat this as a bonus, not a farm. Chest locations are on [Volcanic Biome](/locations/volcanic-biome).

### Route 4 — The Cultist King

The boss drops **either the Infernal Crossbow or the Infernal Sword per kill, never both** — so it is a 50/50, and he costs four Cultist Gems to summon. Worth it only if you are already going for his **Antler** (which the [Infernal Helmet](/items/how-to-get-infernal-helmet) needs). Full tactics on [The Cultist King](/entities/the-cultist-king).

## Infernal Crossbow vs Crossbow

Both live in the same biome and neither uses ammo — so the choice is real:

| | Crossbow | Infernal Crossbow |
|---|---|---|
| **Damage** | ~**30–40** | **55** plus burning |
| **Ammo** | Infinite reserve | Infinite reserve |
| **Magazine** | 1 bolt | 1 bolt |
| **Reload** | ~3 seconds | Slightly **faster** than the Crossbow |
| **Effect** | None | **Burning**; cooks meat on kill |
| **Source** | Volcanic **Hot Chests**, carried by cultists | Infernal Chests, Fire Bandit, ritual, Cultist King |

**The crossbow is the strictly better one, and you do not have to choose.** Because it doesn't replace the base Crossbow, the strongest setup is both equipped — the plain Crossbow stays useful while the Infernal version reloads. If you only carry one, carry the Infernal.

## Is It Better Than the Infernal Sword?

They share almost every source, but they are not competitors — they cover **different ranges**:

- **Infernal Sword** — melee, ~40–50+ per hit, the strongest fire melee weapon. You have to close the distance.
- **Infernal Crossbow** — ranged, 55 plus burn, and **infinite ammo**. You fight from where it is safe.

If you are choosing which to chase, the crossbow is usually the more useful half: ranged fire is what keeps you alive in the Volcanic Biome, and the infinite ammo removes the one thing that makes range unreliable. The sword's edge is raw per-hit damage and the melee slot it fills.

## The Fire Bandit Class (If You Go That Route)

If the crossbow is your goal, the class is the shortcut — and it is a genuinely strong pick in its own right:

- **Cost:** **200 Diamonds**.
- **Starter tools:** **1 Infernal Crossbow + 1 Wildfire** (a thrown fire explosive).
- **Level 2:** your other weapons gain a chance to **set enemies on fire**, so the burn build works with any gun you pick up.
- **Level 3:** a **10% chance** for chests to drop a bonus **Wildfire**.
- **Level-up goals:** kill enemies with fire, and set enemies on fire — so you level the class just by playing the way it wants you to.

The one catch: Wildfire is **limited and rare**, so it is not a spammable weapon. And per the same sources, **bunny kills do not count** toward its level requirements — the same quirk the [Assassin](/weapons/how-to-get-katana) has.

## FAQ

**Can I craft the Infernal Crossbow?**
No. It cannot be crafted at any bench.

**Does it use ammo?**
No source of ammo is needed — its reserve is **infinite**. You still reload after each bolt.

**How much damage does it do?**
**55** direct plus a **burn**. The burn tick is reported differently across guides (10/sec vs 20 total), so treat "55 + burn" as the figure.

**Can I get it without going to the Volcanic Biome?**
Yes — buy the **Fire Bandit class (200 Diamonds)** and you spawn with one.

**Is it better than the normal Crossbow?**
Yes — higher damage, burning, and a slightly faster reload, with the same infinite ammo. And it does **not replace** the Crossbow, so you can carry both.

**Does it cook food?**
Yes. Animals it kills drop **cooked** meat, no campfire needed.

**What is the downside?**
The arrows **arc downwards**, so distance needs aim, and the ~**3-second** reload leaves you open — pair it with a faster weapon like a [Revolver](/weapons/how-to-get-revolver).

**Was there a secret upgrade?**
One source says the developers mentioned an Infernal Crossbow upgrade **nobody has found yet**. That is unconfirmed trivia — we mention it, we do not repeat it as fact.
