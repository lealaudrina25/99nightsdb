## The Only Route: The Assassin Class

The Katana was the **first class-exclusive weapon** in the game, and it has never left that status. Equip the Assassin before a run and you begin with **one Katana and 120 Throwing Knives**.

That is the entire obtainment story. Every source we checked says the same thing in different words: **no chest, no crafting recipe, no event drop, no trader**. If you are not running the Assassin, the Katana does not exist for you.

Two things come bundled with that purchase, and they are not optional extras — they are the cost side of the trade:

- **Price: 500 Diamonds.** Diamonds and Gems are the same currency here; see [How to Get Diamonds Fast](/items/how-to-get-diamonds-fast) for how long that actually takes to save.
- **Reduced maximum health.** The Assassin trades HP for mobility. Our own [Best Class](/classes/best-class) page describes it the same way: "faster sprint at the cost of some HP."

This matters more than it sounds. **You cannot evaluate the Katana as a weapon in isolation**, because you can never hold one without also being down some health. That lower HP pool is why the throwables in the same loadout are not flavour — they are how you fight without taking hits.

The full class comparison, including the Assassin's crit and sprint bonuses, is on [All Classes](/classes/all-classes).

## Katana vs Spear: Settling the Reskin Argument

Community opinion splits on this, so here is what the numbers actually say.

| Weapon | Damage | Swing rate | Base DPS | How you get it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Katana** | 25 | 0.45s | **~55.6** | Assassin class only (500 Diamonds) |
| **Spear** | 25 | 0.50s | ~50 | Common, Good and Iron Chests; Pelt Trader second trade |

**Same damage per hit, different cadence.** The Katana swings about 0.05 seconds faster, which works out to roughly 11% more damage per second. On paper that makes "it is just a reskin" wrong.

In practice, be honest about what that 11% buys you. One published melee tier list records **identical hits-to-kill** for both — Rabbit in 2, Wolf in 4 — which is what you would expect from identical per-hit damage against small health pools. The tier list ranks the Katana **A-tier** and calls it "functionally identical to the Spear but with style points."

So the fair verdict is:

- **Not a pure reskin.** The swing rate difference is real and measured, and it does produce a distinct DPS profile.
- **But not a meaningful upgrade either.** You are not paying for a better weapon; you are paying for the Assassin's mobility kit, and the Katana is what comes with it.

If you want the Spear instead, it is free — chests and the Pelt Trader's second rung both produce it, and the Pelt Trader arrives on **Day 2**. Our [How to Get the Rifle](/weapons/how-to-get-rifle) page shows the full trade ladder the Spear sits on.

## How to Fight With It

The Katana's advantage is reach plus rhythm, not damage. Three habits get that out of it:

1. **Swing at maximum reach, then step out** during the enemy's response and re-enter. Standing still and trading blows wastes the reach that makes this weapon safe.
2. **Do not kite Bears the way you kite Wolves.** A Bear's attack reach is long enough to connect anyway; the hit-and-step rhythm that works on wolves will get you hit.
3. **Use the throwables.** With a reduced health pool, closing the distance unnecessarily is the expensive mistake. The 120 Throwing Knives let you open at range first.

One clarification worth making, because a lot of sources blur it: the **Kunai** *is* the Throwing Knives you spawn with. It is the same item under two names — the class menu calls it "Throwing Knives", your inventory calls it "Kunai". So the 120 you start with **are** 120 Kunai, and each deals **35** damage. There is no separate purchase to chase; see [How to Get the Kunai](/weapons/how-to-get-kunai).

## Is the Assassin Worth It?

Only you can answer that, but the decision is clearer when you put it in these terms:

**Take it if** you want mobility and a scouting playstyle, you are comfortable fighting around a smaller health pool, and you want both a melee and a throwing option from the first minute of a run.

**Skip it if** you want the weapon without the class. At 500 Diamonds you are buying a playstyle, and there are cheaper classes that hand you a usable weapon — see the class-by-class cost table on [All Classes](/classes/all-classes).

Note that on our [Best Weapons](/weapons/best-weapons) ranking the Katana does **not** appear in the consensus S-tier. It is a strong starter, not an endgame weapon: the S-tier melee options (Morningstar, [Obsidiron Hammer](/weapons/how-to-get-obsidiron-hammer), Infernal Sword, Ice Sword) all sit well above 25 damage per hit. Treat the Katana as a very good day-one weapon that you will eventually replace.

## FAQ

**Can I get the Katana without the Assassin class?**
No. It is class-exclusive with no alternative route.

**Can I craft it?**
No recipe exists.

**Does it drop from chests?**
No chest tier contains it — not Gold, not Ruby, not biome chests.

**Is it better than the Spear?**
Same 25 damage per hit, ~11% faster swing rate, so slightly higher DPS. Hits-to-kill against rabbits and wolves are reported as identical.

**What else does the Assassin give me?**
Faster sprint, crit hits, and 120 Throwing Knives — plus reduced maximum health as the tradeoff. See [All Classes](/classes/all-classes).

**Is the Kunai included?**
Yes — the Kunai *is* the Throwing Knives you spawn with (same item, two names). You start with 120 of them, at 35 damage each, and can restock from Ruby Chests. See [How to Get the Kunai](/weapons/how-to-get-kunai).
