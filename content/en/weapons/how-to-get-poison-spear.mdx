The **Poison Spear** is the melee weapon that comes out of the Frog Invasion content — a spear that hits exactly as hard as the basic Spear but **poisons whatever it touches**. It is the prize from one of the game's more involved side-bosses, the **Frog King**, and the drop is not generous.

This page covers the numbers, the full Frog King route (keys, whirlpool, cave, boss), the drop rates, and the detail most guides skip: the Poison Spear is a *strict upgrade* to the Spear you already have.

Last verified: **2026-09-30**.

## Quick Answer

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Where | **Frog King's reward chest** (the "Frog Chest") — and fishing the frog pond in his cave |
| Drop rate | **~3%** — the rarest single item in the frog pool |
| Damage | **25** base (32.5 as [Brawler](/classes/brawler-class), 40 with a fully boosted Berserker) |
| Swing rate | **0.5 seconds** — identical to the basic Spear |
| Special | **Poisons enemies on hit** — they keep taking damage after the strike |
| Added | **22 August 2025**, the Frog Invasion update |
| Best class | Brawler (32.5), with the poison stacked on top |
| Worth it? | Yes if you are clearing the Frog King anyway — it is a free upgrade to any Spear |

## What It Does

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| **Damage** | 25 base; 32.5 with Brawler; 40 with a fully boosted Berserker |
| **Swing rate** | 0.5 seconds (~50 damage per second on the base figure) |
| **Effect** | **Poison on hit** — enemies keep losing health after you stop swinging |
| **Reach** | Spear-class — long enough to jab and step back |

25 damage is **modest**, and it is the same 25 the ordinary Spear deals. What you are buying is the **poison rider**: every hit keeps working after the hit. That is what earns it a place in the S-tier conversation on [Best Weapons](/weapons/best-weapons) despite the small per-hit number — in a game where the strong melee weapons all make you stand still for a long swing, a weapon that keeps dealing damage *while you back off* is genuinely useful against groups.

### Poison Spear vs Spear

The comparison is short, because the two weapons are the same weapon plus one effect:

| | Spear | Poison Spear |
|---|---|---|
| Damage | 25 | 25 |
| Swing rate | 0.5s | 0.5s |
| Effect | None | **Poison on hit** |
| Source | Craftable; chests; Pelt Trader | **Frog King** (~3%) |

**The Poison Spear is a strict upgrade** — same damage, same speed, plus a damage-over-time effect. The only cost is how hard it is to get. The basic Spear is craftable early and the Pelt Trader offers it on his second rung, while the Poison Spear is a low-percentage boss drop. Taking the Spear while a Poison Spear or a Shadow Dagger is held behaves in opposite ways — see [The Pelt Trader](/items/pelt-trader). If you already fight with a Spear, this is the upgrade path.

## How to Get It — The Frog King Route

Five separate guides describe the same chain, which makes this one of the cleaner side-bosses to document. The whole thing is gated behind **eight keys**:

**1. Collect 8 Frog Keys.** Each key sits in a different **pond** across the forest, as a **glowing object in the water**. One pond, one key. The catch is not the finding — it is that frog species patrol the pond edges, so secure the glowing item fast or clear the area first.

**2. Find the giant whirlpool.** With all eight keys, go to the **giant whirlpool** on the map and jump in. It pulls you down into the [Frog Cave](/locations/frog-cave), which that page maps out — chamber, gates and what waits inside.

**3. Open the locked door.** Inside the cave is a locked door that needs **all 8 keys** placed at once. If you are even one short, the mechanism will not trigger — check your count before diving, or you have wasted the run.

**4. Defeat the [Frog King](/entities/the-frog-king).** Behind the door is the **Frog King** boss arena. The chest cannot be opened until he is dead, so plan to finish the fight in the same trip.

**5. Open the reward chest.** His chest holds the Frog Invasion loot — and the Poison Spear is the **rarest** item in it.

### The Drop Table

Sources give the same four figures, and they are all low:

| Item | Drop rate |
|---|---|
| **Poison Spear** | **3%** |
| Poison Armor | 1% |
| Blowdart | 1% |
| Frog Boots | 1% |

**Read that top number carefully.** At 3%, the Poison Spear is roughly a 1-in-33 clear of the Frog King. Budget for repeated runs, and treat any guide that describes it as a guaranteed drop as wrong.

### The Second Route: Fishing the Cave Pond

There is a slower alternative that does not need the boss chest: **fish the frog pond inside the Frog Cave**. One source (Gamezebo's frog-secrets writeup) reports the pond uses the **same drop pool and the same rates** (Poison Spear 3%, Poison Armor 1%, Blowdart 1%, Frog Boots 1%), which is how you fill in a piece the chest roll did not give you.

Two other guides list "fishing" or "the Frog Pond" as a Poison Spear source without publishing rates, which corroborates the **location** even if the exact pond rates rest on one source. The practical version: clear the Frog King, then fish his pond while you are already down there.

### Other Reported Sources

Two one-off mentions are worth knowing but not relying on:

- **Jungle Chest** — listed by 99-nights-in-the-forest.com as a third route. The four-temple loop that opens it is on [Jungle Biome](/locations/jungle-biome).
- **gold Presents** — rblxguide lists the Poison Spear inside gold Presents, which reads as a **seasonal gift** rather than a permanent source.

Both come from a single guide each, so treat them as "maybe" rather than a farm plan.

The fishable version of this loot pool is the detail most guides leave out, and it is the one that gives you a second chance after the chest has already rolled. Our [**fishing guide**](/guides/fishing-guide) has the full table.

## The Rest of the Frog Set

The Poison Spear is not the only reason to fight the Frog King — the same chest feeds a small **poison-themed kit**:

- **Poison Armor** (1%) — the poison-thorns piece: anything that melee-attacks you gets poisoned (sources split on whether it also reduces damage). Full breakdown on [How to Get the Poison Armour](/items/how-to-get-poison-armour).
- **Blowdart / Blowpipe** (1%) — a **poisoned ranged** option. Some guides place it in the Frog Cave and the **Poison Master** class, which is the ranged half of the poison build.
- **Frog Boots** (1%) — the utility drop from the same pool.

The **Poison Master class (200 Diamonds)** is the themed match for all of this: the [class list](/classes/all-classes) describes it as "strong poison damage and spread". Note the distinction, though — the Poison Spear is a **boss drop**, not a class starter. Buying the class does not hand you one; you still have to clear the Frog King for it.

## FAQ

**Can I craft the Poison Spear?**
No recipe exists. It is a drop from the Frog King's chest, or a rare catch from fishing his pond.

**Is it guaranteed from the Frog King?**
No. It is the rarest item in the chest at about **3%**, so expect several clears.

**Where do I get the Frog Keys?**
Eight of them, one per pond across the forest, each a glowing object in the water. You need all eight to open the cave door.

**How do I enter the Frog Cave?**
Jump into the **giant whirlpool** on the map with all eight keys in hand. See [Frog Cave](/locations/frog-cave) for the pond that hosts the whirlpool, the two gates on the door, and what the chamber looks like once you are down.

**Is it better than the Spear?**
Yes — identical 25 damage and 0.5-second swing, plus poison on hit. Same weapon, one extra effect.

**Does the Poison Master class give me one?**
No. The class is poison-themed, but the Spear is a Frog King drop, not a class item.

**What else drops from the Frog King?**
Poison Armor (1%), Blowdart (1%) and Frog Boots (1%), alongside the spear.
