The **Rifle** is the mid-game ranged weapon most players graduate to after the [Revolver](/weapons/how-to-get-revolver). It one-shots a [Wolf](/entities/the-wolf) and is the cheapest reliable answer to a [Bear](/entities/the-bear) short of a Laser Cannon. This page is the practical "where do I get one" — damage numbers and how it ranks live on [Best Weapons](/weapons/best-weapons).

Last verified: **2026-09-30**.

The third trade sits two rungs above a [Bunny](/entities/the-bunny) foot, and our Bunny page farms the bottom of that chain.

## The Four Ways to Get a Rifle

There is no single source for the Rifle. Across the published guides the same four routes show up every time.

| # | Route | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Gunsmith Shed** | A workstation you can build or find that assembles the Rifle. The most reliable "craft it yourself" path. |
| 2 | **Iron Chest** | A mid-tier chest (needs campfire Level 4, like every Rare chest). |
| 3 | **Legendary Chest** | A top-tier chest; lower odds than the Iron Chest but it also drops the high-end gear. |
| 4 | **Pelt Trader — third trade** | He arrives on **Day 2** and works through four pelts: Bunny Foot → Wolf Pelt → Alpha Wolf Pelt → Bear Pelt. The Rifle is the **third** rung — the Alpha Wolf Pelt — so you must bring him two earlier pelts first. |

The Pelt Trader route is covered in more detail on [Best Weapons](/weapons/best-weapons) and the flute/XP loop that gates it is on [Taming Guide](/taming/taming-guide). The full four-pelt ladder, every conditional reward and the trades that can cost you a pelt are on [The Pelt Trader](/items/pelt-trader). If you want the Rifle fast without grinding, the Gunsmith Shed or an Iron Chest is the realistic target. The Iron Chest is one of seven tiers on [Chest Tiers](/items/chest-tiers).

Some Chests hand out seeds; the complete seed list is on our [Seed Packs](/items/seed-packs) page.
Sixty damage with a five-second reload is a weapons system with a ceiling far below its peak. The sustained figure sits well under the weakest melee, and the arithmetic is in our [weapon damage and time to kill guide](/guides/weapon-damage-and-time-to-kill).

## What the Rifle Actually Does

This is where sources stop agreeing, so read the table, not a single number.

| Source | Damage | Magazine / reload |
|---|---|---|
| Dedicated entity wiki (99-nights-in-the-forest.com) | 50 (headshot 75) | 1 round, ~5 s reload |
| Best Weapons (this site, single source) | 60–90 | 6 per reload |
| namu.wiki | ~80 (some builds 50) | not stated |
| bloxswaps | 60 (headshot 90) | not stated |

**Treat the exact damage and magazine size as version-dependent.** Patches move both numbers, and no two guides publish the same figure. What is stable is the *role*: it is a high-damage, slow-firing rifle that beats everything up to a Bear.

For scale, enemy health is roughly **Wolf 75 and Bear 350** (and the cave [Brute Cultist](/entities/the-brute-cultist) sits above the bear) — (and the cave [Brute Cultist](/entities/the-brute-cultist) sits above the bear) — (and the cave [Brute Cultist](/entities/the-brute-cultist) sits above the bear) — (see [Best Weapons](/weapons/best-weapons)). That is why the Rifle one-shots a wolf and takes four to six shots to drop a bear — and why it is the go-to for the bear caves on [Missing Kids Locations](/locations/missing-kids).

Ammunition for both is documented as coming out of the water, which is why the cheapest angling class pairs with a gun instead of competing with it. [**Fisherman Class**](/classes/fisherman-class) covers the supply side.

The two guns differ more in their reload than in their damage, and our [Ammo and Guns](/guides/ammo-guide) guide collects both reload timings in one table.


| | Revolver | Rifle |
|---|---|---|
| Damage | 25–37 | 50–90 (per-source range) |
| Fire rate | Fast, 6-round cylinder | Slow, ~1 shot |
| Best for | Wolves, cultists at close range | Bears, anything you would rather not touch |
| How you get it | Ranger class (70 Diamonds), Pelt Trader, chests | Gunsmith Shed, Iron/Legendary Chest, Pelt Trader third trade |

If you are choosing one, the [Revolver](/weapons/how-to-get-revolver) is the early pick and the Rifle is the upgrade. Most players carry both: Revolver for the swarm, Rifle for the bruiser.

Melee weapons carry their own caveats, and our [Scythe](/weapons/how-to-get-scythe) page covers the one whose damage changes depending on which copy you mean.

## Sources

The four acquisition routes (Gunsmith Shed, Iron Chest, Legendary Chest, Pelt Trader third trade) appear consistently across rblxguide, 99-nights-in-the-forest.com, namu.wiki and bloxswaps, all checked 2026-09-30. Damage figures are split as shown above; none are corroborated across more than one or two sources, so confirm in your own build.
