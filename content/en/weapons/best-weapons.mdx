Ask five sites for a weapons tier list and you get five different answers. So this page does something slightly different: it shows you **where the lists agree**, and it flags the one source that actually publishes damage numbers instead of vibes.

Last verified: **2026-10-02**. Weapons and chest tables shift with patches, so treat this as a map, not scripture.

## The Consensus Top Tier

These are the weapons that rank S-tier across **three or more** independent tier lists. Everything below this line is where the lists start arguing.

| Weapon | Type | Where it comes from | Why it ranks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Laser Cannon | Ranged, AoE | UFO crash sites, Alien Chests; Cyborg class starts with one | Huge area damage per shot; the answer to alien waves |
| Tactical Shotgun | Ranged | Ruby Chest, inside the Cultist Stronghold | Widely called the best gun in the game |
| [Infernal Crossbow](/weapons/how-to-get-infernal-crossbow) | Ranged | Infernal Chests (Volcanic Biome), [Fire Bandit class](/classes/fire-bandit-class) | Infinite ammo plus burn damage over time |
| Admin Axe | Melee | **Update Party events only** | One-shots almost everything — but you may never see one |
| Cultist King Mace | Melee | **Disputed** — weapon lists say the boss drops it; others say admin-only | Highest melee damage in the game |

**A warning on the Admin Axe:** it is event-exclusive. Every list puts it at the top and almost nobody has one. Do not plan a build around it.

**A bigger warning on the Cultist King Mace:** whether it drops at all is genuinely contested. Most "all weapons" lists say the Cultist King drops it, but the fandom wiki states it is admin-only and unobtainable, and Sportskeeda addresses the rumour directly to deny it. **We treat it as not obtainable** — do not farm that boss for it. Full breakdown of both sides on [The Cultist King](/entities/the-cultist-king).

Just below, ranked S by two lists each: **Morningstar, Obsidiron Hammer, Infernal Sword, Ice Sword, [Poison Spear](/weapons/how-to-get-poison-spear), [Flamethrower](/weapons/how-to-get-flamethrower)**.

## The Only Damage Numbers Anyone Has Published

Most tier lists give no figures at all. One source does, so here it is — with the caveat that it is **a single source** and the rest of the internet does not corroborate the exact values.

| Weapon | Damage | Source |
|---|---|---|
| [Laser Sword](/weapons/how-to-get-laser-sword) | 25 base, 30 at full charge | Broken UFOs, [Mothership](/locations/the-alien-mothership), Alien Chests |
| Laser Cannon | 70–87 (AoE) | UFOs, Alien Chests, Cyborg class |
| Rifle | 60–90, 6 per reload | Iron/Legendary Chests, Gunsmith Shed, Pelt Trader |
| [Morningstar](/weapons/how-to-get-morningstar) | 70, 1s swing | Gold Chests |
| [Obsidiron Hammer](/weapons/how-to-get-obsidiron-hammer) | 70, 1s swing | Obsidiron Chest (Meteor event) |
| [Ice Sword](/weapons/how-to-get-ice-sword) | 35 (45.5 Brawler), **56 boosted Berserker — a reported figure, not a ×1.6 rule**, see [Berserker](/classes/berserker-class), 0.5s swing | Ice Chests — Polar Bear Cave, [Ice Temple](/locations/ice-temple) |
| Revolver | 25–37 | Common/Good/Iron Chests, Armory |
| [Raygun](/weapons/how-to-get-raygun) | 10 (15 headshot) | Alien class (100D) / UFOs / Alien Chests |

Two things worth reading out of that table:

**The Morningstar and Obsidiron Hammer are statistically identical** — same 70 damage, same swing speed, and both fill an explosion bar that detonates for area damage when it tops out. The only difference is how you get them.

**A warning about aggregator damage numbers.** Several weapon databases publish absurd figures like a Laser Sword at 2,530,232 or a Katana at 2,532. These are **formatting bugs**, not balance — the stat fields are being run together without separators (25 + 30 + 2 + 32 = 2530232). If a damage number has more than three digits, the page is broken. We show the corrected reading on the [Laser Sword](/weapons/how-to-get-laser-sword) page.

**For scale, enemy health is roughly Wolf 75 and Bear 350.** That is why a Rifle one-shots a wolf and takes four to six shots on a bear — the same reason the bear caves on [Missing Kids Locations](/locations/missing-kids) feel like a wall.

## How You Actually Get Weapons

**1. Pick a class that starts with one.** The fastest route to a usable weapon is the class menu, not a chest.

- **Assassin** (500 Diamonds) — the [Katana](/weapons/how-to-get-katana) plus 120 [Kunai](/weapons/how-to-get-kunai) (the class menu calls them "Throwing Knives"), so both ranges are covered from minute one.
- **Ranger** (70 Diamonds) — Revolver plus 18 ammo.
- **Lumberjack** (70 Diamonds) — Good Axe: a weapon and a tool.
- **Pyromaniac** — Flamethrower, which is S-tier crowd control from the start.
- **Cyborg** (600 Diamonds) — Laser Cannon, skipping the alien grind entirely.
- **Vampire** (600 Diamonds) — a [Vampire Scythe](/weapons/how-to-get-scythe) and a Vampire Cloak, plus lifesteal on any melee weapon you pick up.

**2. Trade with the Pelt Trader.** He shows up on **Day 2** of every run and works through four pelts, one per trade: **Bunny Foot → Wolf Pelt → Alpha Wolf Pelt → Bear Pelt**. The rewards climb with them — Good Axe and Good Sack at the first rung, Spear or Revolver at the second, a Rifle at the third, and the top tier at the fourth (**Strong Axe, [Iron Body](/items/how-to-get-iron-body) or Strong Flashlight**). He is limited, so on a team decide who gets what before he arrives. The rewards he is actually offering, conditionals included, are on [The Pelt Trader](/items/pelt-trader).

**3. Open chests, in the right order.** Regular and Good chests hold basics. **Rare chests need campfire Level 4.** Gold Chests sit near the Cultist Stronghold and hold the mid-to-high tier. Ruby Chests are the top. Biome chests are thematic: Ice Chests in the snow, Infernal in the volcanic zone. The other tiers, and what each of them rolls, are on [Chest Tiers](/items/chest-tiers).

**4. Run the Cultist Stronghold.** It resets roughly every 20 minutes and is the single most repeatable source of Gold and Ruby Chests in the game.

**5. Special sources.** [Trident](/weapons/how-to-get-trident) comes from fishing (the Bone Lake is the best spot). Alien weapons come from UFO crash sites. The **Chainsaw** is crafted at the Workshop from 4 Cultist Gems, 4 Bear Pelts, 2 Alpha Wolf Pelts and 10 Scrap — see [How to Get Wood Fast](/items/how-to-get-wood-fast), because it is a tool before it is a weapon.

## The Early-Game Ladder

What to carry when, if you are not buying a weapon class:

- **Day 1–2:** whatever your class gave you, plus a **Good Axe** from the Pelt Trader or a chest. A Revolver from a Common Chest can save your first wolf encounter.
- **Mid game:** move to **Rifle** — 60–90 damage, and one-shots wolves.
- **Late game:** push for **Morningstar** or **Tactical Shotgun**, and add an elemental weapon for whichever biome you are farming.

## Hard Mode: Corrupted Weapons

Hard Mode has its own variants — **Corrupted Axe, Corrupted Shotgun, Corrupted Revolver and Corrupted Throwing Axe** — from the Corrupted Tools update of 31 January 2026. They carry boosted stats and are considered the meta for pushing past night 99, but players report drawbacks with sustained use. Try one before you commit a loadout to it.

## What to Actually Carry

The most consistent advice across every source is **combined arms**: one melee, one ranged, one utility.

- **Melee** for wolves and cultists that close distance.
- **Ranged** for bears and anything you would rather not touch — and this is what you use on the caves.
- **Utility:** something with an elemental or crowd-control effect, or a no-ammo option like the Crossbow or Blowpipe so you always have a fallback.

And the cheapest upgrade of all: **stop fighting things you can recruit.** Several of the animals on this list are also pets — see [Taming Guide](/taming/taming-guide).
