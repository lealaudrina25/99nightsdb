The **Revolver** is the most reliable early ranged gun in 99 Nights in the Forest: 6 rounds, quick to draw, no energy bar. It will not out-damage a Rifle or the S-tier guns, but it is the sidearm you want before you can reach them. This page covers every way to get one and where the ammo comes from. ## Four Ways to Get a Revolver

The acquisition routes below are corroborated across techwiser, deltiasgaming, fdaytalk and supercheats, all checked 2026-09-29.

**1. Ranger Class (70 Diamonds) — the cheapest guaranteed route.**
The Ranger class starts each run with a Revolver and 18 ammo. If you are willing to spend Diamonds, this is the only way to be holding a gun from minute one. See [Best Class](/classes/best-class) for the class menu.

**2. Pelt Trader (arrives Day 2).**
The Pelt Trader works through four pelts, one per trade: Bunny Foot → Wolf Pelt → Alpha Wolf Pelt → Bear Pelt. The Revolver is the **second** rung, paid for with a **Wolf Pelt** — a pelt you are already farming. He is limited per run, so on a team decide who calls the Revolver. See [The Wolf](/entities/the-wolf) for where the pelt comes from. Every reward on this rung, including the Revolver Ammo swap, is listed on [The Pelt Trader](/items/pelt-trader).

**3. Chests.**
Common and Good Chests can contain a Revolver. Rare chests (campfire Level 4+) improve the pool but are not required for a Revolver specifically.

**4. Gun Cabinets.**
Forest cabins and houses near your camp sometimes hold a Revolver (usually with ammo). Check every building you pass.

Where Revolver Ammo comes from, and what each source is actually worth, is the subject our [Ammo and Guns](/guides/ammo-guide) guide opens with.


Revolver Ammo comes **6 rounds per item**, from **Good Chests, the Ranger class, Cultists, and Ammo Crates**. It is common enough to stock, but conserve it for real fights rather than wolves you could club with an Old Axe.

Keeping it fed matters more than owning it, and one 50-Diamond class produces the rounds for free. See [**Fisherman Class**](/classes/fisherman-class).

Twenty-five per shot and a five-second reload is the early-game wall every run hits. Set against the animals you will actually meet on night one, in our [weapon damage and time to kill guide](/guides/weapon-damage-and-time-to-kill).

## Is It Worth It?

The Revolver is the dependable early-game sidearm. The only published damage figure sits around **25–37** (a single source — most tier lists rank by feel, not numbers), which drops a wolf in a couple of shots. Against [The Boar](/entities/the-boar), whose published health is the only figure any source gives, that same range works out at roughly **four shots** — see that page for how the arithmetic lands. Tier lists place it anywhere from **A to B**; either way it is the gun you carry until you can reach a **Rifle** (60–90 damage) or the S-tier **Tactical Shotgun / Laser Cannon**.

If you want the gun without spending Diamonds, the **Pelt Trader route** is the efficient play — the Wolf Pelts it costs are a byproduct of normal farming.
