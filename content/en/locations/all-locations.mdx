The map of 99 Nights in the Forest is not a fixed set of coordinates — it generates around your campfire in a **radial pattern**. A safe inner ring of forest, then dangerous outer sectors radiating outward. This page is the overview: what each biome holds, the landmarks worth travelling to, and the utility buildings that keep a run running. For the two biggest set-piece locations, see [Cultist Stronghold](/locations/cultist-stronghold) and [Missing Kids Locations](/locations/missing-kids).

Last verified: **2026-09-30**.

## How the Map Generates

Most guides describe a **concentric model**: a safe inner circle (0–500m) built around your campfire, with outer biomes splitting off in sectors. Your run does not reuse a fixed map, so the layout shifts every time — but the **categories** of place are stable.

| Ring | What is there | Danger |
|---|---|---|
| **Inner (Forest)** | Your whole early game: Campfire, Crafting Bench, Farm Plots, the first loot sheds | Low by day, high by night ([The Deer](/entities/the-deer) hunts) |
| **Outer sectors** | Snow / Volcanic / Jungle — each with its own enemy set, materials and landmarks | High; endgame content lives here |

## The Biomes

**Forest (always).** Your start biome. Wolves, Alpha Wolves, the occasional Bear, plus [The Deer](/entities/the-deer) and [The Owl](/entities/the-owl) at night. Almost every early structure (sheds, cabins, towers, the Animal Shelter) spawns here.

**Snow.** Cold-pressure biome. Home to [The Owl](/entities/the-owl), Polar Bears, [Arctic Foxes](/entities/the-arctic-fox), and the **Ice Temple** (a reliable Cultist Gem route and the Ice Bow line). Also holds the **Owl's Nest**. You need [warm clothing](/items/warm-clothing) to stay long.

**Volcanic.** Endgame biome: [**The Ram**](/entities/the-ram) at night (it replaces The Deer here, like The Owl replaces it in Snow — the charge, the stun rules and what it never drops are all on its own page), [Scorpions](/entities/the-scorpion), lava terrain, and the **Cultist King Palace**. Lava ignites you and destroys dropped items permanently. Full breakdown on [Volcanic Biome](/locations/volcanic-biome).

**Jungle.** Dense hostile camps, [Boars](/entities/the-boar), Frogs, and temple loot — plus [**The Cat**](/entities/the-cat) at night, which follows you in here from the Forest but only exists on runs that have a Jungle. Listed by several location guides, but see the disagreement note below — not every source treats it as a per-run biome. The full route — the campfire gate, the four temples, and what the Overlord Jungle Chest actually holds — is on [Jungle Biome](/locations/jungle-biome).

## Landmark Categories

**Loot structures (the scrap grind).** Sheds, Wooden/Metal Sheds, cabins (Medium, Large), barns, armories and towers. The **Watchtower** is the standout early find: it reliably holds **5× Old Flashlights**. These are the structures covered in [How to Get Scrap Fast](/items/how-to-get-scrap) — they are where your metal and wood come from.

**Quest locations.**
- [Cultist Stronghold](/locations/cultist-stronghold) — the repeatable Diamond source (3 floors, 20-min reset).
- [Missing Kids caves](/locations/missing-kids) — four key-gated caves, the main quest.
- **[Ice Temple](/locations/ice-temple)** (Snow) — Cultist Gems and the Ice Bow route. Two floors, and a Frozen Beam in the middle that holds the gems.
- **Cultist King Palace** (Volcanic) — spend four Cultist Gems.
- **Owl's Nest** (Snow) — feather deposits since the 22 August 2026 Biome Refresher.
- **[Frog Cave](/locations/frog-cave)** — the [Frog King](/entities/the-frog-king) encounter, reached through the whirlpool pond near your camp; home of the [Poison Spear](/weapons/how-to-get-poison-spear) and the [Poison Armour](/items/how-to-get-poison-armour), and the **Beastmaster** badge (event-linked).
- **[The Caverns](/locations/the-caverns)** — **four** caves; clear one and a green gem shows you two statues (Deer, Owl, Ram or Bat) to pick from, granting a **server-wide blessing**. The main cave is on the map on **Day 1**; the other three need **TNT** from inside it. Home of the unkillable [Bat](/entities/the-bat) and the three cave-only cultists.
- **Jungle Temples / Mother Temple** (Jungle) — four pyramid temples; gather **4 Crystal Skull Keys** from their altars and place them on the central Mother Temple to open its treasure room (the Overlord Jungle Chest: Poison Claws, [Poison Armour](/items/how-to-get-poison-armour), Blowpipe). Temple by temple, with what fires when you take a key and how the resets work, on [Jungle Biome](/locations/jungle-biome).
- **Command Ship / Mothership** — the alien event raid; repair the crashed Command Ship near your campfire with **5 Alien Batteries**, then ride the beam up to the [Alien Mothership](/locations/the-alien-mothership) (event-linked).

**Utility buildings.**
- **[Pelt Trader](/items/pelt-trader)** — arrives on Day 2; each pelt is one trade, and the second rung (a Wolf Pelt) buys a **Spear or Revolver**.
- **[Tool Workshop](/locations/tool-workshop)** — the three-floor stone castle that holds the Anvil: four top-tier recipes, one craft at a time with no cancel, and the biome rule that decides which helmet your run can make. Its anvil map icon only appears once you have discovered the building on foot. Do not mix it up with the **Tool Trader** merchant inside the castle or your camp's **Crafting Bench**.
- **Abandoned Animal Shelter** (Domestication Cabin) — where you get Taming Flutes; see [Taming Guide](/taming/taming-guide).
- **Fishing Hut / Ponds** — rods and fish (Mackerel, Salmon) for Green Frog food.
- **Market / Grocery Store** — carrots, apples, occasional seeds and healing items.
- **Farm Plots** — passive crops (Carrot, Corn, Pumpkin, Chili, Cake) for taming food; see [Pet Food](/taming/pet-food).
- **Sawmill** — converts logs into Planks.

**Your base.** The campfire area is the only true safe zone — [The Deer](/entities/the-deer) will not enter its radius while the fire burns, and most bench upgrades live here. Keep 10+ logs stocked before nightfall; the campfire itself is covered in [How to Get Wood Fast](/items/how-to-get-wood-fast).

## Where Sources Disagree

**How many outer biomes per run.** rblxguide states each run gets **one** outer biome — a 50/50 Snow *or* Volcanic roll, never both. Several other location guides also list a **Jungle** sector. The safest reading: the exact outer-biome set is version-dependent, so check your own map borders early and plan around whatever you got.

**Fixed vs radial.** A few older write-ups describe fixed coordinates; the current consensus is radial generation around the campfire. Either way, the *categories* above are what matter for farming.

**Structure loot tables.** The fandom wiki lists exact item counts per shed/cabin; those numbers are not independently corroborated and shift with distance from the campfire (loot scales the further out you go). Treat specific counts as approximate.
