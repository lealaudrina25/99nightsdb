# Crafting Bench in 99 Nights in the Forest

Every run in 99 Nights in the Forest starts the same way: a campfire, a pile of logs, and one station next to it where the whole build tree lives. Upgrade it five times and it takes you from a 3-Wood Map to machines that skip a night. Ignore it, and you are still crafting Old Beds on Day 30.

This page is the build tree in full: the tier ladder, every recipe the bench owns and how many of each material it eats, what each tier unlocks, what the whole ladder costs in total, and the naming problem that makes half the guides on the internet sound like they are describing five different buildings when they are partly describing one.

## Quick Answer

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| What is it | The **Crafting Bench** — the station beside your campfire where you spend **Wood**, **Scrap**, and increasingly **Cultist Gems** and **Gems of the Forest** |
| How many tiers | **Five.** Each upgrade unlocks a new recipe tier and nothing unlocks out of order |
| Total ladder cost | **100 Wood + 81 Scrap + 2 Cultist Gems + 1 Gem of the Forest** (our addition of the four rungs) |
| First craft, always | The **Map** — 3 Wood, Tier 1, and it can be opened anywhere |
| What it is not | It has no anvil, it does not craft the four Tool Workshop items, and it is not where the Taming Flute is upgraded |
| Biggest gate | **Tier 5** needs a **Gem of the Forest**, which is a late-run material, not a grindable one |

Naming is the sourcing problem on that page; [Kiwi](/entities/the-kiwi) has four names for one tree trunk, and ours counts them instead of picking a winner.

## Where It Is, and the Name Five Sources Cannot Agree On

**Where.** The fandom wiki puts it "to the east of the Campfire right next to it." bloxboom says you "find the Crafting Bench right behind your campfire." Both agree on the one thing that matters, that it sits in your base and never leaves — the two are describing the same corner of the camp from different camera angles. Do not go looking for it out in the forest.

**What to call it.** This is the part that makes search frustrating, so it is worth laying out plainly. The same station goes by at least five names in published material:

| Name used | Who uses it | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Crafting Bench** | bloxboom, beebom, and most recipe databases | The name the build tree is actually indexed under |
| **Grinder** | the fandom wiki | The same station: "Crafting works by putting certain materials into the Grinder located to the east of the Campfire" |
| **Upgrade Station** | several taming write-ups | Used for the *flute* upgrade step, which is a different interaction |
| **Skills Building** | PC Gamer, Digital Tech Explorer | A **structure** name, and it is where they place the flute upgrade |
| **Upgrade Building** | Stealthy Gaming | Another structure name, marked on the map by a **hammer icon** |

Two things follow from that table, and both matter:

1. **The Crafting Bench and the Tool Workshop are not the same building.** The Tool Workshop is the three-floor stone castle with the Anvil — four top-tier recipes, one craft at a time, an anvil map icon. It has its own page. Every recipe in this article is a bench recipe; none of them need a trip to the workshop. See the [Tool Workshop](/locations/tool-workshop) page for the other half of the crafting system.
2. **The flute upgrade is not a bench recipe, and we are not going to pretend the sources say it is.** The taming guides put it at a structure called the Skills Building, the Upgrade Building, or the Workshop with a crossed-tools icon; PC Gamer and Digital Tech Explorer both note it "typically unlocks around Campfire Level 3" and sometimes closer to 4. Our own [Taming Guide](/taming/taming-guide) sends you there by the Upgrade Station name. What none of the recipe databases do is list a flute upgrade recipe. **If you are looking for the flute route, the [Taming Guide](/taming/taming-guide) is the page** — and if you are looking for the recipes, you are in the right one.

One corroboration worth having: the Tool Trader, the merchant inside that same cluster, is described by Escore News as selling an Old Taming Flute "for 20 coins" from the tool station. That is a separate transaction from the upgrade, and it is the trade our [Pelt Trader](/items/pelt-trader) page ends on.

Tier 4 is also the Crafting badge, one of the cheapest entries in the table on our [All Badges](/guides/all-badges) page.

The last tier is where the Respawn Capsule lives, competing with two other machines for the same Gem of the Forest. What it does and what it costs to keep running is on the [**death and respawn**](/guides/death-and-respawn) page.

Tier 4 is where the Ammo Crate appears, and the cost per round our [Ammo and Guns](/guides/ammo-guide) guide publishes is why that tier matters to gun runs.


Select a recipe, hand over the materials, and it is instant — there is no craft timer at this station. Many items "can be placed down more than once," so the tier tables are a menu, not a one-off shopping list. A few items carry a **use counter**: the fandom wiki notes that items show a white circle with a number inside, which is how many uses they have left before they die. Farm Plots give 20 carrots. Bear Traps reset 10 times.

### Tier 1 — the only three items you start with

| Item | Cost | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Map** | **3 Wood** | Shows full map info, including chests and structures. Can be opened anywhere, not just at the bench |
| **Old Bed** | **20 Wood** | Day counter increases by +1 |
| **Bunny Trap** | **5 Wood** | Place near a bunny burrow to catch food; a carrot bait can yield 4 morsels |

The Map is the single best-value craft in the game and every source we checked lists it first. Spend 3 Wood on it on Day 1.

### Tier 2 — the camp starts defending itself

| Item | Cost | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sun Dial** | **5 Scrap** (fandom) / **5 Wood** (bloxboom) | Shows how long is left until day or night |
| **Regular Bed** | **5 Scrap** | Day counter +1 |
| **Compass** | **3 Scrap** | Shows which direction you are facing |
| **Freezer** | **4 Scrap** | Stores food cleanly. Listed by the fandom wiki and beebom only |
| **Farm Plot** | **10 Wood** | Grows carrots automatically every 2 days. Max 20 per player |
| **Wood Rain Storage** | **5 Scrap** | Keeps stored wood dry in rain. Place more than once |
| **Shelf** | **2 Scrap** | Storage. Place more than once |
| **Log Wall** | **12 Wood** | Fortifies your base |
| **Bear Trap** | **3 Scrap** | Ground trap dealing damage. Resets 10 times, then dies |

- **The Tier 3 item people forget.** [Chef and Cook](/classes/chef-class) — the Crock Pot is not the only station behind this tier; unlock the Chef and a second one opens at the same time.

### Tier 3 — the utility tier, and where most runs actually stall

| Item | Cost | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Crock Pot** | **15 Wood + 10 Scrap** | Combines food into better food; berries and peppers are rejected |
| **Radar** | **15 Wood + 10 Scrap** | Scans for important places. Each scan costs 15 Scrap |
| **Boost Pad** | **15 Wood + 10 Scrap** | Get out of base faster. Unlocked at the bench by the [Ice Temple](/locations/ice-temple) Boost Pad Recipe Tablet |
| **Biofuel Processor** | **12 Wood + 12 Scrap** | Turns logs and meat into better fuel; pelts work too |
| **Torch** | **6 Wood + 6 Scrap** | Expands your safe zone by a small amount |
| **Good Bed** | **10 Wood + 10 Scrap** | Day counter +1 |
| **Lightning Rod** | **8 Scrap** | Attracts lightning so it stops hitting players. Place more than once |

### Tier 4 — the gem wall begins

| Item | Cost | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ammo Crate** | **20 Wood + 30 Scrap + 1 Cultist Gem** | Trades Scrap for ammo |
| **Oil Drill** | **25 Wood + 35 Scrap + 1 Cultist Gem** | Drills for oil periodically |
| **Giant Bed** | **20 Wood + 30 Scrap + 1 Cultist Gem** | Day counter +1 |
| **Teleporter** | **15 Wood + 25 Scrap + 1 Cultist Gem** | Teleport anywhere; **needs 2+ to work** |

Four Cultist Gems per rung is what turns Tier 4 into a Cultist grind rather than a wood grind.

### Tier 5 — the endgame machines, all on one Gem

| Item | Cost | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Respawn Capsule** | **40 Wood + 40 Scrap + 1 Gem of the Forest** | Respawns anyone nearby once charged |
| **Temporal Accelerometer** | **40 Wood + 40 Scrap + 1 Gem of the Forest** | Skips a day/night cycle once charged |
| **Weather Machine** | **40 Wood + 40 Scrap + 1 Gem of the Forest** | No rain or thunder for 3 days once charged |

All three sit behind the same single Gem of the Forest, so the tier is not a wall you grind past — it is a material you have to have earned elsewhere first. Where that Gem comes from is covered on the [How to Get Diamonds Fast](/items/how-to-get-diamonds-fast) page.

Hard Mode hands you a fourth and it is the most useful of the three. The Research Monitor blueprint drops from the outpost chests once corruption starts, and [Hard Mode](/guides/hard-mode) explains what it prints and why there is no percentage on screen.

The bench and the campfire are the same budget in two different currencies. [Campfire](/guides/campfire) is the other half of it: six levels, the fuel each one costs, and the badges at the end.

Beds sit at the front of that budget in a [speedrun in 99 Nights in the Forest](/guides/speedrun-guide), because they pay for themselves every night.

Bench tiers and fire levels together decide which of the game's six keys you can use, the case our [Keys and Locked Doors](/guides/keys-and-locked-doors) guide makes in the last section.

How a run actually finishes is a question almost nobody answers up front. The true ending needs every missing child back in camp before Day 99 resolves, and [how to win 99 Nights in the Forest](/guides/how-to-win-99-nights) takes the win condition out of the campfire and ammo advice it usually gets buried under.

One thing this route does not answer: what tier 5 costs. The **Gem of the Forest** is that gate, and more sources lead to it than any single page lists. The [Gem of the Forest](/items/gem-of-the-forest) page collects them.

The [Pumpkin](/items/pumpkin) page is the reference for this one, including why the old advice to look for pumpkins under trees stopped working after the Halloween wild-spawn nerf.

The Bench ladder is priced in wood and gems, but running what it builds costs Steak, and our [Steak](/items/steak) page is the food side of that budget.

A Farm Plot costs 10 Wood and returns a crop every two days, and a Bunny Trap eats one [Carrot](/items/carrot) to hand back four Morsels.

## The Upgrade Ladder as a Budget

Four rungs, and this is the arithmetic the recipe tables bury:

| Rung | Wood | Scrap | Other |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 → 2 | 5 | 1 | — |
| 2 → 3 | 15 | 10 | — |
| 3 → 4 | 30 | 20 | **2 Cultist Gems** |
| 4 → 5 | 50 | 50 | **1 Gem of the Forest** |
| **Total** | **100** | **81** | **2 Cultist Gems + 1 Gem of the Forest** |

That total is our addition of the four rungs. The per-rung numbers come from the sources below, and the Wood, Scrap and Gem columns are corroborated by three or more of them. The reason it is worth adding up: **81 Scrap on the ladder alone**, before you buy a single Bear Trap or Lightning Rod. Scrap that goes into rungs 2 and 3 does not go into your defenses, and the ordering argument below is entirely about that.

The same ladder, with its sourcing notes and the Blacksmith shortcut, is documented from the scrap side on the [How to Get Scrap Fast](/items/how-to-get-scrap) page. This page is the menu; that page is the money.

The order a speedrun defends is the same one argued there, listed again in [speedrun in 99 Nights in the Forest](/guides/speedrun-guide).

Two of the structures on that ladder belong to the food economy rather than the gear one: Farm Plots grow the only four-fork crop, and the Crock Pot is where the four-fork dishes are made. See [**Hunger**](/guides/hunger).

Our [Weather and Night Events](/guides/weather-and-night-events) guide is the one page that shows where the meteor materials in a Tier 5 bench recipe actually come from.


Stated as reasoning rather than as fact — this is our judgement built on the tables above, not a published tier list.

1. **Map — Day 1, 3 Wood.** No argument. It is Tier 1, cheap, and changes how every other step goes.
2. **Farm Plot (Tier 2) second, not the beds.** Carrots every 2 days, permanently, is the only renewable food source on this list. The Regular Bed that costs 5 Scrap and buys one day is the weakest thing you can spend Tier 2 on.
3. **Wood Rain Storage (Tier 2) before anything perishable.** Rain destroying stacked wood is the kind of loss you notice a week later.
4. **Bear Traps (Tier 2) in real numbers.** 3 Scrap each and they reset 10 times. What a trap does to each animal, and the eight-trap cap, are on our [Bear Trap](/items/bear-trap) page. They are the cheapest damage in the game and they are why a Tier 2 bench still survives a bad night.
5. **Sun Dial and Compass are comfort crafts.** The Sun Dial's cost is itself disputed (below). Nobody's run has ended because they skipped them.
6. **Tier 3 is where you pause and think.** Radar, Crock Pot, Torch and Good Bed all compete for the same 15 Wood and 10 Scrap, and Tier 4 is about to demand 2 Cultist Gems. Craft Tier 3 for the specific problem you have, not for the tier number.
7. **Do not craft Tier 5 until the Gem is already in your pack.** Rushing to Tier 4 to reach 5 does not skip the Gem, it just spends the Scrap first.

## Where Sources Disagree

| Point | What each source says | Our reading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Bench 2 → 3 Scrap** | **10** (beebom, Finding Dulcinea, and our own scrap page) vs **15** (bloxboom) | We use **10** — three sources to bloxboom's one, and it is the number the ladder total is built on |
| **Sun Dial cost** | **5 Scrap** (fandom, beebom) vs **5 Wood** (bloxboom) | Genuinely unsettled. Note that the *tier* agrees everywhere and only the material is contested |
| **Tier 2 item list** | **Freezer** appears on the fandom wiki and beebom; bloxboom's Tier 2 list omits it | Two sources, so we include it and flag it |
| **The Hammer** | beebom and bloxboom file it under the **Tool Workshop** (5 Wood + 5 Scrap); the fandom wiki's bench tables do not assign it a tier at all | We treat it as a workshop item and leave it there. Our [Tool Workshop](/locations/tool-workshop) page records the 5/5 recipe from a single source |
| **Bench 4 → 5 cost** | **50 Wood + 50 Scrap + 1 Gem of the Forest** (four sources) vs a calculator listing **40 / 40 with 3 Cultist Medallions** | We use 50/50. The calculator also names a material — Cultist Medallion — that no other source uses, which reads as an older schema |
| **Where the station is** | "East of the campfire" (fandom) vs "right behind your campfire" (bloxboom) | Cosmetic. Both place it in base |
| **How many items are unlocked at start** | The recipe tables list **three** Tier 1 items; beebom says you start with four craftable items | Minor, recorded rather than resolved |

## The Blacksmith Skips a Rung

The one class that changes this ladder is the Blacksmith. It crafts one tier higher without paying for that rung, and the rung it skips is almost always **3 → 4** — the one carrying 2 Cultist Gems. On a normal run, those two gems are the difference between "Tier 4 now" and "Tier 4 next week." What that is worth, and the point at which it stops being worth anything, is argued out in full on the [Blacksmith class](/classes/blacksmith-class) page.

## What the Bench Does Not Do

Worth stating, because the naming table above causes this confusion as often as the rest of this page combined:

- **No Anvil recipes.** The Chainsaw, Riot Shield, Tactical Shotgun, Thorn Body, and the two helmets all come from the [Tool Workshop](/locations/tool-workshop), not here.
- **No pelt trading.** That is the [Pelt Trader](/items/pelt-trader).
- **No flute upgrade.** See the [Taming Guide](/taming/taming-guide) for that route.
- **No campfire upgrade.** Feeding the fire is its own loop — see [How to Get Wood Fast](/items/how-to-get-wood-fast).

## FAQ

**Do I have to upgrade in order?**
Yes. Every source that addresses it states the bench must be upgraded sequentially; you cannot buy Tier 4 recipes from a Tier 2 bench. The one exception is the Blacksmith class, which is allowed a one-rung skip.

**Is upgrading the bench consuming?**
No, it is permanent for the run. The bench stays and keeps its tier. Recipes, by contrast, are *not* permanent — items with a use counter die when it runs out.

**What is the cheapest useful thing I can craft?**
The **Map**, at 3 Wood. It is Tier 1 and it is the reason you can plan anything else on this page.

**Why is my Tier 4 item greyed out?**
It needs **2 [Cultist Gems](/items/cultist-gem)** per rung. Gems are not a bench currency and cannot be substituted — see the [Ice Temple](/locations/ice-temple) for the most reliable source.

**Can I craft several of the same thing?**
Yes for most structures. Farm Plots, Wood Rain Storage, Shelf, Bear Trap and Lightning Rod are explicitly placeable more than once. Farm Plots cap at **20**.

**How many Farm Plots can I own?**
The fandom wiki states a maximum of **20** per player. Single-source, so treat it as likely rather than certain.
