The **Snow Biome** is the cold outer sector of the map in 99 Nights in the Forest. It is where the game's best Cultist Gem route lives, and the only place the **Mammoth** spawns. This page covers the cold mechanic, what you meet there, and why it is worth the trip. The map model it sits inside is on [All Locations](/locations/all-locations).

Last verified: **2026-09-29**.

For the rest of the ladder this sits on, see [Campfire Level Gates](/guides/campfire-level-gates): all fifty-nine campfire level gates from Level 1 to Level 6, including the disputed ones.

## Where It Sits

The map generates as concentric rings around your campfire. The inner ring (roughly 0–500m) is safe forest; beyond it the map splits into outer sectors — **Snow, [Volcanic](/locations/volcanic-biome) and Jungle** — each with its own enemy set and loot pool. The Snow Biome is the cold one. The radial model and the other rings are detailed on [All Locations](/locations/all-locations).

Warmth and fire are the same resource here: the cold is beaten by a lit campfire and the fuel that keeps it lit. The six levels and what they cost are on [Campfire](/guides/campfire).

Cold is the other drain, and the thing that separates them is that only two foods in the game produce warmth. Both are on the [**hunger**](/guides/hunger) page.

The cold is only one half of the weather system; rain and meteor storms are tracked separately in our [Weather and Night Events](/guides/weather-and-night-events).


Entering the Snow Biome brings up a **freeze (or temperature) meter** that drains over time. If it empties, you **cannot run** — dangerous if a Polar Bear is on you. Ringing the **Bell Tower** bell slows the drain for that run.

Bring warm gear, or keep a campfire fueled: [How to Get Biofuel](/items/how-to-get-biofuel) explains the rain-proof fuel that holds a fire at max intensity, which is the easy way to stay warm deep in the snow.

Three of the biggest Steak sources in the game are Snow animals, the Alpha Wolf, Polar Bear and Mammoth, and our [Steak](/items/steak) page counts them one by one.

The Owl on that list is the snow's night boss, and our [Bosses](/guides/boss-guide) guide is where the unkillable five are gathered.

## Structures and Loot

- **Ice Temple** — one of the more reliable **Cultist Gem** sources in the game (corroborated by the community biome map). Cultist Gems gate [Crafting Bench](/locations/crafting-bench) 4 and deeper progression. That gate is why a [Blacksmith](/classes/blacksmith-class) can save real time here: its tier skip reaches Bench 4 without spending those gems. See the [Ice Temple](/locations/ice-temple) page for the floor plan, what the Frozen Beam actually holds, and why the gems there do not follow the usual last-kill drop rule.
- **Owl's Nest** — takes feathers in batches and awards Snow Biome loot, including the **Ice Bow** at higher tiers.
- **Bell Tower** — ring it to slow the freeze meter.
- **Chests:** Ice Chest and Cold Chest (not guaranteed). Items reported across the wikis: [Ice Sword](/weapons/how-to-get-ice-sword), Frozen Shuriken, Snowball, Ice Bow, and the Boost Pad Recipe Tablet (from the Ice Temple). The Ice Chest's full loot table is on the [Ice Temple](/locations/ice-temple) page.

If you are mapping the late game, the gate behind this is the **Gem of the Forest** — four fragments make one gem, and tier 5 wants five of them. The [Gem of the Forest](/items/gem-of-the-forest) page is the one that says so.

The noseless snowman in this biome will take one [Carrot](/items/carrot) and hand back a Cultist Gem, which is the only place that price is written down.

You go for the [Chili](/items/chili) the Arctic Fox wants, a reason to visit that is paid in a day rather than in a fight, set out on our Chili page.

Chili only ever arrives from a Seed Pack, as our [Seed Packs](/items/seed-packs) page explains.
## Why You Go

Two reasons dominate:

1. **[Cultist Gems](/items/cultist-gem).** The [Ice Temple](/locations/ice-temple) is a steadier gem route than fighting the [Cultist Stronghold](/locations/cultist-stronghold), and gems are what unlock the later missing-kids caves (see [Missing Kids Locations](/locations/missing-kids)).
2. **Pelts.** The snow pelts — **Polar Bear Pelt** and **[Arctic Fox Pelt](/entities/the-arctic-fox)** — are for warm clothing (the Polar Bear Hat and Arctic Fox Hat), *not* the Pelt Trader. The full recipe table, the shop's location, and what the gear does — and does not — fix are on [Warm Clothing](/items/warm-clothing). The pelts that trade there are **Wolf, Alpha Wolf and Bear** pelts — see [The Pelt Trader](/items/pelt-trader) for the order they come in. See [Pet Food](/taming/pet-food) for the full list.

If you are hunting the **Mammoth** tame, this is the only biome it appears in.

## A Note on Conflicting Guides

One guide (gamermovil) describes a more elaborate "winter expansion" — Yetis, a Temperature System with hypothermia stages, Warm Hats, and a North Wind Cave entrance after night 30. The other community wikis do not describe those specifics, so treat the Yeti / Warm-Hat details as reported by a single source and verify them in your own run. The core facts above — cold outer ring, freeze meter, Polar Bear / Arctic Fox / Mammoth, Ice Temple gems, Bell Tower — are agreed across multiple wikis.
