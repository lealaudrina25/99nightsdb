The **Snow Biome** is the cold outer sector of the map in 99 Nights in the Forest. It is where the game's best Cultist Gem route lives, and the only place the **Mammoth** spawns. This page covers the cold mechanic, what you meet there, and why it is worth the trip. The map model it sits inside is on [All Locations](/locations/all-locations).

Last verified: **2026-09-29**.

## Where It Sits

The map generates as concentric rings around your campfire. The inner ring (roughly 0–500m) is safe forest; beyond it the map splits into outer sectors — **Snow, [Volcanic](/locations/volcanic-biome) and Jungle** — each with its own enemy set and loot pool. The Snow Biome is the cold one. The radial model and the other rings are detailed on [All Locations](/locations/all-locations).

## The Cold Mechanic

Entering the Snow Biome brings up a **freeze (or temperature) meter** that drains over time. If it empties, you **cannot run** — dangerous if a Polar Bear is on you. Ringing the **Bell Tower** bell slows the drain for that run.

Bring warm gear, or keep a campfire fueled: [How to Get Biofuel](/items/how-to-get-biofuel) explains the rain-proof fuel that holds a fire at max intensity, which is the easy way to stay warm deep in the snow.

## Structures and Loot

- **Ice Temple** — one of the more reliable **Cultist Gem** sources in the game (corroborated by the community biome map). Cultist Gems gate Crafting Bench 4 and deeper progression. That gate is why a [Blacksmith](/classes/blacksmith-class) can save real time here: its tier skip reaches Bench 4 without spending those gems. See the [Ice Temple](/locations/ice-temple) page for the floor plan, what the Frozen Beam actually holds, and why the gems there do not follow the usual last-kill drop rule.
- **Owl's Nest** — takes feathers in batches and awards Snow Biome loot, including the **Ice Bow** at higher tiers.
- **Bell Tower** — ring it to slow the freeze meter.
- **Chests:** Ice Chest and Cold Chest (not guaranteed). Items reported across the wikis: [Ice Sword](/weapons/how-to-get-ice-sword), Frozen Shuriken, Snowball, Ice Bow, and the Boost Pad Recipe Tablet (from the Ice Temple). The Ice Chest's full loot table is on the [Ice Temple](/locations/ice-temple) page.

## Why You Go

Two reasons dominate:

1. **Cultist Gems.** The [Ice Temple](/locations/ice-temple) is a steadier gem route than fighting the [Cultist Stronghold](/locations/cultist-stronghold), and gems are what unlock the later missing-kids caves (see [Missing Kids Locations](/locations/missing-kids)).
2. **Pelts.** The snow pelts — **Polar Bear Pelt** and **[Arctic Fox Pelt](/entities/the-arctic-fox)** — are for warm clothing (the Polar Bear Hat and Arctic Fox Hat), *not* the Pelt Trader. The full recipe table, the shop's location, and what the gear does — and does not — fix are on [Warm Clothing](/items/warm-clothing). The pelts that trade there are **Wolf, Alpha Wolf and Bear** pelts. See [Pet Food](/taming/pet-food) for the full list.

If you are hunting the **Mammoth** tame, this is the only biome it appears in.

## A Note on Conflicting Guides

One guide (gamermovil) describes a more elaborate "winter expansion" — Yetis, a Temperature System with hypothermia stages, Warm Hats, and a North Wind Cave entrance after night 30. The other community wikis do not describe those specifics, so treat the Yeti / Warm-Hat details as reported by a single source and verify them in your own run. The core facts above — cold outer ring, freeze meter, Polar Bear / Arctic Fox / Mammoth, Ice Temple gems, Bell Tower — are agreed across multiple wikis.
