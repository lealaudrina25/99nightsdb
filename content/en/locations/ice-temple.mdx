The **Ice Temple** is the Snow Biome's most rewarding structure and the least obvious one. It looks like a vault. It is a vault — but the thing that opens it is also the thing that wakes three cultists inside it.

At the centre of the ground floor sits a giant **Frozen Beam**. Break it and you get **Gem of the Forest** and **Cultist Gems** out of it; at some point during the damage you hit, the cultists encased around it **melt and turn hostile**. That single interaction is the whole location, and this page is about the parts the structure's own description does not tell you: which loot comes from where, which chests are actually guaranteed, what the second floor holds, and why the gem route here behaves differently from everywhere else in the game.

If you want the whole ladder in one place rather than one gate per page, [Campfire Level Gates](/guides/campfire-level-gates) lists all fifty-nine campfire level gates by level, and flags the ones the sources disagree about.

The Temple's roughly triple sprint speed is the strongest boost in the game, and our [Sprint Speed](/guides/sprint-speed) guide is where it is ranked.

## What It Looks Like

Outside it is a **two-storey stone structure thickly covered in ice, with spikes protruding from the walls**. Inside on the ground floor the floor is iced over, there are **two staircases on either side**, and the cultists are frozen in blocks of ice around the beam in the middle. The second floor is reached by those stairs and holds the **Ice Chest**, which spawns on **either the left or the right side** — the left staircase leads to the left side, the right staircase to the right.

That last detail is worth acting on: **go up before you start swinging at the beam.** If you break the beam first, the melt is already running and you will be splitting your attention between a wave of cultists and a chest you have not opened yet.

The temple's beam is a cold-weather structure, and the surrounding event cycle is described in our [Weather and Night Events](/guides/weather-and-night-events).


Everything about this location turns on the beam, and the important thing to understand is that **it does two jobs at once**.

1. **It holds the gems.** The **Gem of the Forest** and the **[Cultist Gems](/items/cultist-gem)** are documented as contained *within* the beam, not dropped by anything you kill.
2. **It arms the room.** As you damage it, the encased cultists melt and become hostile. Sources describe this as happening **after a certain damage milestone** — a threshold nobody has published a number for.

So the loop is three stages, not one: chip the beam until the milestone → deal with the melt → keep chipping until it breaks. **The most common mistake is trying to do the melt and the beam at the same time.** The documented advice is to stop hitting the beam, clear the melted cultists, then go back to it.

A related trick from one source: after the melt, **stand in front of the entrance** rather than deep in the room. The Frozen Beam sits in the cultists' line of sight, so the entrance is largely outside it — you get the same view of the room without being stood in the middle of it.

## Where the Loot Actually Comes From

This is the part guides collapse into one messy list, so it is worth separating:

| Loot | Container | Guaranteed? |
|---|---|---|
| **Gem of the Forest** | The Frozen Beam | No — sources say only that it is *in* the beam |
| **Cultist Gems** | The Frozen Beam | No — same phrasing, plural, no count published |
| **Ice Chest** (Ice Axe, Ice Sword, Frozen Shuriken, Fuel Canister, 1 Diamond) | Floor 2, left or right | **Sources disagree** — see below |
| **Legendary Chest** | In the temple | Marked **not guaranteed** |
| **Strong Axe**, **Strong Flashlight** | Unsettled — see below | No |
| **Boost Pad Recipe Tablet** | Encased in ice near the centre | Added with the 22 Aug 2026 Biome Refresher |

**The strong tools are the shakiest rows.** One source lists the Strong Flashlight and Strong Axe as things the temple *contains*, alongside the gems, but explicitly annotates the gems as the beam's contents and leaves the two tools unattributed. Elsewhere the **Legendary Chest** is a documented container whose table includes the **Strong Axe** — but not the Strong Flashlight. So the clean reading is: **the Strong Axe plausibly comes from the Legendary Chest, and the Strong Flashlight's home is not settled.** Do not build a run around either one.

**On the "how many gems" question:** no source we found publishes a count for the beam's contents. Gems are described in the plural and nothing more. If a guide tells you it drops a specific number, that number is invented.

## The Chests, and the Name That Does Not Exist

**A disagreement worth knowing before you go hunting.** One source states an Ice Chest **always spawns** on the temple's second floor. The temple's own item listing marks both chests in the building as **not guaranteed**. We cannot resolve those two claims from the published tables, so the safe assumption for planning is **neither chest is certain**. One of them is an Ice Chest and the other a Legendary Chest, both listed on [Chest Tiers](/items/chest-tiers).

Then there is the naming question that trips people up, including our own earlier pages. The **Ice Chest's** verified contents are consistent across every loot table we found — **Ice Axe, Ice Sword, Frozen Shuriken, Fuel Canister and one Diamond** — and the confirmed Ice Chest spawn points are two: the **Polar Bear Caves** and this floor. The **Cold Chest** is a separate, lesser Snow structure chest (Ice Axe, Leather Body, Snowball, Fuel Canister, 1 Diamond).

What no loot table we can find lists is a **"Silver Ice Chest"** or a **"Fancy Ice Chest"** — only **Ice Chest** and **Cold Chest**. We are therefore treating **Ice Chest** as the verifiable container for the Ice Sword and Frozen Shuriken, and marking the other two names as unverified. Our own [Ice Sword](/weapons/how-to-get-ice-sword) and [Best Weapons](/weapons/best-weapons) pages used the "Silver Ice Chest" wording; that wording is not corroborated, and this page supersedes it. Both chests also have to be **broken out of** with an axe before they open.

## The Boost Pad Recipe Tablet

The newest reason to clear this place, added with the **Biome Refresher on 22 August 2026**.

The **Boost Pad Recipe Tablet** is a frozen blueprint, encased in ice near the centre of the temple and freed by the same beam break. Take it back to camp and feed it to the **Grinder** beside the [Crafting Bench](/locations/crafting-bench), which permanently unlocks the **Winged Boost Pad** recipe. Using the tablet also awards the **Aerodynamics** badge.

Why it matters is the upgrade itself:

| | Boost Pad (**base**) | Winged Boost Pad |
|---|---|---|
| **How it fires** | You charge it: hold Interact for about **5 seconds** | **Automatic**, the moment you leave the campfire radius |
| **Effect** | Same speed boost as eating a **Chili**, roughly **triple** normal sprint speed | Same boost, no charging |
| **Tell-tale** | Pale red body with darker red arrows when spent | — |
| **Unlocked by** | Bench **level 3** | The Boost Pad Recipe Tablet, ground at the Grinder |

For a base that sits at the centre of a map whose outer rings kill you on a timer, a pad that fires itself on the way out is the difference between a camp and a functioning logistics point.

**Single-source details, flagged:** that the recipe needs **Crafting Bench level 3** and **15 Wood + 10 Scrap** to build, that the tablet requires **Campfire Level 4** to reach, and that the temple is sometimes called the **"Ice Palace"** rather than the Ice Temple all come from one aggregator we treat as unverified. The tablet's existence, its location, the Grinder step, the Winged behaviour and the Aerodynamics badge are corroborated by the game's item listing.

There is a third infinite route that involves neither: fishing the Algal and Bone Ponds. Our [**fishing guide**](/guides/fishing-guide) puts all three side by side.

If you are mapping the late game, the gate behind this is the **Gem of the Forest** — four fragments make one gem, and tier 5 wants five of them. The [Gem of the Forest](/items/gem-of-the-forest) page is the one that says so.

For the pumpkin side of this, where a Pumpkin grows and how rare it actually is, see [Pumpkin](/items/pumpkin); the sink side is on the pages above.

The Temple's boost pad is the effect a [Chili](/items/chili) imitates, and our Chili page prices the repeatable one against the once-per-visit one.

Chili only ever arrives from a Seed Pack, as our [Seed Packs](/items/seed-packs) page explains.
## Why the Gem Route Here Beats the Stronghold

This is the point most guides miss, and it is the one that decides whether you should bother.

Our own [Cultist](/entities/the-cultist) page documents the game's rule that **a Cultist Gem drops from the last cultist killed in the run** — which makes the [Cultist Stronghold](/locations/cultist-stronghold) a **lottery**: you can do everything right and go home empty because someone else was your last kill.

The Ice Temple's gems are different. They are **inside a container you open**, not attached to a kill. **Our reading:** a gem you pull out of a broken beam is not subject to the last-kill rule, which is exactly why the temple is described as a *steadier* route — steadier being the operative word, not *guaranteed*. You are no longer betting on who died last; you are spending a beam break.

Two honest caveats. First, we cannot confirm from any source that the game applies the last-kill rule **only** to kills — it may or may not also police what a container gives you. Second, and more practically: the **melted cultists you kill inside the temple are still kills**, so if one of them happens to be the last cultist of your run, that one can drop a gem on the normal rule. The temple does not remove the rule. It adds a source that does not depend on it.

Either way, that is the answer to "where do I get [Cultist Gems](/locations/all-locations)" that does not involve a fight you cannot control: **the Frozen Beam.**

The sigil marks the territory of the Owl, one of the five night monsters you cannot kill, per our [Bosses](/guides/boss-guide) page.

## The Owl Sigil

On the front of the temple, above the door, there is an **owl drawing**. That single detail explains something our [The Owl](/entities/the-owl) page has to report with a hedge — that the Owl is "reported around the Ice Temple". The temple carries the owl's sigil, and the Owl is documented as patrolling the snow. The two are linked in the map, not just in rumour.

## How to Run It

**Check the biome first.** The Snow sector competes with Volcanic for the outer ring, so the Ice Temple is not guaranteed to exist in your run. Do not set out from a camp you have not scoped.

**Bring the right kit:**
- **Warm clothing.** The freeze meter is the quiet killer here; you will be standing still in one spot long enough to feel it.
- **An axe that can actually break ice.** Any axe damages the beam; a **Strong Axe** is reported to make the exposure much shorter. This is the one piece of advice we would not skip.
- **Something that kills a Juggernaut.** The temple holds one, and it is the reason the room punishes you for being careless.
- **Stews or food** for warmth on a long break, if you have them.

**Then:** go up and take the Ice Chest first → chip the beam → on the melt, stop and clear **one at a time**, starting with the ranged one → return to the beam → take what falls → grab the Boost Pad Recipe Tablet on the way out.

**If you just want the chests:** one source points out the fight can be **skipped entirely**. Leave the beam intact, take the two chests and the ground loot, and come back later with a better loadout. Less loot, none of the pressure.

The Ice Sculpting badge is one of the entries here we could only corroborate against two sources — its row is in the event block on our [All Badges](/guides/all-badges) page.

## Where Sources Disagree or Are Silent

- **Is the Ice Chest guaranteed?** One source says it always spawns on floor 2; the temple's own listing marks both chests as not guaranteed. Unresolved.
- **"Silver Ice Chest" / "Fancy Ice Chest."** Neither appears in any loot table we could find. We treat them as unverified names for the Ice Chest.
- **Where the Strong Flashlight comes from** is not settled, and only the Strong Axe has a plausible home (the Legendary Chest).
- **The beam's damage milestone has no published number**, and neither does the beam's health, or how many gems it holds.
- **Campfire Level 4 as a gate**, plus the Bench 3 / 15 Wood / 10 Scrap cost for the Winged Boost Pad, and the "Ice Palace" name, all trace to a single aggregator.
- **Ice Temple vs Ice Palace** is a naming disagreement between sources; the temple pages we trust all use **Ice Temple**.
- One source we do not rely on for mechanics (99nightsintheforest.wiki) is on our blacklist for fabricating weapon and class data; we used the location and loot tables from sources we can corroborate instead.

## FAQ

**Where exactly is the Ice Temple?**
In the **Snow Biome**, in the outer ring of the map. Snow competes with Volcanic for that ring, so it does not always generate. See [All Locations](/locations/all-locations) or [Snow Biome](/locations/snow-biome).

**Do I have to break the Frozen Beam?**
To get the **gems**, yes — they are inside it. To get the **chests**, no; you can leave the beam intact and just open the two chests.

**Why did cultists suddenly attack me?**
You hit the beam's damage milestone and the encased cultists melted. Stop hitting the beam, clear the melted ones, then resume.

**Is there a second floor?**
Yes. Up the two staircases, and the Ice Chest spawns on the left or the right depending on which staircase you take.

**What is the badge for?**
**Ice Sculpting**, for breaking the Frozen Beam. There is a second one, **Aerodynamics**, for using the Boost Pad Recipe Tablet.

**What does the Boost Pad Recipe Tablet do?**
Unlocks the **Winged Boost Pad** recipe once ground at the Grinder. The Winged pad boosts you automatically when you leave the campfire radius, with no charging.

**Does the gem here count toward the last-kill rule?**
The beam's gems do not come from a kill, so the last-kill rule does not obviously apply to them — that is the reading we take, and it is why the route is described as steadier. The cultists you kill during the melt still follow the normal rule.

**Can I get the Ice Sword here?**
It comes from the **Ice Chest** on this temple's second floor, or from the Polar Bear Caves. See [How to Get the Ice Sword](/weapons/how-to-get-ice-sword).
