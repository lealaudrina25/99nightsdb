The Alien Mothership is the flagship raid of the game's alien event — a multi-room dungeon in orbit that you can only reach by **repairing a crashed Command Ship first**. It is where the alien-tech loot comes from, and it is the one alien set-piece that plays like a proper raid rather than a random UFO crash.

Last verified: **2026-10-02**.

## Is It Even Available Right Now?

**Only while an alien event is live.** The Mothership is not a permanent fixture — it exists as part of the alien invasion event, and the version most players know is the one that shipped with the **Aliens' Revenge** update on **27 June 2026**. That event window has closed, so outside a live alien event the Command Ship will not spawn and there is nothing to board.

Practical rule: check the [Update Log](/guides/updates-patch-notes) and your own lobby on a patch Saturday. If the alien event is not active, everything below is what to expect when it returns.

## How to Board It

**1. Wait for Night 3.** The event is **automatic**: on the **third night** a cutscene plays showing **UFOs crashing near your campfire**, and a large **Command Ship** (a giant UFO) appears wrecked behind the camp. No purchase or trigger needed.

**2. Collect 5 Alien Batteries.** These are the green cylindrical items that repair the ship. Sources name **four ways** to get them, and they are all over the map's alien structures:

| Source | Risk | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Alien Sites** | None | Passive loot — an Alien Chest and a battery sitting unattended |
| **Alien Outposts** | High | Towers guarded by alien mobs; clear them, then climb to the battery at the top |
| **Alien Ponds** | Low | Glowing green water; fish the battery up alongside normal catches |
| **Alien Drops** | Medium | Blue launch pads — deliver **3 alien corpses** to activate; pays a battery + a chest |

If you have the map/scanner, Outposts show as marked locations, which makes routing much faster. A widely repeated tip: plan your battery route before Night 3 ends rather than after.

**3. Repair the ship, then walk into the beam.** Insert the batteries into the open compartment on the wreck. Once the last one is in, the ship **lifts off** and projects a **green light beam** underneath. **Walk into the beam** to ascend and board the Mothership.

**One small discrepancy:** most guides say you need **five** batteries, but one (BloxBoom) says **one is already installed** and you only need to find **four more**. Same structure, same end result — just do not be surprised if the ship only asks for four.

### The older entry method (worth knowing)

Before the 2026 rework, the Mothership was reached **directly**: during the original **Alien Invasion** event (18 July 2025), one of the crashed UFOs carried a **portal/teleporter** that took you straight up to the ship — no battery repair step. If you see older guides describing a UFO portal rather than a Command Ship, that is the version they are describing. The current raid adds the battery-repair gate in front of the same flagship.

## Inside the Mothership

The ship is a **linear raid with locked rooms**. You clear aliens, use **corpses as keys**, and push toward the reward chest.

**Who you fight**

| Enemy | Role |
|---|---|
| **Alien Engineer** | Melee attacker; the common mob |
| **Alien Trooper** | Ranged attacker; the other common mob |
| **Alien Commander** | Orange ranged unit — **its corpse unlocks doors** |
| **Alien Scout** | Small blue melee unit — **its corpse unlocks doors too** |

**The door mechanic is the whole puzzle.** Throughout the ship are locked hatches fitted with **alien face scanners**. They do not take keys — they take **bodies**. Kill an **Alien Commander** or **Alien Scout**, **carry its corpse to the matching scanner**, and the door opens. Every locked section costs you one fresh corpse, which is why a rushed solo run is really a loop of "kill, carry, scan" three times over. **Corpses do not stack**, so you ferry them one at a time.

**Three side rooms, then the final chamber.** You must clear **all three side rooms** to progress. Clear them all **in a single run** and you also trigger the **Xenotech** badge. After the side rooms, the **final chamber** opens: it can look empty, so be ready — several aliens spawn in the last fight (one guide notes they come in carrying Rayguns). Win that wave and the **big reward chest** in the middle of the room unlocks.

**Then leave through the "Teleport to Earth" door.** A green doorway in the same room returns you to the forest and completes the run. Do not die in the final wave: dying there forfeits both the chest loot and the badge credit for that run, so it is worth retreating and re-boarding if it goes badly.

## Rewards

**The chest** holds the alien event's headline loot:

- **Alien Shotgun** — a new alien ranged weapon (the Alien Scientist class also spawns with one)
- **Dissolve Ray** — another alien weapon from the same patch
- **Alien-tech blueprints** — crafting materials for the alien tech tree
- plus other high-tier alien crafting materials

**Three badges**, all earnable in one well-planned raid:

| Badge | How to get it |
|---|---|
| **Engineering** | Repair the Command Ship — deposit all 5 batteries and trigger the tractor beam |
| **Mutiny** | Defeat the **Alien Command Center**, the boss in the main Mothership room |
| **Xenotech** | Enter **every side room in one run** — the longest objective, since it forces the three-corpse door loop |

Badge progress **resets between runs**, so the efficient play is one purpose-built raid that does everything rather than three quick ones.

**A limited cosmetic.** The event also offered the **Alien Wolf Skin**, available only during the event window. Like every limited cosmetic in this game, treat it as gone once the event closes.

### The Alien Scientist class

The patch's new class, **Alien Scientist (600 Diamonds)**, is the thematic fit: it **starts with an Alien Shotgun, a Dissolve Ray and a Raygun**, plus **+4 sack space**. Its perk line **dissolves weakened enemies into "Essence"** — enemies below **10% HP** at level 1 (rising to **15%** at level 2), and collecting Essence speeds up Alien Energy regeneration; level 3 adds a sprint-speed bonus from Essence.

It is **not required** — any class can repair the ship and complete the raid. See [All Classes](/classes/all-classes) for where it sits.

## Is It Worth Running?

**Yes, if the event is live.** It is the source of the **Alien Shotgun** and **Dissolve Ray**, it pays alien-tech blueprints, and it is the only way to clear the **Mutiny** and **Xenotech** badges. It is also the natural companion to the [Alien Armour](/items/how-to-get-alien-armour) and the alien weapons — the same event feeds all of them.

**Two practical warnings.** First, **bring ranged, not melee** — the Alien Commanders and Elites hit hard up close, and every guide recommends keeping distance. Second, **stock healing before you board**: you cannot easily resupply mid-raid, and dying in the final wave costs you the whole run's loot.

## FAQ

**How do I get to the Alien Mothership?**
Repair the crashed **Command Ship** near your campfire with **5 Alien Batteries**, then walk into the green beam under the ship to board.

**When does the event happen?**
Automatically on **Night 3** of a run, during a live alien event. The current raid shipped with **Aliens' Revenge** (27 June 2026).

**Where do the Alien Batteries come from?**
Four sources: **Alien Sites** (unattended loot), **Alien Outposts** (clear the guards, then climb the tower), **Alien Ponds** (fish them), and **Alien Drops** (deliver 3 alien corpses to a launch pad).

**How do I open the locked doors inside?**
With **corpses, not keys**. Carry an **Alien Commander** or **Alien Scout** body to the matching face scanner.

**What does the chest give?**
The **Alien Shotgun**, the **Dissolve Ray**, alien-tech blueprints and other high-tier alien materials.

**How do I leave?**
Through the green **"Teleport to Earth"** door in the final chamber.

**Do I need the Alien Scientist class?**
No. It helps (strong alien loadout from minute zero), but any class can finish the raid.
