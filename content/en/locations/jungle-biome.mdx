The **Jungle Biome** is the newest sector on the map and the only one where the ground moves under you. It does not gate you with a keyhole like the [Frog Cave](/locations/frog-cave) does — it gates you with four temples, four skull keys, and one central pyramid that opens a room most players never see. This page is the full route: how to reach it, what each temple gives, what happens the second you take a key, and which of the biome's numbers are agreed and which are not.

## Is It Even There?

Same question as everywhere else on the outer ring, and the answer is the most useful thing to know before you walk out.

**The Jungle is the newest outer biome, and that is why it is the one most likely to be present.** The older consensus in our guides — rblxguide's map guide — is that a run gets **one** outer biome, a Snow *or* Volcanic roll, *and* that the Jungle is always present. That pairing used to sound contradictory. Read chronologically it is not: the Jungle arrived **last** (Part 1, 28 February 2026), long after the Snow-and-Volcanic roll was written down. So the cleanest reading is:

- If your map predates the Jungle content — an older guide, or a run you have not started since February 2026 — the Jungle may be absent.
- On a current run, plan for it being there.

Our [All Locations](/locations/all-locations) page records the disagreement as it stands, and the [Volcanic Biome](/locations/volcanic-biome) page covers the other side of that coin: if your outer ring rolled Snow or Volcanic instead, the Jungle route is not on your map this run.

One date note, because the two wikis differ: the jungle-temples guide and our own [Cultist](/entities/the-cultist) page both date Part 1 to **28 February 2026**, while the fandom wiki's biome page gives **1 March 2026**. The fandom date is a single source against two, so we use 28 February. Part 2 is dated **7 March 2026** by both.

That campfire level is one gate on a much longer ladder. [Campfire Level Gates](/guides/campfire-level-gates) collects every documented campfire gate in the game — chests, caves, ponds, rescues and buildings — into a single table.

Worth pricing before you commit to a route: finishing means reaching the end of Day 99 with all four children rescued. The day clock behind that counter, and the four ways a run can end, are on [how to win 99 Nights in the Forest](/guides/how-to-win-99-nights).

## Getting In: The Campfire Gate

Three sources give three answers, and the disagreement is worth stating rather than picking a winner.

| Source | Campfire level required |
|---|---|
| escorenews | **2+** for the biome edge to show up; full reveal needs a maxed campfire |
| vixhul | **Level 3** |
| The jungle-temples guide | **3–4**, with sources differing among themselves |

**Our reading: expect to see the Jungle at Level 2, to make a real attempt at 3, and to sweep it properly at 4.** The three numbers are not really in conflict — they are different thresholds answering different questions (can I see it, can I get in, can I clear all of it). What matters practically is that temple interiors and the Mother Temple basement are the content you want, and those sit at the hard end of the range.

## What the Jungle Is Like

The Jungle is the only biome whose ground does not stay put, and that single fact explains most of what feels wrong about running it.

- **Moving terrain.** The fandom wiki states plainly that the Jungle is the **first area in the game with moving terrain**. Ground shifts under you, which is what produces the next point.
- **Cliffs you cannot jump.** Because the terrain moves, movement is faster than in the Forest, and that speed can carry you over edges into gaps that were not there a minute earlier. Treat any long run at full sprint here as a liability, not an upgrade.
- **Sightlines you cannot see down.** Dense plants and large rocks block vision much more aggressively than the Forest. This cuts both ways: it hides you from Boars, and it hides Boars from you.
- **The Jungle tree.** One tree type here needs a **Strong Axe** — two hits — and that applies to its logs *and* its saplings. Smaller trees you simply cannot chop at all. Bring the axe, or plan to buy logs rather than cut them.
- **Chests behind traps.** A lot of the biome's containers sit behind traps rather than in the open.

**Tar** is the biome's own terrain hazard: black, bubbling pools that **slow you**, to the point that standing in one a while leaves you barely moving. Jumping out gets you most of your speed back. The two sources that describe it add different stakes — the fandom wiki emphasises the slowdown, while escorenews adds that **tar makes you prey for the Boars** in it. Take that seriously: a slowed player in a Tar pool is what a Boar is looking for.

**Stone platforms** exist in the Jungle and currently have **no use**. They are listed here so a rumour of a use does not become a target worth farming.

The skull key is the one key in the game that takes its own temple apart, and our [Keys and Locked Doors](/guides/keys-and-locked-doors) guide sets the loop beside the other five keys.

## The Four Temples and the Key Loop

This is the biome's quest, and it is a loop rather than a dungeon crawl: **four temples, one key each, one pyramid at the middle.**

The four **Jungle Temples** sit roughly **north, south, east and west of the Mother Temple**, built in an Aztec style. Each one is a small pyramid, and each has the same shape inside:

1. **The roof rooms.** Two Jungle Cultists and an ordinary container. These are optional — you do not have to clear the roof to finish the temple.
2. **The basement obby.** A trap course. **Blowdart Traps** fire along it; past them there is a small amount of resources, a **Vine Chest**, and the **Skull Trial Stone**.
3. **The stone.** One per temple. This is the key.

On naming, because this trips people up: the jungle-temples guide calls it a **Crystal Skull Key**, and notes the community calls it a **Skull Trial Stone**. The fandom wiki uses **Skull Trial Stone** throughout. Same object, two names — and both are load-bearing, since our own [All Locations](/locations/all-locations) page lists the drop under one and the temple route under the other.

Two escape-route details that save runs:

- **Do not grab the stone before you know where the rope is.** The fandom wiki's tip is to stand **-by the rope first**, then take the Skull Trial Stone. The blast of Blowdarts comes with the stone, and the rope is your out.
- **The rope is the exit, not a locked door.** escorenews is explicit that the rope at the back is how you leave the temple. It is a way out, not a gate you have to find a key for.

## When You Take the Key: The Collapse

Taking the stone is not the reward. It is the alarm.

Per the jungle-temples guide, taking a key **triggers the temple to collapse and fires poisoned darts** — and you get out by the rope at the back. vixhul independently reports a temple collapsing with an **escape timer** attached, and adds that **once you leave a temple you cannot go back into it**.

That last point is the biome's real economy: **the temple is a one-shot per run.** If you walk out early, walk away to loot, and come back, it is gone. Plan the temple as a single continuous visit — get in, get the obby, get the stone, get out — rather than as a place you can pop in between other errands.

## What Each Temple Drops

| Item | Per temple | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Crystal Skull Key** (Skull Trial Stone) | **1**, guaranteed | The point of the visit |
| **[Cultist Gem](/items/cultist-gem)** | **3**, guaranteed | Your gem income while the Jungle is on the map |
| **Gem of the Forest Fragment** | **2**, guaranteed | Not the same thing as a Gem — see the FAQ |
| **Blowpipe** | **1**, common | Occasionally seen in a **Vine Chest** instead |
| **Vine Chest** | **1** | On the temple's upper platform |
| **Poison Claws** | **1**, high drop rate | From the **Overlord Jungle Chest** |
| **Poison Armour** | **1**, high drop rate | From the **Overlord Jungle Chest** |

The last two rows live in one container, which is the whole reason for doing the loop. Biome chests of that kind are catalogued on [Chest Tiers](/items/chest-tiers). The **Vine Chest** itself may hold a Blowpipe, Cultist Gems, a Good Sack, a [Poison Spear](/weapons/how-to-get-poison-spear), a Strong Flashlight, a Crossbow or a Medkit.

**Poison Claws** are worth a caveat before you farm hours for them. The only published numbers come from a single source (Sportskeeda), which puts the **cooldown at 0.2 seconds**, the **hit at roughly 30 damage plus poison**, and adds that the poison does **not stack**, that the **range is short**, and that its real value is **applying poison constantly rather than hitting hard**. Treat those numbers as one source, not five.

## The Mother Temple

The central pyramid is where the four keys become the reward.

The four keys go onto **four altars at the top of the Mother Temple**, which opens a **hidden staircase** down to a **secret treasure room**. Behind that door is the **Overlord Jungle Chest**, and that chest is the reason the loop exists.

Its contents: **Poison Claws, Poison Armour and a Blowpipe** — i.e. the two high-rate items from the table above plus the ranged one. The fandom wiki describes the same room a different way: the Mother Temple basement needs the **4 Skull Trial Stones to open**, and pays **3 Cultist Gems, 2 Gem fragments and 1 Jungle Chest**, where the Jungle Chest may hold **Poison Armour or Poison Claws**.

**These two descriptions are the same room described from different ends**, and the honest reading is: the Overlord Jungle Chest is the container the fandom wiki calls the Jungle Chest, dropping Poison Armour and Poison Claws, while the published per-temple table treats those same two items as the Mother Temple's high-rate drops. Either way the output is the same — one run of the loop is the only reliable route to Poison Claws and Poison Armour.

Two reset rules, and they conflict:

| Rule | Source | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| The challenge **does not reset within a run** | jungle-temples guide | Do not come back to the same temple twice in one run; it is spent |
| Basement resources, chests and Skull Trial Stones **refresh daily** | fandom wiki | New run, new day, fresh temple |
| Jungle Chest per player, but Gems refresh daily | fandom wiki | You get one Jungle Chest, and the Gems underneath it come back |

Our reading: **the three statements are compatible if you keep the units straight.** Nothing inside a temple comes back *in the run you cleared it*; what refreshes is the run itself. What is genuinely unclear is the Jungle Chest's once-per-player rule against a Gem that refreshes — nobody has published a test that separates the two, so do not plan a farming route around it.

## The Fighting Pit

Part 2 of the Jungle update (**7 March 2026**) moved a lot of the reward pressure out of the temples and into two new places, and the **Fighting Pit** is one of them.

It is a **circular stone pit**. Standing on the **button in the middle** spawns waves of **Jungle Cultists and Boars — five waves in total**. Clear all five and the reward is the **Poison Log Wall blueprint**.

The blueprint being the entire payout is the thing to weigh before you farm it: five waves is a real commitment for a single blueprint, so only run the Pit once you have the gear to clear it without a death.

## What Lives Here

| Thing | What to know |
|---|---|
| **[Boar](/entities/the-boar)** | The biome's common animal threat and **harder than a Wolf**, with higher damage. One source calls it only slightly tougher, but says **Corn tames them more easily** than wolves — useful if you are building a pet comp |
| **Jungle Cultist** | ~**120 HP**, either a **Poison Spear (30)** or a **Blowpipe (45)**, wearing **poison armour that punishes you for meleeing it**. Full stat lines, night raids and corpses are on the [Cultist](/entities/the-cultist) page — the point here is the strategy below |
| **Frogs** | Ordinary frogs plus a **Poison Frog**, distinguished by **tongue colour**. Poison frogs were originally Frog Invasion territory, so if the invasion is not running, this may be the only place to meet them |
| **Mossy Mammoths** | Spawn **naturally inside Jungle structures**, and each one yields **two** |
| **The Cat** | See the next section — it does not care which biome you are standing in, only that a Jungle exists |
| **Tar** | Not a creature, but the reason a slow player gets eaten — see above |

**Fight the Jungle Cultist at range.** With poison armour and a 30-or-45 damage ranged option, closing to melee is exactly what the armour is built to punish, and the [Poison Spear](/weapons/how-to-get-poison-spear) and its neighbours are the reason to carry one.

One more piece of advice from escorenews, and it is a counterintuitive one: if you are not geared up, **skip the roof cultists and loot only the basement** — the basement containers often hold a [Poison Spear](/weapons/how-to-get-poison-spear) or a Blowpipe anyway, which is a cheaper way to get the biome's gear than clearing two cultists on a roof.

## The Cat: It Follows You Into the Jungle

**The Cat is not a Jungle creature. The Jungle is just one of the places it will find you.**

The fandom wiki states that when the Jungle exists, The Cat **wanders the Jungle and the Forest on any run, at night**, and attacks with a **Pounce**. That means the biome you walked into does not decide whether the Cat is out — only the clock does. Going into the Jungle at night is not an escape from the Forest's night hunter; it is a relocation.

The full entity page — the awakening cutscene, the Day 3–4 gate, the two attacks, why stepping out of the red circle can still get you hit, what a wall does and what high ground never will, and whether it can be killed at all — is on [The Cat](/entities/the-cat). Note that the Jungle makes the Cat *possible*; it does not keep it inside the biome, and it is not a 50–50 roll between you and it either. That arithmetic is on the entity page too.

## What to Bring, and A Run Order That Survives

**Bring:** a **Strong Axe** (the Jungle tree takes two hits), a **ranged weapon** (the Jungle Cultist's poison armour makes melee expensive), something that **clears Tar** fast — i.e. do not linger — and a **return path** you can walk out the same way.

**The order that works:**

1. **Check the biome first.** Is the Jungle on your outer ring at all? If not, the Ice Temple is your gem route this run — see [Ice Temple](/locations/ice-temple).
2. **Upgrade the campfire before the sweep.** Below Level 3, half this content is off the map.
3. **Treat each temple as one continuous visit.** Roof, obby, stone, rope. Do not leave and come back.
4. **Take the rope before the stone.**
5. **Bank all four keys, then do the Mother Temple last.** Four keys in, treasure room out — the reward is behind the pyramid, not inside the temples.
6. **Run the Fighting Pit only once you can clear five waves**, since the blueprint is the only payout.

## Where Sources Disagree or Are Silent

**The campfire gate.** Level 2, level 3, level 3–4. Resolved above as three different thresholds rather than three different answers — but no source states the level for the *Mother Temple basement* specifically, so treat that portion as unknown.

**Key names.** Crystal Skull Key versus Skull Trial Stone. Same object; the jungle-temples guide is the only source that names both.

**Reset behaviour.** Not-resets-in-a-run versus refreshes-daily versus once-per-player chests. Partly reconcilable, partly untested — see the Mother Temple section.

**The Jungle Chest versus the Overlord Jungle Chest.** One source gives a container list, another a room list. We read them as the same room; no source resolves which name is official.

**The Part 1 date.** 28 February versus 1 March 2026. We use 28 February (two sources against one).

**Silent.** Nobody publishes how long the collapse timer is, whether the Fighting Pit's waves scale with the day count, or whether any jungle loot is affected by the biome refresher.

## FAQ

**Do I need all four temples for the Mother Temple?**
Yes. The Mother Temple's altars take **four** keys, so a clean run visits all four Jungle Temples before attempting the pyramid. Missing one key means the hidden staircase stays shut.

**Is a Gem of the Forest Fragment a Cultist Gem?**
No. They are separate items, the temple drops **3 Gems and 2 Fragments** per visit, and the fragment should not be spent as if it were a gem.

**Why did my temple have no stone?**
Either you already cleared it this run (the jungle-temples guide says the challenge does not reset within a run), or you are below the campfire level that reveals the room. If it is a new day and a new run, the fandom wiki says the stone comes back.

**Do I have to kill the roof cultists?**
No. If you are undergeared, loot the basement and go. The basement chests are the cheaper gear.

**Is Tar dangerous or just slow?**
Both, and the second is how the first happens. It slows you, and a slowed player in a tar pool is what Boars look for.

**Can I get Poison Claws without the loop?**
Not reliably. They are the Overlord Jungle Chest's high-rate drop, which means four temples and the Mother Temple first.
