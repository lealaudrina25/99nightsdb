The **Caverns** are the only place in the game that pays you in permanent effects instead of gear. Clear a cave, pick a statue, and you get a **blessing** that stays with the server: longer days, slower hunger, fewer lightning strikes, less rain. There are four caves and four picks, which is why this system is worth understanding before you understand anything else about it.

What actually stops players is not the reward design. It is a **bat that cannot be killed, cannot see you, and still hits you from across the room** — plus three cultist variants that only exist underground. Both get covered here, because both are properties of the *place*, not of the enemies.

And there is one thing nobody explains: **every source lists eight blessings, and only one of them tells you how the second tier is earned.** That is the section we spent the most time on.

The campfire level above is one row of a much bigger list. [Campfire Level Gates](/guides/campfire-level-gates) is the first page here to gather every gate the six campfire levels open, with a link to the page that documents each one.

## What the Caverns Actually Are

There is not one cave. There are **four**, and they are not the same cave set up three more times.

The **main cave spawns on Day 1**, close to the campfire, and shows up on the map as soon as you craft the **Map Blueprint** — a cave icon marking the entrance, with the main one carrying a yellow exclamation mark. Pro Game Guides describes the central Caves location appearing on the map on Day 1 plainly, which matches.

The other **three are smaller caves**. Two accounts of how they open agree on the method and disagree on the trigger:

- **The trigger is TNT.** All Things How and Desinema both say the smaller cave entrances are blocked off initially and need **TNT from inside the main cave** to blast them open. Once open, they work as extra statue-and-blessing opportunities and as new exits back to the forest. The fandom wiki carries the same TNT requirement.
- **The trigger is a campfire level.** All Things How also frames it as "crafting a map and upgrading the campfire enough to unlock cave access", and Desinema repeats that the three smaller caves become accessible as you upgrade your campfire level.

TNT is the better attested of the two, because it appears in three places rather than two, and because it explains something the campfire version does not: **the TNT comes from inside the main cave**, so the order of operations is main cave first, side caves later. Take the campfire reading as a secondary detail rather than the rule.

One naming note, since this trips people up: the cave system is **The Caverns** on RBLXGuide and on this site, and the fandom wiki files the same content under **Caves**. Same thing, two names. A third name — **"Obsidian Caverns"** — is not real; it comes from a site we blacklist (99nightsforest.wiki) and we have already flagged it in [How to Get the Obsidiron Hammer](/weapons/how-to-get-obsidiron-hammer), which names that fake recipe in full.

## Inside: Dark, Long Corridors, and Crystals You Have to Charge

The Caverns are dark. Every source that describes the interior says the same thing, and every one of them lands on the same loadout conclusion: **a Strong Flashlight is mandatory, not optional.**

The mechanic underneath that advice is specific. There are **crystal clusters set into the cave walls**, and you charge them by **running your flashlight beam over them until they glow**. A charged crystal lights a large area around it, which is what makes the cultists and the Bat trackable. All Things How describes this as the reason to bother — better sightlines near the statue rooms — and it is also the practical answer to "why did I walk past the exit".

The geometry is long corridors with side paths off them, and that shape matters more than it sounds: it is what allows the Bat to be avoided without being fought, and it is what makes the side-chamber sniping spot (below) possible.

The Bat in that list is one of the game's five unkillable night monsters, collected on our [Bosses](/guides/boss-guide) page.

## Who Is Down There

### The Bat — blind, unkillable, and still ranged

The Bat is the entity this update arrived with, and it behaves unlike anything else near your campfire.

**You cannot kill it.** The stat block lists it as **HP: N/A (Unkillable)**, and the fandom wiki, Deltia's Gaming, RBLXGuide and TechWiser all say the same thing: no health bar, no damage route, survive until it leaves the room.

**It is blind** — and that single fact is the whole counter-play, because it means the flashlight does not work on it the way it works on [The Deer](/entities/the-deer) or [The Owl](/entities/the-owl). The fandom wiki states it outright: the Bat is blind and therefore **cannot be stunned by the flashlight**. Deltia's Gaming states the opposite — that pointing your flashlight straight at the Bat **stuns it and cancels the scream**. That is a genuine disagreement between the two most detailed accounts, and we are recording it rather than splitting it. What is not in dispute is the mitigation: **get behind something solid.**

**How it attacks.** Entering the Caverns for the first time, the Bat lets out a screech and flies away, which is what starts the encounter. It then flies a fixed path, briefly landing at nodes to screech again. Before each screech a **rising screech meter** appears above it — that is your warning — and when it fills, a rapidly expanding **red sphere** leaves the Bat and damages anyone it catches, blurring your screen. The fandom wiki names the cover objects specifically: a **stalagmite, a rock, or any other provided object**. Walls block it completely, and Deltia's notes that simply running far enough away also works.

**Its damage figure is a single source.** RBLXGuide puts it at **35 per hit**; no other source we found publishes a number. Treat 35 as uncorroborated until you see it twice.

Two smaller items worth knowing: Deltia's Gaming says each scream **summons Shadow Cultists** into the cave (a claim only that source makes), and the fandom wiki notes the Bat is the first monster entity to attack another monster entity — it fights [The Deer](/entities/the-deer) in a cutscene — and the only one that can appear when two monsters are already in play. The Bat has a page of its own — see [The Bat](/entities/the-bat), which covers the Deer is Hurt cutscene that opened this cave, the four-phase scream loop, and the flashlight dispute in full.

### The three cultist variants

The Caverns also introduced three cultist types that exist nowhere else. [Our Cultist page](/entities/the-cultist) already lists them with their full stat lines, so here is only what a cave runner needs:

| Variant | Where | What a runner needs to know |
|---|---|---|
| **Shadow** | Caverns and side chambers | ~**120 HP**, low damage, jagged knife, arrives in **groups of 2–4** |
| **Brute** ([page](/entities/the-brute-cultist)) | Caverns and the Final Cave | **400–450 HP**, **40 damage**, battleaxe. Roughly **10–12 spawn at once**, but the slow attack cooldown makes melee survivable |
| **Darkstring** | Caverns, including side chambers | A **ranged** variant that fires **two bolts** per volley |

The Brute numbers are worth flagging: they come from **BloxSwaps alone** in our [Cultist page](/entities/the-cultist), and the fandom wiki corroborates that these variants exist underground but publishes no health figure for them. We carry the figures because they are internally consistent, not because they are double-sourced. Which source prints which figure — including the one that prints none — is itemised on the [Brute Cultist](/entities/the-brute-cultist) page.

**Side chambers are quieter than the main route.** Per the fandom wiki, the side chambers hold **Darkstring and Shadow cultists only** — no Bat, no Brute — which is what makes the third-statue ladder spot worth the climb.

Clearing the Caverns runs into the same lock logic as the missing kids' caves, which our [Keys and Locked Doors](/guides/keys-and-locked-doors) guide sets out plainly.

Four to six bandages is the standard caverns loadout and this page never says where they come from; our [Healing, Bandages and Medkits](/guides/healing-guide) guide does.

Plan this step against the end game rather than in isolation: a winning run has to finish all four rescues inside the day counter, and the last one is campfire Level 6 gated. The full win condition is on [how to win 99 Nights in the Forest](/guides/how-to-win-99-nights).

If you are mapping the late game, the gate behind this is the **Gem of the Forest** — four fragments make one gem, and tier 5 wants five of them. The [Gem of the Forest](/items/gem-of-the-forest) page is the one that says so.

## Clearing a Cave, and the Green Gem

The statue room is gated, and the gate is the whole cave: **you must clear the entire cave to open it.**

Clear it, and you walk into a room containing **a green gem set into the wall of a structure**. Activating it shows you **two randomly chosen statues**, drawn from the Deer, Owl, Ram and Bat, and **you must pick one**. Selecting one grants the matching blessing, and — per the fandom wiki — that blessing is **server-wide**, not just yours.

The failure mode is the thing that ruins runs: **if you do not fully clear a chamber, the cultists in chambers 1 through 4 respawn.** Brutes now between you and the next statue are therefore self-inflicted. The fandom wiki recommends clearing as you go rather than rushing, which is also the advice that makes the ladder spot below work.

Blessing I and Blessings II are two separate badges; both are listed with their payouts on our [All Badges](/guides/all-badges) page.

One of the eight is the only blessing in the game that touches the hunger bar, and it can be taken twice. The reduction and the resulting timings are on the [**hunger**](/guides/hunger) page.

None of the eight touch death directly, though one of them touches the bar that leads to it. What happens when that bar empties is on the [**death and respawn**](/guides/death-and-respawn) page.

One Cavern detour buys the Heavy Ammo Crate upgrade, and our [Ammo and Guns](/guides/ammo-guide) guide prices what that changes at the crate.


Four statues, two tiers each. Every list we found contains the same eight entries, and the effects are stated identically across them:

| Statue | Tier | Effect (as stated) |
|---|---|---|
| **Deer** | I / II | Day length **+10 seconds**, night length **−10 seconds** |
| **Bat** | I / II | Hunger drain **reduced by 10%** |
| **Ram** | I / II | **Reduced chance of lightning** |
| **Owl** | I / II | **Reduced chance of rain** |

Three of the four are qualitative — the Ram and Owl effects are expressed as a lower *chance*, with no percentage published by anyone. Worth stating plainly: **the Ram on this page is a statue you choose, not [The Ram](/entities/the-ram) the monster** — two different things that share a name, and the monster is the one that cannot spawn down here. Only the Deer and Bat numbers are concrete, and only the Deer one carries a figure attached to it: **10 seconds each way**.

### The Tier II problem

Here is the thing no source solves. All eight blessings above are listed everywhere, every guide lists the **II** versions, and **not one of them says how you obtain the second tier.** Pro Game Guides, All Things How, Beebom and Desinema all describe Tier II as simply "better than Tier I" and stop there.

The only mechanism on offer is the fandom wiki's: **"a statue's blessing can be received multiple times by completing multiple caves and selecting it multiple times."** Under that reading, Tier II is what you get for selecting the same statue again in a later cave — the later pick is the stronger version.

That conflicts slightly with the arithmetic other guides state. All Things How and Pro Game Guides both say there are **four caves and a maximum of four blessings per run**, one pick per cave. Four picks could be four different statues, or four picks of one statue — but if four picks are spent on four different statues, you have four Tier I blessings and **no Tier II at all**.

Two things make that resolvable without inventing an answer:

1. Our own [patch notes page](/guides/updates-patch-notes) records a **Caves Update Part 2 in January 2026** that added **Blessing I and II badges** alongside new cave areas and the explosives. That is the earliest we can find anywhere for the *badge* tier existing as a tracked pair.
2. The guides that list only Tier I — Beebom, Desinema — read as the older material, and the ones that discuss both tiers (Pro Game Guides, All Things How) are the later ones.

**Our reading:** Tier II is the January 2026 addition, obtainable by selecting the same statue in a second cave, and the guides that still show one tier per statue predate the addition. We flag this as our inference, not a sourced fact — **no source states the mechanism, so treat Tier II acquisition as unconfirmed until you test it in game.**

## Which Statue to Choose

If you take one prioritisation from the sources, take the fandom wiki's: **Day Length first, then Hunger, then Lightning — and never pick Rain.** The reason is in the table above: the Owl is the only blessing whose entire job is to make a bad thing less likely, while the Deer and Bat change a resource you are actively spending.

Two nuances that change the value of the Deer:

- **The Deer blessing does not make the game faster.** The time it takes from the night goes straight into the day. Fandom says this explicitly, and it is the single most useful line in the whole blessing guide — without it you will pick Deer expecting a speedrun and end up with a more forgiving run instead.
- **The Deer, Owl and Ram cannot spawn in the Caverns**, and neither can wolves or alpha wolves. A cleared chamber is therefore the safest place to sit out a night — even the AFK spot, if you are willing to share it with the Bat. That is not a bug in the design; it is what makes a failed cave run less punishing than it looks.

## The Loot Dispute

This is where the sources stop agreeing entirely, and the disagreement is not cosmetic.

**One party says the Caverns are full of loot.** The fandom wiki describes the side chambers holding a **high number of Legendary Chests, Gold Chests, Iron Chests, Ruby Chests, and the cave-exclusive "Miner's Chest" Those tiers are catalogued on [Chest Tiers](/items/chest-tiers).**. It lists what a Miner's Chest can contain:

- **Impact Grenade** — a hotbar weapon, **×5 ammo per pickup**, throwable, no reload, solid splash damage.
- **Dynamite** — a sack item, set on fire, **delays, then explodes** for large splash damage.
- **Explosive rifle ammo / explosive revolver ammo** — **×6 ammo**, sizzles on firing, slightly more damage (marked as an assumption), small chance to set the target on fire. Rifle and revolver fire explosive rounds *before* regular ammo.
- **Heavy Ammo Crate Recipe Tablet** — always in the **final** Miner's Chest, the **Gold Miner's Chest beside the last blessing chamber**. Grind it at your campfire to upgrade the Ammo Crate blueprint; the Heavy Ammo Crate then sells explosive rounds for **5 [scrap](/items/how-to-get-scrap)** each.

**The other party says there is nothing down there.** Beebom states flatly that there is **no loot in the cave, not even a single chest**. Desinema says the same — no traditional loot, no chests, no items. Neither fandom's Miner's Chest nor its explosive ammo appears anywhere in their text.

We cannot resolve it from the sources, so we are not going to claim there are chests. What both sides agree on is the practical rule, and it comes from fandom: **do not spend your Impact Grenades in fights until every side chamber is open**, or you can end up unable to open a Legendary Chest for lack of explosives. That advice holds under either reading of the dispute.

Some Chests hand out seeds; the complete seed list is on our [Seed Packs](/items/seed-packs) page.
The Dynamite usage note is fandom-only and worth knowing before you pick it up: because the fuse is long, it is near-useless in combat **except** from the top of a Child's Cave, where you can drop it on the animal guards below.

Cavern runs and meteor nights overlap often enough that our [Weather and Night Events](/guides/weather-and-night-events) guide is a useful companion before you descend.


Four reasons, none of them equipment:

1. **Blessings are the only permanent payoff the cave system has.** One pick per cave, four caves, and the effects are server-wide. Nothing in the cave can be farmed for a number the way Cultist Gems or Frog Chest drops can.
2. **It is the safe night spot.** No Deer, Owl, Ram, wolf or alpha wolf can reach the Caverns. The only thing that can hurt you down there is the Bat, and it can be out-ranged by a wall.
3. **It is where the explosives come from**, under the fandom reading — and the Heavy Ammo Crate upgrade is what makes explosive ammo a purchasable economy rather than a lucky drop.
4. **The Bat cannot follow you out.** It is confined to the caves, which is why this is the only monster in the game that can never reach your campfire.

a caverns run is mostly a carrying problem, and what the trip is worth depends on the sack you are wearing — see [sacks and inventory capacity](/guides/inventory-capacity).

## How the Caverns Compare

| | **The Caverns** | [Ice Temple](/locations/ice-temple) | [Frog Cave](/locations/frog-cave) | [Cultist Stronghold](/locations/cultist-stronghold) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Gate** | Cave unlocked on **Day 1**; side caves need **TNT** | Snow biome, and Snow competes with Volcanic | **8 Frog Keys** + Campfire Level 2 | None — walk in, break the beam |
| **Main threat** | **The Bat** (unkillable) + three cultist variants | Frozen Beam and the frozen cultists | Frog King and his split cascade | Cultist archers and the difficulty ladder |
| **Boss killable?** | **No** — the Bat has no health | n/a | Yes | Yes |
| **Main reward** | **Blessing** (permanent, server-wide) | Cultist Gems from the beam | [Beastmaster](/entities/the-frog-king) badge, 1–3% chest rolls | Diamonds |
| **Exit rule** | Cleared chambers stay cleared; uncleared ones **respawn** | Repeatable | Yes | Yes |
| **Per-player or shared** | **Server-wide** blessing | Per run | Per run | Per run |

The row that matters is the last one. Every other location on this site hands a player their reward; the Caverns hand it to everyone in the server, which makes the four picks a social decision rather than a solo one.

## Where Sources Disagree or Are Silent

- **How Tier II is earned** — the fandom wiki's "select the same statue again" is the only mechanism on offer; Pro Game Guides and All Things How both cap you at four blessings and never reconcile the two.
- **Whether the Caverns contain loot** — fandom lists Legendary/Gold/Iron/Ruby/Miner's Chests with contents; Beebom and Desinema both say there is not a single chest.
- **Whether the flashlight stops the Bat** — fandom says the Bat is blind and cannot be stunned; Deltia's Gaming says a direct beam stuns it and cancels the scream.
- **Whether the side caves need TNT or a campfire upgrade** — TNT is stated by fandom plus All Things How and Desinema; the campfire reading appears in All Things How and Desinema only.
- **The Bat's damage** — **35** is RBLXGuide alone; nobody else publishes a figure.
- **The Brute's 400–450 HP and 40 damage** — BloxSwaps alone; fandom confirms the variants, not the numbers.
- **Whether screams summon Shadow Cultists** — Deltia's Gaming alone.
- **Nobody publishes:** the exact chance reduction on the Ram and Owl blessings, the animal spawn count per cave, whether the blessing cap of four is a hard limit, or whether the second tier can be earned after a run's fourth pick.

## FAQ

**Do I need to find the cave on the map first?** The main cave is on the map from **Day 1**, marked with a cave icon and a yellow exclamation. The other three appear as you progress.

**How do I get into the smaller caves?** With **TNT from the main cave**, per the sources that specify a method.

**Can I kill the Bat?** No. It has no health and cannot be damaged by any means we can find. Survive it, do not fight it.

**How do I avoid the Bat's scream?** Watch for the rising **screech meter**, then put a stalagmite, rock or wall between you and it. Distance also works.

**Does the flashlight stop the Bat?** The sources disagree. Fandom says it is blind and cannot be stunned; Deltia's Gaming says a direct beam cancels the scream. Cover is the reliable answer either way.

**Is the Bat worth the risk for the blessing?** The Bat is only one of the reasons to clear a cave, and it never leaves the cave system.

**How many blessings can one run give?** Four, according to every source that states a cap — one per cave.

**Can Deer, Owl or Ram appear in the Caverns?** No. Neither can wolves or alpha wolves, which is why a cleared cave is a safe place to wait out a night.

**Do I get to keep my blessing?** It is granted server-wide when the statue is activated, and the fandom wiki says the same statue's blessing can be taken more than once by clearing more caves.

**Is there loot in the Caverns?** The sources flatly disagree — one side lists Miner's Chests and four chest tiers, the other says there is not one chest. We report both rather than pick one.
