The **Volcanic Biome** is the endgame sector of 99 Nights in the Forest — the one that kills you with the floor. It holds the Infernal gear, the Cultist King, and the sacrifice altar, and every one of those rewards is paid for in lava damage and cultist waves. This page is the map: what the biome contains, what each structure guards, and the two numbers that decide whether a trip pays off.

## Is It Even There?

This is the first thing to check, and it is why some runs never see a volcano.

**Snow and Volcanic are a roll, not a guarantee.** rblxguide's location guide puts it plainly: each run gets **one** outer biome — a Snow *or* Volcanic roll — and the Jungle is **always** present. A separate event report describes the same mechanic from the other direction: when an event temporarily forced the Volcanic Biome in, it **replaced the Snow Biome**, and afterwards "either biome will appear at random".

Our [Snow Biome](/locations/snow-biome) page covers the other side of that coin. If your outer ring came up Snow, there is no volcano this run — plan around what you got rather than waiting for one.

Lava and poison are both chip damage and this is the biome where a downed player is least reachable; our [Healing, Bandages and Medkits](/guides/healing-guide) guide covers the heal budget under constant damage.

## The Lava Rules

Lava is the reason this biome is dangerous in a way Snow and Jungle are not.

- **It damages you and ignites you.** Lava pools hurt players *and* non-invincible enemies, and set them on fire for additional damage over time. This is also why enemies can die in it without your help.
- **It destroys your loot.** **Nearly every item dropped into lava disappears permanently.** This sounds like trivia until your first opening goes wrong: the Volcanic Church's chest sits on a **roof**, and items that roll out of it can slide into the lava below. Open roof and edge chests fast, and never fight near a pool with a full sack.
- **You can fish in it.** Lava fishing yields the **Lionfish** and the **Lava Eel** — the biome's own fish pool.
- **The Infernal Helmet is the counter.** It is crafted specifically so you take **no lava damage when it is equipped**. The recipe and the environmental-lava debate are on [How to Get the Infernal Helmet](/items/how-to-get-infernal-helmet) — and the existence of a "no lava damage" perk is itself a strong argument that lava very much does damage you by default, which is how we resolved that argument.

the Red Spiked Chest is where nearly all Infernal Sacks come from, and the odds attached to those two chests are set against the rest of the ladder in our [sacks and inventory capacity](/guides/inventory-capacity) guide.

## The Two Chest Types

The biome replaces ordinary chests with two of its own. **The difference between them is the whole economy of the biome.**

| Chest | How rare | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| **Blue Spiked** (also called the **Hot Chest**) | Common across the biome | Wildfire potion, Sacrifice Totem, Lava Mine Blueprint, Crossbow |
| **Red Spiked** (elsewhere called the **Legendary Infernal Chest**) | **Only 2 in the entire biome** | Infernal Sack (~80%), Infernal Sword (~10%), [Infernal Crossbow](/weapons/how-to-get-infernal-crossbow) (~10%) |

**Read that second row carefully, because it changes how the sword should be hunted.** A Red Spiked Chest is most likely to hand you an **Infernal Sack** — the sword and crossbow are roughly **1-in-10** outcomes each. There are **exactly two** of these chests in a run: one at the **back of the Ammo Furnace**, and one on the **roof of the Volcanic Church**.

So the realistic plan is not "open Infernal Chests until the sword drops". It is "get both Red Spiked Chests every run, and expect a Sack most of the time". Our [How to Get the Infernal Sword](/weapons/how-to-get-infernal-sword) page covers the third and better route — the altar — and has now been corrected to reflect the payout order below.

Naming note, because it costs people real time: the **Infernal Chest / Inferno Chest / Red Spiked Chest / Legendary Infernal Chest** labels in circulation all point at the same red chest, and **Hot Chest** is the blue one. There is no separate hidden tier. The classic tiers they replace are on [Chest Tiers](/items/chest-tiers).

Oil Barrels sit at the centre of that: they are the campfire fuel the biome's drill economy runs on, and the biome itself is gated by campfire level. [Campfire](/guides/campfire) covers the fuel table and the levels.

## Every Structure, and What Guards It

| Structure | Guards | What's inside |
|---|---|---|
| **Volcano** | 3 melee + 3 crossbow cultists | The sacrifice altar — see below. Requires campfire **Level 4+** |
| **Cultist King Palace** | 2 melee + 2 crossbow cultists | **4 pedestals** — place four Cultist Gems to summon him. See [The Cultist King](/entities/the-cultist-king) |
| **Ammo Furnace** (The Furnace) | 4 melee + 4 crossbow cultists | Drop **any fuel** at the top and it outputs revolver, rifle or shotgun ammo at random. One of the two **Red Spiked Chests** is around the back |
| **Volcanic Church** | 3 melee + 3 crossbow cultists | Healing items inside; the second **Red Spiked Chest** is on the **roof**, reached by a short obby. Watch for loot sliding into the lava |
| **Cultist Tower** | 4 cultists | An Infernal Chest, and a **[Cultist Gem](/items/cultist-gem)** from the last kill |
| **Cultist Lavaduct** | 3 cultists | An Infernal Chest, and a **[Cultist Gem](/items/cultist-gem)** from the last kill |
| **Scorpion Pit** | 7 Scorpions | One Blue Spiked Chest and three resources |
| **Burning house** | — | Small loot, 0–3 items |

Two practical notes: the **Ammo Furnace block is a known problem** — the map sometimes spawns a boulder across the first ladder, and you have to go around and double-jump the cliff near the barrels to get up. And fuel for the furnace is free, so if you need ammo this is the cheapest place in the game to get it.

## The Volcano: How the Payout Actually Works

This is the biome's best reward loop, and the part most guides simplify into something misleading.

**The mechanic.** Go inside the volcano (campfire **Level 4+**), get on the open-sided platform over the lava pool, and throw bodies or totems in to **charge the altar**. Every **8 charges** pays out a reward — and **the rewards come in a fixed order, repeating**:

| Charges | Reward |
|---|---|
| 8 | A **Gem of the Forest** fragment |
| 16 | **3× Cultist Gems** |
| 24 | **Infernal Sword** *or* [Infernal Crossbow](/weapons/how-to-get-infernal-crossbow) |
| 32 | Back to a Gem of the Forest fragment — the cycle repeats |

**The sword is the third payout, not the first.** That is the detail our own Infernal Sword page had wrong before this page: eight offerings gets you a Gem fragment, and you need to reach the **third** cycle — around **24 charges** — before the sword or crossbow is on the table.

Charges are not all worth the same:

- **A normal cultist body = 1 charge.**
- **A Sacrifice Totem = 2 charges**, and so does a **large cultist** (Brute, Juggernaut). A Brute is also the one body where sacrificing it and raising it are said to cost different things — see [Brute Cultist](/entities/the-brute-cultist). - **Players can be sacrificed, living or dead, once each** — their bodies reappear next to the lava.
- **Lost Children can be thrown in but do not charge it** — they reappear beside the lava, unharmed. Do not waste the trip.

So the efficient route is to bring **Sacrifice Totems** (a Blue Spiked Chest drop) rather than farming 24 individual cultists — or to save the Juggernauts you meet in higher-level [Cultist Stronghold](/locations/cultist-stronghold) raids for the altar.

Two more creatures are exclusive in a quieter way — the Lava Eel and the Lionfish exist in no other body of water in the game. [**Fishing in 99 Nights in the Forest**](/guides/fishing-guide) maps every water to its catch pool.

## What Lives Here

| Enemy | Notes |
|---|---|
| [**Scorpion**](/entities/the-scorpion) | 50 HP, 20 damage **plus poison over time**, walkspeed 20 (**30** in the Scorpion Pit or nest). Drops up to one Morsel and up to one **Scorpion Shell** — the Infernal Helmet ingredient. Dry **bushes can be Scorpion nests**: linger in range and one spawns |
| [**Cultists**](/entities/the-cultist) | The melee and crossbow mix you know from the Stronghold, guarding nearly every structure above |
| **Hellephant** | The hostile red Mammoth at the **Hellephant Ritual Circle**. See [How to Tame a Hellephant](/taming/how-to-tame-a-hellephant) |
| [**The Ram**](/entities/the-ram) | An **unkillable** Volcanic hazard (reported 25 damage) that **charges in a straight line and flattens trees**. Do not plan to fight it — plan to leave. Behaviour, what can stun it, and the walk-speed dispute are on [The Ram](/entities/the-ram) |

Also worth knowing: the biome's **leafless trees give 3 logs but zero saplings**, and fall to fewer Old Axe hits than normal trees. The biome is a bad place to build and a bad place to farm wood — bring your logs with you.

Worth adding to that list: the lava pools hold two fish found nowhere else, both worth more cooked. See [**Hunger in 99 Nights in the Forest**](/guides/hunger).

Add Bandages to that list: this is the biome where a downed player is least likely to be reachable in time. The revive procedure is on the [**death and respawn**](/guides/death-and-respawn) page.

What to bring to the Volcanic Biome is really an ammunition question, answered in our [Ammo and Guns](/guides/ammo-guide) guide.


- **Ranged weapons.** Scorpions punish melee range with poison, and the Hellephant ragdolls you. The Ammo Furnace means ammo is the one thing you can top up on site.
- **Something for the heat.** The [Infernal Helmet](/items/how-to-get-infernal-helmet) removes lava damage outright, which is the single biggest quality-of-life item here.
- **A cleared return path.** The biome is far from camp and the biome blocks are one-or-the-other, so the walk back is long.
- **Empty sack space, and discipline about it.** Items in lava are gone permanently. Do not open the church roof chest with a full sack and no room to move.
- **Sacrifice Totems** if the altar is the goal. Eight charges per payout is a lot of cultists otherwise.

If wood and campfire levels are what are keeping you out, that is the real gate — see [How to Get Wood Fast](/items/how-to-get-wood-fast).

## FAQ

**How do I know if my run has the Volcanic Biome?**
Look at your outer ring. Snow and Volcanic are one-or-the-other per run; the Jungle is always there. Check your map borders early rather than assuming.

**What campfire level do I need?**
The biome appears from **Level 2+** and is fully revealed at **3**, but the **Volcano interior needs Level 4+**.

**Does lava kill you?**
It damages and ignites you for damage over time, and destroys nearly every item dropped into it permanently.

**Can I swim or walk through it?**
No — treat every pool as lethal and as a loot shredder.

**Where is the Infernal Sword?**
It is one of three possible payouts at the volcano altar (the third 8-charge cycle, around 24 charges), or a ~10% roll from one of the biome's two Red Spiked Chests. Full breakdown on [How to Get the Infernal Sword](/weapons/how-to-get-infernal-sword).

**How many Red Spiked Chests are there?**
Exactly two: the Ammo Furnace's rear chest and the Volcanic Church roof.

**What is the Hot Chest?**
The blue spiked chest — the common one. Wildfire, Sacrifice Totem, Lava Mine Blueprint, Crossbow.

**What is The Ram?**
An unkillable Volcanic entity that **charges in a straight line and cannot be stunned mid-charge**. Read the wind-up and step sideways, or run to the fire — the three-phase loop, the stun rules and the walk-speed dispute are on [The Ram](/entities/the-ram).

**Can I fish here?**
Yes — lava fishing gives Lionfish and Lava Eel.
